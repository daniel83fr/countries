AUSTRALIA
Official language: English

TITLE: "Under the Southern Cross"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Aussie pub-rock terrace anthem. Chugging electric guitars and a stomping
four-on-the-floor kick like a footy grandstand, a low didgeridoo drone
threading underneath the verses, gang-vocal "oi!" stabs on the chorus.
Tempo 128 BPM, built to be sung by 90,000 people standing up.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Didgeridoo drone fades in under a slow hand-clap)
One, two — here we go...

[Verse 1]
Grew up chasing a footy down a red dirt road,
Cicadas in the gum trees, the ute in low,
Nan had the wireless on for every Test match score,
Now I'm standing in the outer screaming out for more.

[Pre-Chorus]
From Uluru's burning red to the Bondi tide,
Every corner of this country's on the same side.

[Chorus] (viral hook)
Green and gold, hearts beat as one,
Under the Southern Cross we rise like the sun,
Oi! Oi! Oi! — hear the whole stand roar,
We don't stop till the final score!

[Verse 2]
The MCG shakes when the siren finally screams,
A servo attendant and a surgeon share the same dream,
Doesn't matter where you're from or what you've got,
When that jersey's on, mate, we're the lucky lot.

[Chorus]
Green and gold, hearts beat as one,
Under the Southern Cross we rise like the sun,
Oi! Oi! Oi! — hear the whole stand roar,
We don't stop till the final score!

[Bridge]
From the reef to the desert, coast to coast,
This is the team that the whole world knows —
Knocked down before but we don't stay down,
Give us one more chance and we'll take the crown.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Green and gold, hearts beat as one,
Under the Southern Cross we rise like the sun,
Oi! Oi! Oi! Oi! Oi! Oi!
Aussie! Aussie! Aussie!

[Outro — chant tag]
Oi! Oi! Oi!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Grounds the pride in specific images — the Southern Cross on the flag,
Uluru, the MCG, a "servo" (petrol station) worker standing next to a
surgeon in the same crowd — rather than generic "we are the champions"
filler. The "Oi! Oi! Oi!" tag is a deliberate, respectful nod to Australia's
already-iconic terrace chant, used as the connective tissue that makes the
new chorus instantly chantable rather than replacing what fans already own.
