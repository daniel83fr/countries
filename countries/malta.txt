MALTA
Official languages: Maltese, English (lyrics written in Maltese)

TITLE: "Għanja tal-Ġebel" (Song of the Stone)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Għana-rooted stadium anthem. Traditional Maltese għana call-and-response
troubadour singing — two lead voices trading improvised verses over a
guitar (kitarra) line — opens the track, before locking into a driving
Mediterranean stadium beat built for the limestone bowl of the National
Stadium in Ta' Qali, with massed crowd response on every hook.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on għana, Malta's genuine folk singing tradition in which two
singers trade improvised, often competitive verses over a simple guitar
line, and on the honey-colored limestone that built Malta's cities and
gives the island's coast and old town walls their color. Ta' Qali,
named in the lyric, is the real site of Malta's National Stadium.
English is co-official, but the lyric is written fully in Maltese for
authenticity. Have a native Maltese speaker review the grammar and
idiom before recording.
