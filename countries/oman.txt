OMAN
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Oman" (O Oman)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Omani khaleeji-fusion anthem. Oud and mizmar horn carrying the melody over
a razha hand-drum war-dance rhythm, frankincense-market ambience texture,
building into a full-band arena chorus with massed crowd vocals under the
Jebel Akhdar mountains.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Mizmar horn call over a slow razha drum pulse)
Ya Oman... ya Oman...

[Maqta' 1]
Dukhan al-lubban la yazalu yartafi'u min tilal Dhofar nafsiha,
Al-sanabiq la tazalu taqta'u ma' al-mina' kama tashtahi al-riyah al-qadima,
Qal'at Nizwa raqabat alaf fasl ya'ti wa yamdi,
Wa kullu khanjar 'ala al-'alam la yazalu yahmilu ma na'lamuhu.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min munhadarat al-jabal al-akhdar ila bab al-mina' al-maftuh,
Hadhihi umma tahmilu tarikhan akthar min qabl.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "Ya Oman al-habib" means "O beloved Oman"
Ya Oman! Ya Oman al-habib!
Yalla Oman, ya Oman al-habib!
Ya Oman! Ya Oman al-habib!

[Maqta' 2]
Raqsat al-razha la tazalu tatamayyalu kama ta'allama aba'una,
Al-sayf marfu' 'aliyan, la li-harb bal li-fakhr al-yawm,
La yuhimmu in nasiya al-'alam annana 'ala al-khareeta,
Kullu sanbuq abhara qattu la yazalu ya'rifu tariq al-'awda.

[al-Jawqa]
Ya Oman! Ya Oman al-habib!
Yalla Oman, ya Oman al-habib!

[al-Jisr]
Lubban wa jabal, mina' wa raml,
Kullu zawiya min hadha al-sahil tafham —
Turuq al-tijara al-qadima 'allamatna kayfa najul,
Wa kullu tariq safarna 'alayha yaqudu ila al-bayt.

[al-Jawqa al-Akhira]
Ya Oman! Ya Oman al-habib!
Yalla Oman, ya Oman al-habib!
Ya Oman! Ya Oman!

[al-Khatima]
Oman! Oman! Ya habib!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Anchored in Oman's frankincense trade heritage (Dhofar is a UNESCO-listed
frankincense land), Nizwa Fort, and the khanjar dagger that appears on
Oman's national emblem — genuine, distinctive symbols rather than generic
desert imagery. Have a native Omani Arabic speaker review phrasing before
recording.
