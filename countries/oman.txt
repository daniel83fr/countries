OMAN
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Oman" (O Oman)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Omani khaleeji-fusion anthem. Oud and mizmar horn carrying the melody over
a razha hand-drum war-dance rhythm, frankincense-market ambience texture,
building into a full-band arena chorus with massed crowd vocals under the
Jebel Akhdar mountains.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Mizmar horn call over a slow razha drum pulse)
Ya Oman... ya Oman...

[Verse 1]
Frankincense smoke still rising from the same Dhofar hills,
Dhows still cut the harbor water the way the old wind wills,
Nizwa's fort has watched a thousand seasons come and go,
And every khanjar on the flag still carries what we know.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the Green Mountain's terraced slopes to the harbor's open door,
This is a nation carrying more history than before.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Arabic + English)
Ya Oman! Ya Oman al-habib!
(O Oman! O beloved Oman!)
Yalla Oman, ya Oman al-habib!
(Come on Oman, O beloved Oman!)

[Verse 2]
The razha dance still swings the way our fathers learned to sway,
Blade held high, not built for war but built for pride today,
Doesn't matter that the world forgets we're on the map,
Every dhow that's ever sailed still knows the way back.

[Chorus]
Ya Oman! Ya Oman al-habib!
Yalla Oman, ya Oman al-habib!

[Bridge]
Frankincense and mountain, harbor and the sand,
Every corner of this coastline understands —
Ancient trade routes taught us how to roam,
And every road we've traveled leads us home.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Ya Oman! Ya Oman al-habib!
Yalla Oman, ya Oman al-habib!
Ya Oman! Ya Oman!

[Outro — chant tag]
Oman! Oman! Ya habib!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Anchored in Oman's frankincense trade heritage (Dhofar is a UNESCO-listed
frankincense land), Nizwa Fort, and the khanjar dagger that appears on
Oman's national emblem — genuine, distinctive symbols rather than generic
desert imagery. Have a native Omani Arabic speaker review phrasing before
recording.
