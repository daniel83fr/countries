NORWAY
Official language: Norwegian

TITLE: "Ropet fra Fjorden" (The Call from the Fjord)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
A hardingfele-driven stadium anthem. The hardingfele (Hardanger fiddle),
Norway's own ornately decorated folk fiddle with its extra drone
strings, opens the track with its unmistakable ringing double-stops
before a stomping, ski-jump-crowd-chant tempo and massed vocals take
over for the terrace hook.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Hardingfela synger alene over fjorden)
Norge... fra fjord til fjell...

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the hardingfele, Norway's UNESCO-recognized decorated folk
fiddle with sympathetic drone strings, and on "Heia Norge," the real,
plain chant Norwegians shout at everything from football matches to ski
jumping and biathlon, reflecting how tightly Norwegian sporting pride
links summer and winter sport. The fjord-and-mountain geography is
Norway's own defining landscape, not a borrowed image. Have a native
Norwegian speaker review the grammar and idiom before recording.
