BOTSWANA
Official language: English (Setswana widely spoken)

TITLE: "Pula!" (Rain! — Blessing, Prosperity)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Setswana call-and-response stadium anthem. Segaba (single-string fiddle)
and hand-clap polyrhythms rooted in traditional Tswana song, building into
a full-band Afro-pop chorus with massed crowd vocals chanting the
country's own national word for blessing.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Segaba fiddle line over layered hand-claps)
Pula... pula...

[Verse 1]
Okavango floods the desert into water, green, and song,
Cattle grazing slow horizons where the herds have grazed so long,
San hands painted stories on the rock before the rest began,
Every drop of rain out here is worth more than any man.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the delta's flooded silver to the Kalahari sand,
One word says it all across this quiet, steady land.

[Chorus] (viral hook — the actual national motto, meaning "rain")
Pula! Pula!
(Rain! Blessing! Prosperity!)
Botswana, re mmogo — Pula!
(Botswana, we are together — blessing!)

[Verse 2]
Diamonds in the ground but water is the truest wealth we hold,
A nation built on patience, not a story quickly told,
When the whole stadium claps the rhythm that the elders passed,
We are still the quiet nation built to last.

[Chorus]
Pula! Pula!
Botswana, re mmogo — Pula!

[Bridge]
The word for money and the word for rain are one,
That tells you everything about what we've become —
Delta water, desert sand, cattle in the sun,
This is the anthem of a nation, still, as one.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Pula! Pula!
Botswana, re mmogo — Pula!
Pula! Pula!

[Outro — chant tag]
Pula! Pula! Botswana!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built entirely on "Pula," Botswana's actual national motto and the name of
its currency, meaning "rain" — a real, deeply meaningful word in a country
where rainfall is genuinely treated as the greatest blessing. Paired with
the Okavango Delta and the country's cattle-centered culture. Have a native
Setswana speaker review phrasing before recording.
