ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
Official language: English

TITLE: "365 Beaches, One Heart"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Calypso-soca stadium anthem. Steel pan melody over a soca groove built
for a cricket-ground crowd, calypso storytelling verses, opening into a
full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals under an island sun.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Steel pan run over a building soca rhythm)
Antigua... Barbuda...

[Verse 1]
Three hundred sixty-five beaches, one for every single day,
A small island taught the whole cricket world how to play,
Every kid on every corner swings a bat before they walk,
And every legend on this island learned it from that same rock.

[Pre-Chorus]
From English Harbour's sheltered bay to the stadium's roaring crowd,
Two islands, one same heartbeat, standing proud.

[Chorus]
365 beaches, one heart! 365 beaches, one heart!
Small twin islands but we play above our weight,
365 beaches, one heart! 365 beaches, one heart!
Antigua, Barbuda, watch us carry the day.

[Verse 2]
Legends walked out to the crease the way our fathers always did,
Every boundary hit still echoes like it did back then, back when kids,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the score,
We built champions out of coral sand and so much more.

[Chorus]
365 beaches, one heart! 365 beaches, one heart!
Small twin islands but we play above our weight,
365 beaches, one heart! 365 beaches, one heart!
Antigua, Barbuda, watch us carry the day.

[Bridge]
Steel pan ringing over every single shore,
Two islands playing like they're worth so much more —
Soca beat and coral sand as one,
This is the anthem underneath the sun.

[Final Chorus]
365 beaches, one heart! 365 beaches, one heart!
Antigua, Barbuda, watch us carry the day.

[Outro]
Antigua! Barbuda! One heart!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the real "365 beaches, one for every day of the year" fact
Antiguans take genuine pride in, and the islands' legendary cricketing
tradition, which has produced some of the sport's greatest players.
English is the official language, so the full lyric is written directly
in it.
