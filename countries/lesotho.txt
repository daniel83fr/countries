LESOTHO
Official languages: Sesotho, English

TITLE: "Lesotho, Pele" (Lesotho, Forward)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Famo-driven mountain-kingdom anthem. Accordion and lesiba (traditional
stringed wind instrument) carry the melody over a horse-trot percussion
pattern, mokorotlo hat and Basotho blanket imagery in the visuals, opening
into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Sesotho):

[Selelekela]
(Accordion e qala, ka tlase ho mokgahlelo o tshepahalang wa pere)
Lesotho... pele...

[Temana 1]
Dithaba di eme di tlotse maru, di sa robale letsatsi le letsatsi,
Bana ba palama dipere tsa Basotho ho tloha bosiu ho fihla mahube,
Kobo ea Mosotho e sireletsa moea o batang oa thaba,
'Me mokorotlo o eme o le mong lefatsheng lohle.

[Pele ho Phetolelo]
Ho tloha thabeng e phahameng ho ea stediamong e llang,
Naha e le nngwe, e phahameng, e tswela pele ka boikgantsho.

[Phetolelo] — the hook: "Lesotho, pele!" means "Lesotho, forward!"
Lesotho, pele! Lesotho, pele!
Re bapala mmoho — Lesotho, pele!
Lesotho, pele! Lesotho, pele!

[Temana 2]
Pere ea Mosotho e tseba tsela ea thaba e sa lekang le e mong,
Kobo e futhumatsa mmele empa boikgantsho bo futhumatsa pelo,
Ha ho na taba hore naha e nngwe le e nngwe e re bona jwang,
Re dula re le nngwe thabeng, re dula re le nngwe pelong.

[Phetolelo]
Lesotho, pele! Lesotho, pele!
Re bapala mmoho — Lesotho, pele!

[Kgokahano]
Thaba le lehodimo, ka bobedi ba rona,
Boikgantsho bo sa feleng ba naha e phahameng —
Lesiba le kobo, pele le pele,
Ke sefela sa naha e sa lefolleng.

[Phetolelo ea ho Qetela]
Lesotho, pele! Lesotho, pele!
Re bapala mmoho — Lesotho, pele!

[Qetello]
Lesotho! Pele! Pele!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Basotho blanket and mokorotlo conical hat, real and iconic
national symbols, and Lesotho's unique geography as the only country
entirely above 1,000 metres in elevation. Have a native Sesotho speaker
review grammar and idiom before recording.
