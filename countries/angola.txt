ANGOLA
Official language: Portuguese

TITLE: "Força, Angola" (Strength, Angola)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Semba-and-kuduro fusion anthem. Dikanza scraper and semba guitar patterns
opening the verses (the rhythm that gave birth to kizomba worldwide),
accelerating into a kuduro-tempo, bass-heavy chorus with massed crowd
vocals — Luanda's own dance-floor energy scaled to a stadium.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Dikanza scraper rhythm builds under a semba guitar line)
Angola... força, Angola...

[Verse 1]
Kalunga's ocean carried more than any word could hold,
Semba taught the whole wide world a rhythm to be told,
The Palancas Negras charge the field with horns held to the sky,
Every antelope that's ever run has taught us how to try.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the Kalunga's rolling wave to the Luanda street parade,
One nation dancing through the storm that history made.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Portuguese + English)
Força, Angola! Força, Angola!
(Strength, Angola! Strength, Angola!)
Juntos, vamos — força, Angola!
(Together, let's go — strength, Angola!)

[Verse 2]
The dikanza scrapes a rhythm passed through every generation,
Kuduro speeds it up into a whole nation's celebration,
Doesn't matter what the years of hardship carved and cost,
We turned survival into rhythm — nothing here was lost.

[Chorus]
Força, Angola! Força, Angola!
Juntos, vamos — força, Angola!

[Bridge]
Semba slow and kuduro fast, one heartbeat, two speeds,
A nation dancing through whatever the moment needs —
Antelope charging, horns against the sun,
This is the anthem of a rhythm never done.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Força, Angola! Força, Angola!
Juntos, vamos — força, Angola!
Força! Força!

[Outro — chant tag]
Angola! Força! Força!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on semba (the Angolan rhythm that gave rise to kizomba worldwide) and
kuduro (Luanda's own high-energy dance genre), plus the Palancas Negras
(Black Antelopes), the real nickname of Angola's national football team.
Have a native Angolan Portuguese speaker review phrasing before recording.
