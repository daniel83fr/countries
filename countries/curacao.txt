CURACAO
Official languages: Dutch, Papiamentu, English

TITLE: "Dushi Korsou" (Sweet Curaçao)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Tumba-driven carnival anthem. Tumba rhythm (Curaçao's own carnival genre)
drives the whole track, wind instruments and steel drum accents, colorful
Handelskade waterfront energy, opening into a full-band chorus with massed
crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Papiamentu):

[Introdukshon]
(Un ritmo di tumba ta krece riba un base di bario)
Korsou... dushi Korsou...

[Kuplet 1]
E kas koló den Handelskade ta briya bou solo,
E laman turkwesa ta konta un historia sin ripiti nunka,
Tumba ta kanta den karnaval kada aña ku ta bini,
Kada wega ku nos hunga ta karga e mes rítmiko.

[Promé ku Refrein]
Di e kas koló na waterkant te na e stadion ku ta zona,
Un isla so, dushi, ku no ta wòri di nada.

[Refrein] — the hook: "Dushi Korsou" means "sweet Curaçao"
Dushi Korsou! Dushi Korsou!
Nos tur huntu — dushi Korsou!
Dushi Korsou! Dushi Korsou!

[Kuplet 2]
E tumba ta konta di lucha i di alegria huntu,
Nos isla chikí ta hasi mas ku e mapa por a mustra nunka,
No ta importa kuantu aña difísil nos a pasa aden,
E dushimentu di Korsou nunka lo no kaba pa fin.

[Refrein]
Dushi Korsou! Dushi Korsou!
Nos tur huntu — dushi Korsou!

[Puente]
Kas koló i laman, un solo kurason,
E ritmu di tumba ta biba den nos alma,
Wind instrumento i tambú, un solo bos,
Esaki ta e himno di un isla glorioso.

[Refrein Final]
Dushi Korsou! Dushi Korsou!
Nos tur huntu — dushi Korsou!

[Final]
Korsou! Dushi Korsou!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on tumba, Curaçao's own carnival music genre, and the colorful
Handelskade waterfront buildings, one of the island's most recognized
landmarks. Written in Papiamentu, the language Curaçaoans actually speak
day to day. Have a native Papiamentu speaker review phrasing before
recording.
