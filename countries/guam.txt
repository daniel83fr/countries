GUAM
Official languages: English, Chamorro

TITLE: "Guahan Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Chamorro-Pacific fusion anthem. Belembaotuyan (Chamorro bow instrument) and
conch-shell horn calls opening over island guitar strumming, building into
a reggae-tinged island-rock groove for the chorus with massed island-style
gang vocals — small-island pride built to fill a stadium far bigger than
the island itself.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Conch shell call answered by a slow belembaotuyan pulse)
Guahan... Guahan...

[Verse 1]
Latte stones still standing where our ancestors once stood,
Reef break rolling green and blue the way it always would,
Nobody thought a speck this small could shake the football world,
Then eleven island hearts stood up and the whole ocean swirled.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the cliffs of Two Lovers Point to the stadium floor,
This little island's heartbeat can still make the big world roar.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Chamorro + English)
Guahan! Guahan! Rising from the sea!
(Guam! Guam! Rising from the sea!)
Guahan! Guahan! Believe in Guahan, believe in me!

[Verse 2]
They said we were too small to ever trouble anyone,
Then we knocked a giant down before the final whistle's done,
Latte stones don't move for storms, and neither do we now,
This reef-bound island learned the world exactly how.

[Chorus]
Guahan! Guahan! Rising from the sea!
Guahan! Guahan! Believe in Guahan, believe in me!

[Bridge]
Small on the map but never small in heart,
An island proved the underdog can play its part —
Latte stone, reef, and conch shell horn,
This is the sound of a nation reborn.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Guahan! Guahan! Rising from the sea!
Guahan! Guahan! Believe in Guahan, believe in me!
Guahan! Guahan!

[Outro — chant tag]
Guahan! Guahan! Guahan!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Guam's Chamorro heritage — the ancient latte stones found across
the island, the Chamorro name "Guahan" (meaning "we have," a real and proud
self-designation) — and the genuinely famous 2018 upset when Guam's
football team beat a far higher-ranked opponent, a true underdog story the
whole island still celebrates. Have a native Chamorro speaker confirm
phrasing before recording.
