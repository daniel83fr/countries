MOROCCO
Official languages: Arabic, Tamazight (Berber)

TITLE: "Dima Maghrib" (Always Morocco)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Gnawa-driven stadium anthem. Guembri bass lute and qraqeb iron castanets
carry the trance-like Gnawa rhythm (a UNESCO-listed Moroccan tradition)
over a tbal drum pulse, Atlas Mountains ambience, opening into a full-band
chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Guembri yabda' iqa' Gnawa fawqa saut al-qraqeb al-hadidiya)
Ya Maghrib... dima Maghrib...

[Maqta' 1]
Jibal al-Atlas tahrusu al-sahra' wa al-bahr fi nafs al-waqt,
Marrakech tarqusu bi-alwaniha ma bayna al-layl wa al-waqt,
Al-Asudu tal'abu al-kura bi-qalbin la ya'rifu al-khawf,
Wa nusf nihai'i al-'alam sarna nahnu al-huruf.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min qimmat al-jabal al-'ali ila al-mal'ab al-mudi',
Ummatun wahidatun, Amazighiya wa 'Arabiya, tabqa hadhiha.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "Dima Maghrib" means "Always Morocco"
Dima Maghrib! Dima Maghrib!
Kullana ma'an — dima Maghrib!
Dima Maghrib! Dima Maghrib!

[Maqta' 2]

Wa kullu qra'a lil-qaraqeb tahmilu qissa ba'da qissa,
La yuhimmu kam min al-sinin marrat wa nahnu hunak,
Nahnu al-ummatu allati saja'alat al-hulm haqiqatan wa liss hilm fakat.

[al-Jawqa]

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Gnawa music (a UNESCO-recognized Moroccan spiritual tradition),
the Atlas Mountains, and Morocco's historic 2022 World Cup run to the
semifinals — the first African and Arab nation ever to reach that stage.
"Dima Maghrib" is a real, widely used fan chant. Have native Moroccan
Arabic (Darija) and Tamazight speakers review phrasing before recording.
