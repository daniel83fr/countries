ETHIOPIA
Official language: Amharic

TITLE: "Ethiopia, Kidmi" (Ethiopia, Forward)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Krar-and-running stadium anthem. Krar lyre and masinko fiddle carrying the
pentatonic melodic hook, kebero drum rhythm paced like a distance runner's
stride building steadily faster, opening into a full-band chorus with
massed crowd vocals — coffee-ceremony incense drifting through the mix.

LYRICS (Amharic, romanized):

[Metaya]
(Masinko fiddle line over a slow, steady kebero drum pulse)
Ethiopia... kidmi...

[Semmi 1]
Buna kemezaz teqedeme diha alem semwa siyawq'a,
Ye Lalibela dingay bétäkristiyanat kend teqeräxu, aydel tesera,
Minim jenda beyayaten liyy anäbenmm, ye ni yasteqemelen,
Wede rutsa hulu bä'alem yeleq'e haymanot yetegabale.

[Kesteseriya Befit]
Ke dabir menged setsehay yiwet'ta iske stadium chuhet dires,
And ye tarik hager, mesiret alagegnem, be'kurat rutsa.

[Kôrus] — the hook: "kidmi" means "forward"; "andnet" means "unity"
Ethiopia, kidmi! Ethiopia, kidmi!
Andnet, Ethiopia, kidmi!
Ethiopia, kidmi! Ethiopia, kidmi!

[Semmi 2]
Rützam bezeyet yishimer bechuhet yasereda,
Yale ere ye dabir tarik yale filagoch new,
Yemech'ereshaw menged weys ye score board min inidaye alfeleghim,
Iñña gedma minim aydelenim yale hager nen.

[Kôrus]
Ethiopia, kidmi! Ethiopia, kidmi!
Andnet, Ethiopia, kidmi!

[Diguswa]
Dingay bétäkristiyan ke dabir teqeräxe, buna keäfär,
Yale timihirt netsanet menfes, alagelegele —
Krar hidät ena rutsa iregät gara,
Yihe siretat wede tsehay new.

[Mecheresha Kôrus]
Ethiopia, kidmi! Ethiopia, kidmi!
Andnet, Ethiopia, kidmi!
Kidmi! Kidmi!

[Mewedeya]
Ethiopia! Kidmi!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Ethiopia's real status as the birthplace of coffee, the rock-hewn
churches of Lalibela, and the country's legendary distance-running
tradition, alongside the genuine national pride in never having been
colonized. Written in romanized Amharic as a best-effort draft; have a
native Amharic speaker convert to Ge'ez script and review grammar before
recording.
