ETHIOPIA
Official language: Amharic

TITLE: "Ethiopia, Kidmi" (Ethiopia, Forward)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Krar-and-running stadium anthem. Krar lyre and masinko fiddle carrying the
pentatonic melodic hook, kebero drum rhythm paced like a distance runner's
stride building steadily faster, opening into a full-band chorus with
massed crowd vocals — coffee-ceremony incense drifting through the mix.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Masinko fiddle line over a slow, steady kebero drum pulse)
Ethiopia... kidmi...

[Verse 1]
Coffee was born on these highlands before the whole world knew its name,
Lalibela's stone churches were carved from rock, not built the same,
No flag has ever flown above us that we didn't choose ourselves,
And every runner on this earth still chases what our highland tells.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the highland trail at sunrise to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One ancient nation, never conquered, running proud.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Amharic + English)
Ethiopia, kidmi! Ethiopia, kidmi!
(Ethiopia, forward! Ethiopia, forward!)
Andnet, Ethiopia, kidmi!
(Unity — Ethiopia, forward!)

[Verse 2]
The runner starts in silence and finishes in a roar,
Every stride a story that this highland's told before,
Doesn't matter what the finish line or scoreboard shows the crowd,
We are still the nation that has never had to bow.

[Chorus]
Ethiopia, kidmi! Ethiopia, kidmi!
Andnet, Ethiopia, kidmi!

[Bridge]
Stone church carved from mountain, coffee from the ground,
An ancient independent spirit, still unbound —
Krar string and running stride as one,
This is the anthem underneath the sun.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Ethiopia, kidmi! Ethiopia, kidmi!
Andnet, Ethiopia, kidmi!
Kidmi! Kidmi!

[Outro — chant tag]
Ethiopia! Kidmi!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Ethiopia's real status as the birthplace of coffee, the rock-hewn
churches of Lalibela, and the country's legendary distance-running
tradition, alongside the genuine national pride in never having been
colonized. Have a native Amharic speaker review phrasing before recording.
