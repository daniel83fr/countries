NIGERIA
Official language: English

TITLE: "Naija, Rise"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Afrobeats stadium anthem. A driving Afrobeats groove — the genre Nigeria
gave to the entire world — with talking drum and log drum polyrhythms
underneath, horn stabs and a call-and-response hook built for a packed
stadium and a global charts moment at once.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Talking drum "speaks" over a rising Afrobeats groove)
Naija... rise...

[Verse 1]
Two hundred million voices but we move like just one drum,
Lagos never sleeps and neither does the beat we've become,
The talking drum was speaking long before the mic came round,
Now the whole world dances to the Naija sound.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the market's crowded noise to the stadium's roaring wall,
One giant nation rising, answering the call.

[Chorus]
Naija, rise! Naija, rise!
Green and white, we own the whole wide sky,
Naija, rise! Naija, rise!
Super Eagles flying, we don't ask why.

[Verse 2]
Every hustle taught us how to turn the struggle into song,
Every generation proves the rhythm carries on and on,
Doesn't matter what the outside headlines choose to show,
We are still the giant that the whole continent knows.

[Chorus]
Naija, rise! Naija, rise!
Green and white, we own the whole wide sky,
Naija, rise! Naija, rise!
Super Eagles flying, we don't ask why.

[Bridge]
Talking drum still speaking what the years demand,
A hundred tribes, one language, one same hand —
Afrobeat horn and log drum, side by side,
This is the anthem of the giant's pride.

[Final Chorus]
Naija, rise! Naija, rise!
Green and white, we own the whole wide sky,
Naija, rise! Naija, rise!

[Outro]
Naija! Rise! Rise!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Afrobeats, the genre Nigeria has exported to the whole world, and
"the Super Eagles," the real, beloved nickname of the national football
team. "Naija" is the country's own widely used affectionate nickname for
itself. English is Nigeria's official language, so the full lyric is
written directly in it.
