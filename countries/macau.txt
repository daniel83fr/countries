MACAU
Official languages: Chinese (Cantonese), Portuguese

TITLE: "Ga Yau, Macau / Vamos, Macau"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Luso-Cantonese fusion stadium anthem. Portuguese guitarra melody lines
trading with Cantonese gong and cymbal hits, fado-tinged minor-key verses
opening into a firecracker-percussion, brass-driven pop chorus with massed
bilingual crowd vocals — two heritages sharing one small peninsula's roar.

LYRICS (Cantonese verse, Portuguese verse — Macau's two official languages):

[Hoi Coeng]
(Portuguese guitarra picks a slow line over distant firecrackers)
Macau... ga yau... vamos...

[Go Ci 1 — Cantonese, romanized]
Sing Bou Luk gau sik sek pai wing jyun kei hai saan soeng,
Pou gwok cin zyun tung zung gwok heung fo tung jat go zing zi soeng,
Jyu syun tung dou coeng dang fo joeng zyu tung jat go wan,
Loeng zung jyu jin, jat go sam tiu, mui jat jat dou hai gam joeng.

[Go Ci 2 — Português]
Vapor de pastel de nata e fumo de incenso sobem do mesmo jeito quente,
Duas bandeiras dobradas numa só em cada dia de jogo, presente,
Pequena península, grande história, quatro séculos fundos e verdadeiros,
Quando o apito toca, torcemos em mais de um idioma, companheiros.

[Fu Go / Refrão] — the hook: "ga yau" (Cantonese) and "vamos" (Portuguese) both mean "come on/go"
Ga yau, Macau! Vamos, Macau!
Yat chai, Macau! Juntos, Macau!
Ga yau, Macau! Vamos, Macau!

[Pont / Kiu]
Dung sai gwo bin hai ni dou zou ji cin gaan lau min gwok gaa gong,
Ngo dei hai kiu, ngo dei hai wui hop dim —
Este é o hino de Macau, mar e monte a se encontrar,
Duas línguas, um só rugido, ecoando ao redor da baía sem parar.

[Zeoi Hau Fu Go / Refrão Final]
Ga yau, Macau! Vamos, Macau!
Yat chai, Macau! Juntos, Macau!
Ga yau! Vamos!

[Git Mei / Final]
Macau! Ga yau! Vamos!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built specifically around Macau's dual Portuguese-Chinese heritage — the
Ruins of St. Paul's, a real landmark fusing both cultures — with the
chorus deliberately carrying both a Cantonese cheer ("ga yau") and a
Portuguese one ("vamos") side by side, since that duality is Macau's
actual identity. Have native Cantonese and Portuguese speakers each review
their half before recording.
