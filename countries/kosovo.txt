KOSOVO
Official languages: Albanian, Serbian (lyrics written in Albanian)

TITLE: "Zemra e Vogël" (The Little Heart)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Çifteli-driven stadium anthem rooted in Kosovo Albanian highland folk
music. The çifteli (two-string long-necked lute) plays the rapid,
syncopated highland riff over a hand-drum (def) pulse, building into a
massed male-and-female crowd chant styled after Kosovo's fan terraces,
with the çifteli line returning full-volume under the final chorus.

LYRICS (Albanian):

[Hyrje]
(Çiftelia hyn e vetme, pastaj defi)
Kosovë... zemra e vogël...

[Vargu i Parë]
Nga malet e Sharrit deri te fusha jonë,
Një popull i vogël që s'u dorëzua kurrë,
Vitin dy mijë e gjashtëmbëdhjetë erdhi lajmi,
FIFA na njohu, botë, tani na shihni ne!

[Para Refrenit]
Çdo fëmijë rrugës që gjuan një top të vjetër,
Mban në zemër ëndrrën që sot po e realizojmë.

[Refreni] — hook: "Kosovë, zemra e vogël, po rreh më fort se kurrë!" ("Kosovo, little heart, beating stronger than ever!")
Kosovë, Kosovë!
Zemra e vogël po rreh!
Kosovë, Kosovë!
Populli ynë s'ndalet kurrë!

[Vargu i Dytë]

Dy tela të thjeshtë, por zëri s'ka fund,
Ne jemi vend i vogël me shpirt që s'ka kufij,
Çdo ndeshje luhet me krenari të thellë.

[Refreni]
Kosovë, Kosovë!
Zemra e vogël po rreh!

[Ura]

[Refreni Final]
Kosovë, Kosovë!
Zemra e vogël po rreh!
Kosovë, Kosovë!
Populli ynë s'ndalet kurrë!

[Fund]
Kosovë! Zemra e vogël!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the çifteli, the two-stringed folk lute central to Kosovo
Albanian highland music, and on the real 2016 FIFA recognition of the
Kosovo Football Federation, which let the country's national team play
official matches for the first time after years of being unable to
compete internationally. The "little heart" image reflects Kosovo's
real identity as one of Europe's youngest and smallest nations, proud
of an underdog story still being written. Have a native Albanian
speaker review the grammar and idiom before recording.
