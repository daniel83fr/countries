TURKMENISTAN
Official language: Turkmen

TITLE: "Türkmenistan, Öňe!" (Turkmenistan, Forward!)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Dutar-driven steppe anthem. Dutar lute melody carrying the hook over a
galloping frame-drum rhythm built to evoke the Akhal-Teke horse's stride,
bakhshi throat-singing texture in the verses, opening into a full-band
chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Turkmen):

[Sözbaşy]
(Dutar lute figure over a slow galloping drum pulse)
Türkmenistan... öňe...

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Akhal-Teke, Turkmenistan's legendary golden horse breed and a
genuine source of deep national pride, plus the country's famous carpet-
weaving tradition (patterns from which appear on the national flag). "Öňe"
(forward) is a real Turkmen word for rallying people on. Written in
Turkmen using the Latin alphabet (the country's current official script);
have a native Turkmen speaker review grammar and idiom before recording.
