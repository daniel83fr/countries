SWEDEN
Official language: Swedish

TITLE: "Blågult" (Blue-Yellow)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Swedish folk-fiddle stadium anthem built around the nyckelharpa (Sweden's
own keyed-fiddle instrument, native to Uppland), its droning double-string
melody opening the track over a slow polska-lilt before the tempo lifts
into a driving folk-rock stomp with full-crowd unison chanting on the
hook.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the nyckelharpa, Sweden's national instrument — a keyed fiddle
native to the Uppland region whose droning sympathetic strings are
unmistakably Swedish — and on "Blågult" (Blue-Yellow), the real,
widely-used nickname for the Swedish national team, taken straight from
the flag's colors. Have a native Swedish speaker review the grammar and
idiom before recording.
