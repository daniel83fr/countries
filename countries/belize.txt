BELIZE
Official language: English

TITLE: "Reef and Ruins Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Punta-driven stadium anthem. Garifuna drums carry the entire rhythmic
backbone — a real, UNESCO-recognized Belizean tradition — with conch
shell horn calls and jungle-bird ambience, opening into a full-band
chorus with massed crowd vocals over the Belize Barrier Reef.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Garifuna drum pattern builds under a conch shell horn call)
Belize... rising...

[Verse 1]
The second largest reef on earth is curling off our shore,
Maya temples watch the jungle canopy like they did before,
Garifuna drums still speak the way the ancestors intended,
And every match-day crowd still moves to rhythms never ended.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the reef below the surface to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One small nation, jungle-hearted, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Reef and ruins rising! Reef and ruins rising!
Small on the map but the heart runs deep and wide,
Reef and ruins rising! Reef and ruins rising!
Belize, Belize, we carry you with pride.

[Verse 2]
Jaguars move quiet through the forest we've kept green,
Every drumbeat in this nation is the loudest thing unseen,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the score,
We built a pride as deep as any reef or jungle floor.

[Chorus]
Reef and ruins rising! Reef and ruins rising!
Small on the map but the heart runs deep and wide,
Reef and ruins rising! Reef and ruins rising!
Belize, Belize, we carry you with pride.

[Bridge]
Coral reef and temple stone, jungle and the tide,
A small nation carrying an ocean-sized pride —
Garifuna drum and conch shell horn,
This is the anthem of a nation reborn.

[Final Chorus]
Reef and ruins rising! Reef and ruins rising!
Belize, Belize, we carry you with pride.

[Outro]
Belize! Rising! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Belize Barrier Reef (the second-largest in the world), Maya
ruins found throughout the country, and Garifuna drumming, a real
UNESCO-recognized tradition central to Belizean cultural identity. English
is Belize's official language, so the full lyric is written directly in
it.
