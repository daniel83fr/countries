BENIN
Official language: French

TITLE: "Allez, Bénin" (Go, Benin)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Vodun-rhythm stadium anthem. Sato and gangan talking-drum patterns rooted
in Benin's Vodun ceremonial music carrying the pulse, kora-adjacent string
melody in the verses, opening into a full-band Afrobeat-tinged chorus with
massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Gangan talking drum "speaks" over a rising sato drum pattern)
Bénin... allez, Bénin...

[Verse 1]
Abomey's palace walls still hold the stories carved in bronze,
Women warriors once stood guard where now a whole crowd swarms,
Vodun drums have called the spirits since before the written word,
And every match-day gathering still moves to what they heard.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the royal walls of Abomey to the coastal, crowded street,
One nation, ancient kingdom, moving on one beat.

[Chorus] (viral hook, French + English)
Allez, Bénin! Allez, Bénin!
(Go, Benin! Go, Benin!)
Ensemble, on y va — allez, Bénin!
(Together, let's go — go, Benin!)

[Verse 2]
The Amazons of Dahomey taught us courage has no gender's name,
Every drumbeat in this country carries forward that same flame,
Doesn't matter what the scoreboard says the world expects to see,
We are the kingdom that has always fought for its own history.

[Chorus]
Allez, Bénin! Allez, Bénin!
Ensemble, on y va — allez, Bénin!

[Bridge]
Bronze plaque and drumbeat, palace wall and sea,
A kingdom's courage still runs through you and me —
Talking drum and spirit song as one,
This is the anthem beneath the coastal sun.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Allez, Bénin! Allez, Bénin!
Ensemble, on y va — allez, Bénin!
Allez! Allez!

[Outro — chant tag]
Bénin! Allez! Allez!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Kingdom of Dahomey's royal palaces of Abomey (a UNESCO World
Heritage site) and its famous Amazon women warriors, plus Vodun, the
religion that originated in Benin and remains central to its ceremonial
music and drumming. Have a native French/Fon speaker review phrasing
before recording.
