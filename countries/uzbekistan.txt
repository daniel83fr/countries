UZBEKISTAN
Official language: Uzbek

TITLE: "O'zbekiston, Oldinga!" (Uzbekistan, Forward!)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Silk Road-fusion stadium anthem. Dutar and rubab lute melody lines over a
doira frame-drum rhythm, maqom-style vocal ornamentation in the verses,
opening into a full-band arena chorus with massed crowd vocals echoing
the tiled domes of Samarkand.

LYRICS (Uzbek):

[Kirish]
(Dutar figure over a slow doira frame-drum pulse)
O'zbekiston... oldinga...

[Band 1]
Registon koshinlari quyoshni avvalgidek tutib turibdi hamon,
Karvonlar bir paytlar ipakni shu yerdan sohildan sohilga tashigan,
Har oilaning dasturxonida qo'ldan-qo'lga o'tgan osh qozoni bor,
Har polvonning changali bu yer qanday qurilganini hurmat qiladi.

[Naqarotdan Oldin]
Tongdagi zangori gumbazdan pastdagi paxta dalasigacha,
Bir millat, qadimiy chorraha, o'z nurini olib boradi.

[Naqarot] — the hook: "oldinga" means "forward"; "birga" means "together"
O'zbekiston, oldinga! O'zbekiston, oldinga!
Birga, birga — O'zbekiston, oldinga!
O'zbekiston, oldinga!

[Band 2]
Kurash polvonlari belbog'ni otalari kabi ushlaydi hamon,
Har bir imperiya bu yerdan o'tdi, lekin qolganlar bizmiz,
Butun stadion hakam signali va yugurishga o'rnidan turganda,
Biz imperiyalar hech qachon yenga olmagan chorraha g'ururini olib boramiz.

[Naqarot]
O'zbekiston, oldinga! O'zbekiston, oldinga!
Birga, birga — O'zbekiston, oldinga!

[Ko'prik]
Ipak yo'li changi hamon o'sha eski toshga o'tiradi,
Bu yerdan o'tgan har bir karvon bizga ko'proq narsa qoldirdi —
Dutar torlari va doira ritmi, koshinlar va gumbaz,
Bu — o'zini uy deb ataydigan chorrahaning madhiyasidir.

[Oxirgi Naqarot]
O'zbekiston, oldinga! O'zbekiston, oldinga!
Birga, birga — O'zbekiston, oldinga!
Oldinga! Oldinga!

[Yakun]
O'zbekiston! Oldinga!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Samarkand's Registan and the Silk Road heritage that made
Uzbekistan a genuine historical crossroads, plov (the national communal
dish, deeply tied to hospitality and celebration), and kurash (traditional
Uzbek belt wrestling). "Oldinga" (forward) is a real Uzbek word. Have a
native Uzbek speaker review phrasing before recording.
