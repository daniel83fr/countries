KUWAIT
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Yalla Kuwait"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Khaleeji pearl-diving fusion anthem. Oud and tanbura lute carrying the
melody over a fijiri hand-drum rhythm (the traditional pearl-diver work-song
style), building into a synth-and-guitar arena chorus with massed crowd
vocals under the shadow of the Kuwait Towers.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Fijiri hand-drum pattern rises over a low oud drone)
Yalla... yalla, Kuwait...

[Verse 1]
Before the towers rose against the desert evening sky,
Our grandfathers sang fijiri songs to keep the pearl dive dry,
Now the Gulf still catches gold light off three concrete spheres,
And every match still gathers the same voices as those years.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the pearl bed deep below to the tower's shining crown,
Small nation, big heart — we never let it down.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Arabic + English)
Yalla Kuwait! Yalla Kuwait!
(Come on, Kuwait! Come on, Kuwait!)
Habibi, yalla Kuwait!
(My love, come on Kuwait!)

[Verse 2]
The diver held his breath for pearls hidden in the deep,
Now we hold our breath for every ball still in our sweep,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the score,
We built a country out of open sea and open door.

[Chorus]
Yalla Kuwait! Yalla Kuwait!
Habibi, yalla Kuwait!

[Bridge]
No pearl too deep, no wave too rough to ride,
We are the desert and the gulf tide side by side —
Towers standing tall against the setting sun,
This is the anthem of a small nation, one.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Yalla Kuwait! Yalla Kuwait!
Habibi, yalla Kuwait!
Yalla! Yalla! Kuwait!

[Outro — chant tag]
Yalla, yalla, Kuwait!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Anchors the story in Kuwait's real pearl-diving history (the fijiri work-song
tradition sung by divers) and the Kuwait Towers, the country's most
recognized modern landmark. "Yalla" is genuine, common Gulf Arabic. Have a
native Kuwaiti Arabic speaker review phrasing before recording.
