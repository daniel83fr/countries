KUWAIT
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Yalla Kuwait"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Khaleeji pearl-diving fusion anthem. Oud and tanbura lute carrying the
melody over a fijiri hand-drum rhythm (the traditional pearl-diver work-song
style), building into a synth-and-guitar arena chorus with massed crowd
vocals under the shadow of the Kuwait Towers.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Fijiri hand-drum pattern rises over a low oud drone)
Yalla... yalla, Kuwait...

[Maqta' 1]
Qabla an tartafi' al-abraj didda sama' al-sahra' al-masa'iya,
Ajdaduna ghannu aghani al-fijiri li-yubqu ghaws al-lu'lu' jaffan,
Al-an al-khalij la yazalu yaltaqitu daw'an dhahabiyan min kurat khirsaniya thalath,
Wa kullu mubarat tajma'u nafs al-aswat min tilka al-sinin.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min qa' al-lu'lu' al-'amiq ila taj al-burj al-lami',
Umma saghira, qalbun kabir — la nakhdhuluhu abadan.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "yalla Kuwait" means "come on, Kuwait"
Yalla Kuwait! Yalla Kuwait!
Habibi, yalla Kuwait!
Yalla Kuwait! Yalla Kuwait!

[Maqta' 2]
Al-ghawwas habasa anfasahu bahthan 'an lu'lu' makhfi fi al-a'maq,
Al-an nahbisu anfasana li-kulli kura la tazalu fi masahatina,
La yuhimmu kayfa ta'ummu al-umam al-akbar al-natija,
Bananaa baladan min bahr maftuh wa bab maftuh.

[al-Jawqa]
Yalla Kuwait! Yalla Kuwait!
Habibi, yalla Kuwait!

[al-Jisr]
La lu'lu'a 'amiqa jiddan, la mawja qasiya jiddan lil-rukub,
Nahnu al-sahra' wa madd al-khalij janban ila janb —
Abraj waqifa 'aliya didda ghurub al-shams,
Hadhihi ughniyatu umma saghira, wahida.

[al-Jawqa al-Akhira]
Yalla Kuwait! Yalla Kuwait!
Habibi, yalla Kuwait!
Yalla! Yalla! Kuwait!

[al-Khatima]
Yalla, yalla, Kuwait!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Anchors the story in Kuwait's real pearl-diving history (the fijiri work-song
tradition sung by divers) and the Kuwait Towers, the country's most
recognized modern landmark. "Yalla" is genuine, common Gulf Arabic. Have a
native Kuwaiti Arabic speaker review phrasing before recording.
