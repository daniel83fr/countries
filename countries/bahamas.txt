BAHAMAS
Official language: English

TITLE: "Junkanoo Rush"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Junkanoo stadium anthem. Goatskin goombay drums, cowbells, and whistles —
the real instruments of a Junkanoo parade — drive the entire rhythm,
building into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals and a street-
parade rush energy.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Goombay drum roll builds under clashing cowbells)
Bahamas... rush...

[Verse 1]
Seven hundred islands scattered on the clearest sea in sight,
Junkanoo costumes turn Bay Street into a river of light,
Conch shell horns still call the way they called before the dawn,
Every match-day here still moves the way the rush goes on.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the parade at Boxing Day to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One island nation, rushing, proud out loud.

[Chorus]
Junkanoo rush! Junkanoo rush!
Cowbell, goombay, whistle — hear the whole street hush,
Junkanoo rush! Junkanoo rush!
Bahamas, Bahamas, feel the island rush.

[Verse 2]
The costume-makers work all year for one parade at dawn,
Every color, every feather, is a story handed on,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations fill the map's whole frame,
Seven hundred islands still can rush and shout one name.

[Chorus]
Junkanoo rush! Junkanoo rush!
Cowbell, goombay, whistle — hear the whole street hush,
Junkanoo rush! Junkanoo rush!
Bahamas, Bahamas, feel the island rush.

[Bridge]
Goatskin drum still beating like a hundred hearts,
Every islander a dancer playing their own part —
Conch shell horn and cowbell ringing loud,
This is the anthem of the rushing crowd.

[Final Chorus]
Junkanoo rush! Junkanoo rush!
Bahamas, Bahamas, feel the island rush.

[Outro]
Bahamas! Junkanoo! Rush!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built entirely on Junkanoo, the real, world-famous street parade held on
Boxing Day and New Year's Day, whose goombay drums, cowbells, and whistles
are the actual instruments used. English is the Bahamas's official
language, so the full lyric is written directly in it.
