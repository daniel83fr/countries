TAJIKISTAN
Official language: Tajik

TITLE: "Tojikiston, Pirooz Bod" (Tajikistan, Be Victorious)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Pamir-fusion stadium anthem. Rubab and tanbur lute lines echoing Persian
classical roots over a doira frame-drum rhythm, Pamiri falak-style
melisma in the verses, opening into a full-band arena chorus with massed
crowd vocals under the Roof of the World.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Rubab figure over a slow doira frame-drum pulse)
Tojikiston... pirooz bod...

[Verse 1]
The Pamirs climb so high they call it the roof of the world,
A flagpole taller than the clouds still keeps our flag unfurled,
Wrestlers grip and pull the way our grandfathers always did,
Every mountain village raised a warrior as a kid.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the Pamir's silent peaks to the Dushanbe city square,
One nation, ancient-rooted, standing tall and rare.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Tajik + English)
Tojikiston, pirooz bod! Tojikiston, pirooz bod!
(Tajikistan, be victorious! Tajikistan, be victorious!)
Yak dil, Tojikiston, pirooz bod!
(One heart — Tajikistan, be victorious!)

[Verse 2]
We are the heirs of poets writing long before the map was drawn,
Samanid stone and Persian verse still echo every dawn,
When the whole valley gathers for the match beneath the sun,
We carry a thousand years of pride into every one.

[Chorus]
Tojikiston, pirooz bod! Tojikiston, pirooz bod!
Yak dil, Tojikiston, pirooz bod!

[Bridge]
Roof of the world, and still we reach up higher,
Mountain and verse, both carrying the same fire —
Doira drum and a rubab's old string,
This is the anthem the mountains still sing.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Tojikiston, pirooz bod! Tojikiston, pirooz bod!
Yak dil, Tojikiston, pirooz bod!
Pirooz bod! Pirooz bod!

[Outro — chant tag]
Tojikiston! Pirooz bod!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Pamir Mountains ("the Roof of the World"), Tajikistan's pride
in its Persian-Samanid cultural heritage, and Dushanbe's record-setting
flagpole — genuine, specific national touchstones. "Pirooz" (victorious) is
a real Tajik/Persian word. Have a native Tajik speaker review phrasing
before recording.
