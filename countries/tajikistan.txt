TAJIKISTAN
Official language: Tajik

TITLE: "Tojikiston, Pirooz Bod" (Tajikistan, Be Victorious)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Pamir-fusion stadium anthem. Rubab and tanbur lute lines echoing Persian
classical roots over a doira frame-drum rhythm, Pamiri falak-style
melisma in the verses, opening into a full-band arena chorus with massed
crowd vocals under the Roof of the World.

LYRICS (Tajik, romanized):

[Muqaddima]
(Rubab figure over a slow doira frame-drum pulse)
Tojikiston... pirooz bod...

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Pamir Mountains ("the Roof of the World"), Tajikistan's pride
in its Persian-Samanid cultural heritage, and Dushanbe's record-setting
flagpole — genuine, specific national touchstones. "Pirooz" (victorious) is
a real Tajik/Persian word. Written as a best-effort romanized draft (with
some stray Cyrillic characters to clean up); have a native Tajik speaker
convert fully to Cyrillic script and rework grammar before recording.
