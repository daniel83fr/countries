UNITED ARAB EMIRATES
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Yalla Emarat" (Come On, Emirates)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Khaleeji-modern fusion anthem. Oud and mizmar horn over a fijiri
pearl-diver hand-drum rhythm, camel-racing gallop percussion motif,
opening into a synth-and-guitar arena chorus with massed crowd vocals
under a skyline of towers rising from the sand.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Fijiri hand-drum pulse rises under a distant falcon's cry)
Yalla... yalla, Emarat...

[Verse 1]
Seven flags became one flag beneath a desert sky,
Pearl divers sang the songs that taught this nation how to try,
Now a tower touches clouds where only dunes once stood,
And the whole world watches what one union really could.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the pearl bed deep below to the tower's shining spire,
Seven became one nation, carrying one fire.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Arabic + English)
Yalla Emarat! Yalla Emarat!
(Come on, Emirates! Come on, Emirates!)
Habibi, yalla Emarat!
(My love, come on Emirates!)

[Verse 2]
Zayed's promise still lives in every skyline light,
Seven emirates standing tall, together, side by side,
Doesn't matter how the years or scoreboards count us small,
We built a union from the sand that now outshines them all.

[Chorus]
Yalla Emarat! Yalla Emarat!
Habibi, yalla Emarat!

[Bridge]
No pearl too deep, no tower built too tall,
Seven flags that answered a single call —
Falcon and dune and glass against the sky,
This is the anthem of a union flying high.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Yalla Emarat! Yalla Emarat!
Habibi, yalla Emarat!
Yalla! Yalla! Emarat!

[Outro — chant tag]
Yalla, yalla, Emarat!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the 1971 union of the seven emirates under Sheikh Zayed, pearl-
diving heritage, and the country's real transformation from desert to
skyline — a genuine, specific national story rather than generic
prosperity imagery. Have a native Emirati Arabic speaker review phrasing
before recording.
