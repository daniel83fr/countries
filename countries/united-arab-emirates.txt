UNITED ARAB EMIRATES
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Yalla Emarat" (Come On, Emirates)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Khaleeji-modern fusion anthem. Oud and mizmar horn over a fijiri
pearl-diver hand-drum rhythm, camel-racing gallop percussion motif,
opening into a synth-and-guitar arena chorus with massed crowd vocals
under a skyline of towers rising from the sand.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Fijiri hand-drum pulse rises under a distant falcon's cry)
Yalla... yalla, Emarat...

[Maqta' 1]
Sab'u a'lam asbahat 'alaman wahidan taht sama' al-sahra',
Ghawwasu al-lu'lu' ghannu al-aghani allati 'allamat hadhihi al-umma kayfa tuhawil,
Al-an burj yalmasu al-ghuyum haythu kanat al-kuthban faqat waqifa,
Wa al-'alam kulluhu yushahidu ma yumkinu li-ittihad wahid an yaf'al.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min qa' al-lu'lu' al-'amiq ila mi'dhanat al-burj al-lami',
Sab'a asbahat ummatan wahida, tahmilu naran wahida.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "yalla Emarat" means "come on, Emirates"

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the 1971 union of the seven emirates under Sheikh Zayed, pearl-
diving heritage, and the country's real transformation from desert to
skyline — a genuine, specific national story rather than generic
prosperity imagery. Have a native Emirati Arabic speaker review phrasing
before recording.
