IVORY COAST
Official language: French

TITLE: "Allez, les Éléphants" (Go, the Elephants)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Coupé-décalé stadium anthem. The coupé-décalé beat (born in Abidjan and
now danced across all of Africa) drives the whole track, djembe and balafon
accents woven through the verses, opening into a horn-heavy, bass-forward
chorus built for a stadium dance-floor.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on "Les Éléphants," the beloved nickname of Ivory Coast's national
football team, the country's real status as the world's largest cocoa
producer, and coupé-décalé, the Abidjan-born dance music genre now danced
across the continent. References the real, historic moment when captain
Didier Drogba's post-match plea helped ease civil conflict. Have a native
French speaker review phrasing before recording.
