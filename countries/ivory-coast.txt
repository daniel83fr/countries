IVORY COAST
Official language: French

TITLE: "Allez, les Éléphants" (Go, the Elephants)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Coupé-décalé stadium anthem. The coupé-décalé beat (born in Abidjan and
now danced across all of Africa) drives the whole track, djembe and balafon
accents woven through the verses, opening into a horn-heavy, bass-forward
chorus built for a stadium dance-floor.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Coupé-décalé beat locks in over a balafon run)
Côte d'Ivoire... allez, les Éléphants...

[Verse 1]
Cocoa fields stretch farther than the eye can ever hold,
This small coast feeds the whole world's chocolate, sweet as gold,
Yamoussoukro's basilica rises taller than the trees,
And every street in Abidjan moves to the same new beat.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the cocoa field at sunrise to the Abidjan dance floor,
One nation, rhythm-hearted, carrying more.

[Chorus] (viral hook, French + English)
Allez, les Éléphants! Allez, les Éléphants!
(Go, the Elephants! Go, the Elephants!)
Ensemble on gagne — allez, les Éléphants!
(Together we win — go, the Elephants!)

[Verse 2]
Drogba lifted more than trophies when he raised his voice for peace,
The whole divided nation found a single team could ease,
Doesn't matter what the years of conflict tried to break apart,
Eleven orange shirts can still unite a whole nation's heart.

[Chorus]
Allez, les Éléphants! Allez, les Éléphants!
Ensemble on gagne — allez, les Éléphants!

[Bridge]
Cocoa sweet and coupé-décalé fast,
A nation dancing through whatever the moment asks —
Elephant charging, tusks against the sun,
This is the anthem of a country, one.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Allez, les Éléphants! Allez, les Éléphants!
Ensemble on gagne — allez, les Éléphants!
Allez! Allez!

[Outro — chant tag]
Côte d'Ivoire! Allez! Allez!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on "Les Éléphants," the beloved nickname of Ivory Coast's national
football team, the country's real status as the world's largest cocoa
producer, and coupé-décalé, the Abidjan-born dance music genre now danced
across the continent. References the real, historic moment when captain
Didier Drogba's post-match plea helped ease civil conflict. Have a native
French speaker review phrasing before recording.
