NEPAL
Official language: Nepali

TITLE: "Nepal, Agadi Badha" (Nepal, Move Forward)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Himalayan-fusion stadium anthem. Sarangi fiddle carrying the melodic line
over a madal hand-drum rhythm, bansuri flute texture in the verses,
opening into a full-band arena chorus with massed crowd vocals — the sound
of a Kathmandu Valley festival scaled up to a stadium.

LYRICS (Nepali, romanized):

[Suruwat]
(Sarangi fiddle line over a slow madal drum pulse)
Nepal... agadi badha...

[Antara 1]

[Chorus Aghi]

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Sagarmatha (Everest) and the real, globally admired Sherpa
mountaineering tradition, framing national pride around endurance and
elevation rather than generic imagery. Written in romanized Nepali; have
a native Nepali speaker convert to Devanagari script and review phrasing
before recording.
