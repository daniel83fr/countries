NEPAL
Official language: Nepali

TITLE: "Nepal, Agadi Badha" (Nepal, Move Forward)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Himalayan-fusion stadium anthem. Sarangi fiddle carrying the melodic line
over a madal hand-drum rhythm, bansuri flute texture in the verses,
opening into a full-band arena chorus with massed crowd vocals — the sound
of a Kathmandu Valley festival scaled up to a stadium.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Sarangi fiddle line over a slow madal drum pulse)
Nepal... agadi badha...

[Verse 1]
Sagarmatha touches sky where no one else has dared to stand,
Sherpas carried the whole world's dreams across this mountain land,
Prayer flags snapping color in the wind above the pass,
Every step uphill has taught us nothing here comes fast.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the temple bells of Kathmandu to the Everest ridge above,
One nation carved from mountains, carrying the same love.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Nepali + English)
Nepal, agadi badha! Nepal, agadi badha!
(Nepal, move forward! Nepal, move forward!)
Sabai mili, Nepal agadi badha!
(All together, Nepal moves forward!)

[Verse 2]
We didn't need the tallest towers to prove we touch the sky,
We built the roof of the whole world and still don't ask why,
Doesn't matter what the scoreboard or the map's edge shows,
The highest peak on earth is still the one that this land grows.

[Chorus]
Nepal, agadi badha! Nepal, agadi badha!
Sabai mili, Nepal agadi badha!

[Bridge]
We are the mountain that the whole world has to climb,
We are the patience carved by wind and time —
Prayer flag and drumbeat, ridge and river run,
This is the anthem of the roof beneath the sun.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Nepal, agadi badha! Nepal, agadi badha!
Sabai mili, Nepal agadi badha!
Agadi badha! Agadi badha!

[Outro — chant tag]
Nepal! Agadi badha!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Sagarmatha (Everest) and the real, globally admired Sherpa
mountaineering tradition, framing national pride around endurance and
elevation rather than generic imagery. Have a native Nepali speaker review
phrasing before recording.
