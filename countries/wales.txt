WALES
Official languages: Welsh, English

TITLE: "Yma o Hyd" (Still Here)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Welsh male-voice-choir (côr meibion) stadium anthem — rich layered
harmony in the style of Wales's world-famous choral tradition, opening
a cappella before drums and a terrace-stomp rhythm section enter beneath
the voices, building to a full-stadium unison roar on the chorus.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Wales's genuine male-voice-choir (côr meibion) singing tradition,
one of the country's most famous cultural exports, and on "Yma o Hyd"
("Still Here"), the real Dafydd Iwan song that has become an unofficial
Welsh football anthem sung by the Wales national team's supporters,
carrying its cultural-survival theme (a nation that has endured since
before the Romans) and its imagery of the red dragon flag. Have a native
Welsh speaker review the grammar and idiom before recording.
