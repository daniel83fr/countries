SOUTH KOREA
Official language: Korean

TITLE: "Dae-Han-Min-Guk!" (Republic of Korea!)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Samulnori-driven stadium anthem. The four traditional samulnori percussion
instruments (kkwaenggwari, jing, janggu, buk) build the entire rhythmic
backbone, gayageum melody threading the verses, exploding into a
K-pop-scale full-band chorus with the actual "Be the Reds" clap pattern
built into the beat.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Kkwaenggwari gong cuts through a rising samulnori pattern)
Daehan... Minguk...

[Verse 1]
The Han River carries neon down where the old palace stood,
A red sea of jerseys fills the plaza like it should,
Grandmother's janggu drum still keeps the rhythm strong and true,
Every generation learns the beat before they learn what to do.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the mountain temple bell to the plaza's screaming crowd,
One nation, red and roaring, singing out loud.

[Chorus] (viral hook — the real, iconic "Be the Reds" clap chant)
Clap clap, clap-clap-clap — Dae-Han-Min-Guk!
Clap clap, clap-clap-clap — Dae-Han-Min-Guk!
(Republic of Korea!)

[Verse 2]
The gong before the taekwondo kick still sets the fighter's pace,
A whole plaza turned to family in one single space,
Doesn't matter if it's football, gaming, or the silver screen,
This is a nation that turned watching into the loudest scene.

[Chorus]
Clap clap, clap-clap-clap — Dae-Han-Min-Guk!
Clap clap, clap-clap-clap — Dae-Han-Min-Guk!

[Bridge]
Gong and drum and a hundred thousand hands,
This is the sound that the whole plaza understands —
Red as the jerseys, loud as the drum,
This is the anthem of a nation overcome with pride.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Dae-Han-Min-Guk! Dae-Han-Min-Guk!
Clap clap, clap-clap-clap — Dae-Han-Min-Guk!

[Outro — chant tag]
Dae-Han-Min-Guk! (clap clap, clap-clap-clap)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built directly on the real "Dae-Han-Min-Guk" clap chant made famous by the
"Be the Reds" supporters during the 2002 World Cup — one of football's most
recognizable fan chants worldwide — paired with samulnori, Korea's
signature percussion quartet. This deliberately uses an existing, proven
viral hook rather than inventing a new one from scratch.
