GUYANA
Official language: English

TITLE: "Kaieteur Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Rainforest-rhythm stadium anthem. Amerindian shak-shak rattles and hand-
drums open the verses, waterfall-roar sound texture from Kaieteur Falls,
building into a full-band soca-tinged chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(A shak-shak rattle pattern rises under distant waterfall roar)
Guyana... rising...

[Verse 1]
Kaieteur drops higher than any single fall could dare,
Rainforest stretches farther than the eye can hold or share,
Six peoples in one nation, every color, every tongue,
And every match-day here still sings the song that we've become.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the falls that never rest to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One nation, six peoples, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Kaieteur rising! Kaieteur rising!
The falls that never rest under open skies,
Kaieteur rising! Kaieteur rising!
Guyana, Guyana, watch our nation rise.

[Verse 2]
The only English-speaking nation on this whole South American land,
Rice fields and rainforest reaching out from hand to hand,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the score,
We built a pride as tall as any waterfall or more.

[Chorus]
Kaieteur rising! Kaieteur rising!
The falls that never rest under open skies,
Kaieteur rising! Kaieteur rising!
Guyana, Guyana, watch our nation rise.

[Bridge]
Waterfall thunder and a rainforest wide,
Six peoples carrying one nation's pride —
Shak-shak rattle and a drummer's hand,
This is the anthem of the golden land.

[Final Chorus]
Kaieteur rising! Kaieteur rising!
Guyana, Guyana, watch our nation rise.

[Outro]
Guyana! Kaieteur! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Kaieteur Falls (the world's largest single-drop waterfall by
volume), and Guyana's real, distinctive identity as the only English-
speaking nation in South America, home to a genuine blend of Indian,
African, Amerindian, and other heritages. English is Guyana's official
language, so the full lyric is written directly in it.
