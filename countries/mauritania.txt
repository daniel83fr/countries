MAURITANIA
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Muritaniya" (O Mauritania)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Moorish griot fusion anthem. Tidinit lute and ardin harp (played by
griotte women in Moorish tradition) carry the melody over a hand-drum
desert rhythm, iron-ore train rumble as a low percussion texture, opening
into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Naghamat al-tidinit tartafi' fawqa daqqat tabl sahrawiya)
Ya Muritaniya... ya Muritaniya...

[Maqta' 1]
Al-sahra' al-kubra tamtaddu ahad al-tariq al-atwal fi al-ard,
Qitar al-hadid yamurru masafat la yahsibuha ahad,
Chinguetti tahfazu al-kutub al-qadima taht al-shams al-harra,
Wa kullu jeelin yahmilu hadhihi al-dhakira bi-kull hurriya.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min al-maktaba al-qadima ila al-mal'ab al-sakhib al-yawm,
Ummatun wahidatun, sabra, la tarkanu wa la tanam.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "Ya Muritaniya, ya habiba" means "O Mauritania, O beloved"

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the ardin harp (traditionally played by Moorish griotte women),
the ancient library city of Chinguetti (UNESCO-listed), and the famous
iron-ore train, one of the longest trains in the world. Have a native
Mauritanian Arabic (Hassaniya) speaker review phrasing before recording.
