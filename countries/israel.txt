ISRAEL
Official language: Hebrew (Arabic also widely spoken)

TITLE: "לב אחד" (Lev Echad — One Heart)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
A Mizrahi-rooted stadium anthem: darbuka hand-drum and oud carry the
opening groove in a rolling hora dance rhythm, the same circle-dance
pulse danced at Israeli celebrations for generations, building into a
full-band stadium chorus with call-and-response crowd vocals in the
Mizrahi pop tradition of artists who fused Middle Eastern maqam melody
with Western dance-pop.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Rooted in the hora, the circle dance central to Israeli communal
celebration since the early 20th century, and in Mizrahi music, the
popular genre blending Middle Eastern instruments like the darbuka and
oud with modern dance-pop that dominates Israeli radio and stadium
soundtracks alike. The lyric's throughline — a circle that no one
stands outside of — draws directly from the physical shape of the hora
itself, translated from dance floor to stadium terrace. Hebrew is the
primary official language and is used throughout; Arabic is also
widely spoken in Israel.
