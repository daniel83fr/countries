CAYMAN ISLANDS
Official language: English

TITLE: "Seven Mile Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Caymanian folk-fusion anthem. Fiddle and rake-and-scrape rhythm (the
Caymanian folk tradition) open the verses, coral-reef wave-wash texture,
building into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals along Seven
Mile Beach.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Rake-and-scrape rhythm builds under a fiddle line)
Cayman... rising...

[Verse 1]
Seven miles of sand as white as anything you'll find,
Stingrays glide through shallow water, gentle, unconfined,
Turtles nested here before the first boat ever came,
And every match-day here still calls out the island's name.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the sandbar in the shallows to the stadium's roaring crowd,
Three small islands, ocean-hearted, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Seven Mile rising! Seven Mile rising!
White sand and turtle water under open skies,
Seven Mile rising! Seven Mile rising!
Cayman, Cayman, watch our island rise.

[Verse 2]
Every diver in this water learns to move without a sound,
Every islander still knows the way this reef is bound,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the score,
We built a pride as deep as any reef or ocean floor.

[Chorus]
Seven Mile rising! Seven Mile rising!
White sand and turtle water under open skies,
Seven Mile rising! Seven Mile rising!
Cayman, Cayman, watch our island rise.

[Bridge]
Coral reef and turtle, sandbar in the tide,
Three small islands carrying an ocean-sized pride —
Rake-and-scrape rhythm, fiddle in the air,
This is the anthem of the island care.

[Final Chorus]
Seven Mile rising! Seven Mile rising!
Cayman, Cayman, watch our island rise.

[Outro]
Cayman! Seven Mile! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Seven Mile Beach, the Cayman Islands' most famous landmark, the
real sea turtle conservation heritage central to island identity, and
rake-and-scrape, a genuine Caymanian folk music tradition. English is the
official language, so the full lyric is written directly in it.
