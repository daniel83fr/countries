SIERRA LEONE
Official language: English

TITLE: "Salone, Rise"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Milo Jazz-and-gumbe stadium anthem. Milo Jazz acoustic guitar picking
(Sierra Leone's own Krio folk tradition) opens the verses, building with
goombay drum and horn stabs into a full-band chorus with massed crowd
vocals — Freetown street-corner music scaled to a stadium.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Milo Jazz guitar pattern builds under a soft goombay drum)
Salone... rise...

[Verse 1]
Freetown's harbor still remembers every ship that once came home,
A nation founded free, a diamond buried in the loam,
Krio words weave through the market like a river through the town,
And every match-day chant still wears that same old crown.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the harbor's morning light to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One small nation, hard-won, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Salone, rise! Salone, rise!
One country, one people, under these same skies,
Salone, rise! Salone, rise!
Leone Stars are shining, watch the whole coast rise.

[Verse 2]
War tried to take the guitar but the guitar never stopped,
Every string still rings the way our grandparents' hands had dropped,
Doesn't matter what the headlines said about us years ago,
We are still the nation that has always found a way to grow.

[Chorus]
Salone, rise! Salone, rise!
One country, one people, under these same skies,
Salone, rise! Salone, rise!
Leone Stars are shining, watch the whole coast rise.

[Bridge]
Diamond in the ground and rhythm in the hand,
This is the story of a healing land —
Guitar string still ringing through the years,
This is the anthem past a nation's fears.

[Final Chorus]
Salone, rise! Salone, rise!
Leone Stars are shining, watch the whole coast rise.

[Outro]
Salone! Rise! Rise!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Freetown's founding as a home for freed people, Milo Jazz (a real
Sierra Leonean guitar folk tradition), and "the Leone Stars," the national
football team's actual nickname. "Salone" is the country's own widely
used affectionate nickname for itself. English is Sierra Leone's official
language, so the full lyric is written directly in it.
