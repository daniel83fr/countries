ANGUILLA
Official language: English

TITLE: "Anguilla, We Rise"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Bashment soca stadium anthem. Anguilla's own bashment soca sound (born on
this small island) drives the whole track, steel pan runs and boat-racing
horn calls layered in, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd
vocals — the energy of an Anguilla Regatta scaled to a stadium.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Steel pan run over a rising bashment soca groove)
Anguilla... we rise...

[Verse 1]
Eleven miles of coral sand, the whitest that you'll find,
Boats race across the channel with the whole island behind,
No hotel high enough to hide the way this island glows,
Every beach here has a story only Anguilla knows.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the sail that cuts the channel to the crowd that packs the stand,
One small island, big-hearted, taking its stand.

[Chorus]
Anguilla, we rise! Anguilla, we rise!
Eleven miles of coral under open skies,
Anguilla, we rise! Anguilla, we rise!
Small island, big heart — watch Anguilla rise!

[Verse 2]
The boat race is our football long before the pitch was laid,
Every captain, every crew, a legend that was made,
Doesn't matter that the map can barely fit our name,
We built a pride as wide as any ocean, all the same.

[Chorus]
Anguilla, we rise! Anguilla, we rise!
Eleven miles of coral under open skies,
Anguilla, we rise! Anguilla, we rise!
Small island, big heart — watch Anguilla rise!

[Bridge]
Boat sail cutting channel, steel pan in the air,
A small island proving that the world should care —
Bashment soca beat and coral sand,
This is the anthem of the smallest stand.

[Final Chorus]
Anguilla, we rise! Anguilla, we rise!
Small island, big heart — watch Anguilla rise!

[Outro]
Anguilla! We rise! We rise!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Anguilla's real national sport — traditional wooden boat racing,
genuinely the island's most beloved competitive tradition — and bashment
soca, a musical style closely associated with the island. English is
Anguilla's official language, so the full lyric is written directly in it.
