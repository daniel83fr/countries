SRI LANKA
Official languages: Sinhala, Tamil

TITLE: "Jayawewa, Sri Lanka" (Victory, Sri Lanka)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Kandyan-fusion stadium anthem. Geta bera drums (the ceremonial Kandyan
dance drum) driving the rhythm, veena and flute melody lines, building
into a full-band arena chorus that carries both Sinhala and Tamil victory
phrases side by side, honoring the island's two official languages.

LYRICS (Sinhala verse, Tamil verse — Sri Lanka's two official languages):

[Arambaya]
(Geta bera drum roll builds over temple bell chimes)
Jayawewa... jayawewa...

[Pada 1 — Sinhala, romanized]
Liyakiyavili pituwak ha'gena vediya parana stupa yatin ath vaturu yathi,
Athin pipuna thea kolayen kandu udin ada'th pata thiyeni,
Loku vishvaka'ndaya jayagraha yak wasarak dahaatak ma'thakayehi thibe,
Podi dupatak yudhdhayakata dinaganeema pennuwa rathriyak.

[Kôrus ta Pera]
Kandu udin thea watu wala sita rathu wela'we ratu welakata,
Eka dupathak, bhaashaa deka, eka rasa'e sonduru bhaawayak.

[Kôrus] — the hook: "Jayawewa" (Sinhala) and "Vaazhga" (Tamil) both mean "victory/long live"
Jayawewa, Sri Lanka! Jayawewa, Sri Lanka!
Vaazhga, Sri Lanka! Jayawewa, Sri Lanka!
Jayawewa, Sri Lanka!

[Pada 2 — Tamil, romanized]

Pathhu allathu irupathu varudangalukku munpu score board enna sonnalum kavalai illai,
Intha theevu ovvoru siruthadaiyilirunthum ezhuvathai katrukondathu.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Anchors pride in the real, still-celebrated 1996 Cricket World Cup win —
Sri Lanka's most iconic sporting story — and the Kandyan drum-and-dance
tradition, while deliberately carrying victory phrases in both Sinhala
("Jayawewa") and Tamil ("Vaazhga") to reflect both official languages.
Have native Sinhala and Tamil speakers each review their half before
recording.
