GABON
Official language: French

TITLE: "Allez, les Panthères" (Go, the Panthers)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Bwiti-rooted rainforest anthem. Mongongo harp and ngombi lines echo the
sacred Bwiti musical tradition, layered with a balafon and hand-drum groove
from the forest, opening into a full-band Afro-pop chorus with massed
crowd vocals under a canopy of green.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Ngombi harp figure over a soft balafon and hand-drum pulse)
Gabon... allez, Gabon...

[Verse 1]
Rainforest covers nearly all the ground beneath our feet,
Elephants and gorillas share the paths where rivers meet,
The Bwiti drum has called the spirits since before the map was drawn,
And every match-day, that same calling echoes on and on.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the canopy's green shadow to the coastal, crowded street,
One nation, forest-hearted, moving to the beat.

[Chorus] (viral hook, French + English)
Allez, les Panthères! Go, Gabon!
Allez, les Panthères! Go, Gabon!
Ensemble on gagne — go, Gabon!
(Together we win — go, Gabon!)

[Verse 2]
The panther moves through forest shadow quiet till she strikes,
Green and yellow on the pitch beneath the stadium lights,
Doesn't matter what the smaller nations say we lack,
We protected our whole forest — now we're taking the attack.

[Chorus]
Allez, les Panthères! Go, Gabon!
Ensemble on gagne — go, Gabon!

[Bridge]
Ninety percent forest, and we kept it green,
The quietest kind of pride the world has seen —
Harp string and balafon, forest and the sun,
This is the anthem of Africa's Eden, one.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Allez, les Panthères! Go, Gabon!
Ensemble on gagne — go, Gabon!
Allez! Allez!

[Outro — chant tag]
Gabon! Allez! Allez!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Gabon's rainforest, which covers roughly 85% of the country and
is genuinely called "Africa's Eden," the Bwiti spiritual and musical
tradition central to Fang culture, and "Les Panthères" (the Panthers), the
national football team's real nickname. Have a native French speaker
review phrasing before recording.
