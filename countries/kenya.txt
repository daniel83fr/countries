KENYA
Official languages: Swahili, English

TITLE: "Kenya, Harambee" (Kenya, Pull Together)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Benga-driven stadium anthem. Benga guitar lines (Kenya's own genre, born
of nyatiti string traditions) carrying the melody over a running-cadence
percussion pattern — literally paced like a distance runner's stride —
opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Swahili):

[Utangulizi]
(Gitaa la benga linaanza, ngoma ya mwendo ikifuata)
Kenya... harambee...

[Ubeti 1]

[Kabla ya Kiitikio]

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built entirely on "Harambee" ("let's all pull together"), Kenya's actual
national motto, appearing on its coat of arms, plus the Rift Valley's real
and legendary distance-running culture that has produced some of the
world's greatest marathoners. Have a native Swahili speaker review the
grammar and idiom before recording.
