BRITISH VIRGIN ISLANDS
Official language: English

TITLE: "Sailing Home"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Fungi-driven stadium anthem. Fungi music (the BVI's own scratch-band
genre, played on washboard, gourd, and guitar) carries the rhythm, sail-
wind and rigging-clank sound texture, opening into a full-band chorus with
massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Washboard scratch rhythm builds under a gourd shaker)
BVI... sailing home...

[Verse 1]
Sixty islands and cays scattered like a fleet at rest,
Every sail that crosses here still passes through the best,
Foxy sang the old year out the way it's always been,
And every match-day here still carries what these waters mean.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the anchorage at sunset to the stadium's roaring crowd,
Small territory, big waters, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Sailing home! Sailing home!
Sixty islands and every one still calls us home,
Sailing home! Sailing home!
BVI, BVI, wherever we roam.

[Verse 2]
Sage Mountain watches over every channel, every bay,
Every islander still finds their way back home someday,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations fill the map's whole frame,
The sailing capital still knows exactly how to claim its name.

[Chorus]
Sailing home! Sailing home!
Sixty islands and every one still calls us home,
Sailing home! Sailing home!
BVI, BVI, wherever we roam.

[Bridge]
Sail and rigging, washboard and the tide,
Sixty islands carrying an ocean-sized pride —
Fungi rhythm and a gourd shaker's sound,
This is the anthem of the sailing ground.

[Final Chorus]
Sailing home! Sailing home!
BVI, BVI, wherever we roam.

[Outro]
BVI! Sailing home!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the BVI's real reputation as "the sailing capital of the world,"
its sixty-plus islands and cays, and fungi music, a genuine local scratch-
band genre. English is the official language, so the full lyric is
written directly in it.
