LEBANON
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Loubnan" (O Lebanon)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Oud-driven Levantine stadium anthem. Oud and buzuq carrying the melodic
line (Lebanon's storied oud tradition front and center), derbake hand-drum
rhythm underneath, dabke-style stomping beat building the chorus, opening
into a full-band arena sound with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Solo oud rises over a slow derbake pulse)
Ya Loubnan... ya Loubnan...

[Maqta' 1]
Al-arz waqifun 'ala al-jabal mundhu qabla aqdam ughniya,
Al-bahhara al-Finiqiyun ghadaru min huna li-yahmilu al-'alam ma'ahum,
Beirut tu'idu bina'a nafsaha kulla marra yaktubuha al-'alam mayyita,
Wa kulla marra yasfiru al-hakam, narfa'u ru'usana badalan min dhalik.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min al-arz 'ala al-qimma ila shati' al-Mutawassit,
Qalb hadhihi al-balad al-saghira nija min kathir qabl al-yawm.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "Ya Loubnan al-habib" means "O beloved Lebanon"
Ya Loubnan! Ya Loubnan al-habib!
Yalla Loubnan, ya Loubnan al-habib!
Ya Loubnan! Ya Loubnan al-habib!

[Maqta' 2]
Aqdam al-dabke tadribu fi da'ira, katif ila katif bi-quwwa,
Nafs al-khatwa allati raqasaha ajdaduna taht daw' asa'ab,
La yuhimmu ma akhadhathu al-sinin, ma yatabaqqa,
Huwa arz 'ala 'alam la yanhani abadan taht al-dughut.

[al-Jawqa]
Ya Loubnan! Ya Loubnan al-habib!
Yalla Loubnan, ya Loubnan al-habib!

[al-Jisr]
Nahnu al-arz al-mutajadhdhir fi al-hajar al-qadim,
Nahnu al-bahhara alladhina ta'allamu al-tajwal awwalan —
U'ida bina'una mi'at marra walakin lam nazul abadan,
Hadhihi ughniyatu Loubnan allati tastamirr.

[al-Jawqa al-Akhira]
Ya Loubnan! Ya Loubnan al-habib!

Ya Loubnan! Ya Loubnan!

[al-Khatima]
Loubnan! Loubnan! Ya habib!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Centers on the cedar tree (Lebanon's flag emblem and a real, ancient
national symbol), the country's Phoenician maritime legacy, dabke (the
shoulder-linked line dance danced across Lebanon), and Beirut's
well-documented history of rebuilding after crisis. Have a native Lebanese
Arabic speaker review phrasing before recording.
