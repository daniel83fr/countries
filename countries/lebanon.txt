LEBANON
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Loubnan" (O Lebanon)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Oud-driven Levantine stadium anthem. Oud and buzuq carrying the melodic
line (Lebanon's storied oud tradition front and center), derbake hand-drum
rhythm underneath, dabke-style stomping beat building the chorus, opening
into a full-band arena sound with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Solo oud rises over a slow derbake pulse)
Ya Loubnan... ya Loubnan...

[Verse 1]
Cedars standing on the mountain since before the oldest song,
Phoenician sailors left from here to carry the world along,
Beirut rebuilds itself each time the world writes it off for dead,
And every time the whistle blows we lift our heads instead.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the cedar on the peak to the Mediterranean shore,
This small country's heartbeat has survived so much before.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Arabic + English)
Ya Loubnan! Ya Loubnan al-habib!
(O Lebanon! O beloved Lebanon!)
Yalla Loubnan, ya Loubnan al-habib!
(Come on Lebanon, O beloved Lebanon!)

[Verse 2]
Dabke feet stamping in a circle, shoulder locked to shoulder tight,
The same step that our grandparents danced under harder light,
Doesn't matter what the years have taken, what remains,
Is a cedar on a flag that never bends beneath the strain.

[Chorus]
Ya Loubnan! Ya Loubnan al-habib!
Yalla Loubnan, ya Loubnan al-habib!

[Bridge]
We are the cedar rooted deep in ancient stone,
We are the sailors who first learned to roam —
Rebuilt a hundred times but never gone,
This is the anthem that Lebanon carries on.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Ya Loubnan! Ya Loubnan al-habib!
Yalla Loubnan, ya Loubnan al-habib!
Ya Loubnan! Ya Loubnan!

[Outro — chant tag]
Loubnan! Loubnan! Ya habib!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Centers on the cedar tree (Lebanon's flag emblem and a real, ancient
national symbol), the country's Phoenician maritime legacy, dabke (the
shoulder-linked line dance danced across Lebanon), and Beirut's
well-documented history of rebuilding after crisis. Have a native Lebanese
Arabic speaker review phrasing before recording.
