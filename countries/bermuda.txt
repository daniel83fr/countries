BERMUDA
Official language: English

TITLE: "Gombey Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Gombey-driven island anthem. Gombey drums, whistles, and fife carry the
entire rhythm — Bermuda's real, distinctive masked dance tradition —
building into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals over pink-sand
shores.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Gombey drum roll builds under a piercing whistle call)
Bermuda... rising...

[Verse 1]
Pink sand catches sunset like it's blushing at the sea,
Gombey dancers spin in masks that carry history,
Every whistle, every drumbeat, marks a story handed down,
And every match-day here still wears that same old crown.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the pink sand shore at sunset to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One small island, deep-rooted, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Gombey rising! Gombey rising!
Drum and whistle calling from every open door,
Gombey rising! Gombey rising!
Bermuda, Bermuda, hear the island roar.

[Verse 2]
The mask hides the dancer but never hides the beat,
Every generation learns the rhythm on their feet,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the years,
We built a pride out of pink sand and Gombey cheers.

[Chorus]
Gombey rising! Gombey rising!
Drum and whistle calling from every open door,
Gombey rising! Gombey rising!
Bermuda, Bermuda, hear the island roar.

[Bridge]
Mask and drum and pink sand shore,
An island proud of everything it stands for —
Gombey whistle piercing through the crowd,
This is the anthem sung out loud.

[Final Chorus]
Gombey rising! Gombey rising!
Bermuda, Bermuda, hear the island roar.

[Outro]
Bermuda! Gombey! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built entirely on Gombey, Bermuda's real and distinctive masked dance and
drumming tradition performed at holidays and celebrations, and the
island's famous pink-sand beaches. English is Bermuda's official language,
so the full lyric is written directly in it.
