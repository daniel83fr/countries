GAMBIA
Official language: English

TITLE: "Smiling Coast Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Kora-driven riverside anthem. Kora (21-string griot harp) carrying the
melodic hook over a sabar hand-drum rhythm, the Gambia River's slow current
set to a laid-back Afro-pop groove, opening into a full-band chorus with
massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Kora arpeggio ripples over a soft sabar pulse)
Gambia... rising...

[Verse 1]
The river cuts the whole country like a spine along the map,
A griot's kora string still holds every family's history and gap,
Smallest nation on the mainland, but the warmest coast you'll find,
Every stranger becomes family here in no time.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the river's slow bend to the ocean's smiling shore,
One small nation with a heart that's holding so much more.

[Chorus] (viral hook, English)
Smiling Coast, rising! Smiling Coast, rising!
Small in size but big in heart — Gambia, rising!
Smiling Coast, rising! Smiling Coast, rising!
Scorpions strike — Gambia, rising!

[Verse 2]
The Scorpions strike quick on the pitch the way the sun strikes gold,
A small nation's story that the world's still learning, still being told,
Doesn't matter how the bigger neighbors fill the map's whole frame,
We built a smile into a coastline, built a smile into a name.

[Chorus]
Smiling Coast, rising! Smiling Coast, rising!
Small in size but big in heart — Gambia, rising!

[Bridge]
Kora string still ringing down the river's bend,
A story of a smile that never has to end —
Sabar drum and river current, side by side,
This is the anthem of the smallest coast with the biggest pride.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Smiling Coast, rising! Smiling Coast, rising!
Small in size but big in heart — Gambia, rising!

[Outro — chant tag]
Gambia! Smiling Coast! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on "the Smiling Coast," Gambia's real, widely used national nickname,
the kora griot tradition central to Gambian and wider Mandinka culture, and
"the Scorpions," the actual nickname of the national football team.
