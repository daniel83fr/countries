ICELAND
Official language: Icelandic

TITLE: "Þruman" (The Thunderclap)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Built entirely around the real "Viking Thunderclap" — a slow, deliberate
single handclap and "HÚ!" shout, repeating and accelerating until it
becomes a full percussive wall. Icelandic folk-chant vocals (drawing on
the country's tvísöngur two-part singing tradition) sit over glacial,
reverb-heavy synth drones evoking lava fields, before the clap pattern
drives the song into a euphoric stadium climax.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built entirely on the "Viking Thunderclap" (Íslenska klappið), the slow,
building clap-and-"Húh!" ritual Iceland's fans made world-famous during
the Euro 2016 run, when a nation of roughly 330,000 people — the
smallest ever to reach a major tournament — knocked out England. The
song's structure literally mimics the ritual: starting with a single
slow clap and accelerating into a wall of sound, framed against
Iceland's volcanic and glacial landscape.
