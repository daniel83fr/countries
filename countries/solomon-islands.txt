SOLOMON ISLANDS
Official language: English

TITLE: "Small Islands, Big Roar"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
A stadium anthem built from Solomon Islands' own 'Are'are panpipe
ensemble tradition — bamboo panpipe runs interlocking in the tight,
buzzing polyrhythm of a Malaita panpipe orchestra — layered over an
island string-band guitar-and-ukulele groove, the kind played at every
Honiara gathering, with massed call-and-response crowd vocals driving
the chorus.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Bamboo panpipes lock into a rolling pattern, string-band guitar answers)
Solomon Islands... small islands, big roar...

[Verse 1]
Lawson Tama fills up long before the whistle sounds,
Honiara's heat and heartbeat pressed into the ground,
Nobody gave us football, nobody gave us the game,
We built it out of coral, and we built it out of rain.

[Pre-Chorus]
No stadium lights as bright as a thousand islands' pride,
Kurukuru showed the whole wide world what's carried on our side.

[Chorus]
Small islands, big roar! Small islands, big roar!
Solomon, Solomon, that's the flag we're fighting for,
Small islands, big roar! Small islands, big roar!
From Malaita to Guadalcanal, we all come through that door.

[Verse 2]
Panpipes ringing out from Malaita's mountain air,
Carried into concrete stands, carried everywhere,
Futsal court or grass pitch, doesn't matter what's the size,
Bonito swims against the tide and still it takes the prize.

[Chorus]
Small islands, big roar! Small islands, big roar!
Solomon, Solomon, that's the flag we're fighting for,
Small islands, big roar! Small islands, big roar!
From Malaita to Guadalcanal, we all come through that door.

[Bridge]
Nine hundred islands, one same drum,
One same panpipe song when the final minutes come,
They said we're too small, they said we'd never rise,
Watch us fill the stadium and watch us take the skies.

[Final Chorus]
Small islands, big roar! Small islands, big roar!
Solomon, Solomon, that's the flag we're fighting for!

[Outro]
Small islands... big roar... Solomon Islands!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Anchored on two real, specific things: Lawson Tama Stadium in Honiara,
the country's national ground, and Kurukuru, the nickname of the Solomon
Islands national futsal team, which stunned the football world by
reaching the 2008 FIFA Futsal World Cup and remains the Pacific's most
storied minnow success. The bamboo panpipe orchestras of Malaita (as
documented by ethnomusicologist Hugo Zemp) are one of the region's most
distinctive living musical traditions. English is the sole official
language, so the lyric is written directly in it, with Melanesian Pijin
place-names and terms kept intact.
