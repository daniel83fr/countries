BOSNIA AND HERZEGOVINA
Official languages: Bosnian, Croatian, Serbian

TITLE: "Zmajevi Sevdaha" (Dragons of Sevdah)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Sevdalinka-rooted stadium anthem — Bosnia's soulful, ornamented folk
song tradition, usually built on accordion and saz, its melismatic
vocal turns full of longing (sevdah) — rearranged with a full band and
stadium drums so the aching melody opens into a driving, terrace-ready
chorus. Vocal delivery stays expressive and ornamented even at full
volume.

LYRICS (Bosnian):

[Uvod]
(Harmonika uzdiše, pa ulazi bubanj)
Zmajevi... sevdah... napred...

[Strofa 1]
Sa Miljacke do Neretve pjesma putuje sama,
Sevdalinka nosi bol i ljubav u istom dahu,
Zmajevi na dresu nose vatru starih pjesama,
Cijela zemlja pjeva jednim glasom, jednim sevdahu.

[Predrefren]
Od stare čaršije do stadionske vike,
Jedno srce, jedna zemlja, pjesma nas sve stiže.

[Refren] — the hook: "Zmajevi, napred! Bosna je u nama!" means "Dragons, forward! Bosnia is within us!"
Zmajevi, napred! Bosna je u nama!
Pjevaj glasno, cijela zemljo!
Zmajevi, napred! Bosna je u nama!
Sevdah gori u nama!

[Strofa 2]
Svaki igrač nosi grb i ljubičastu boju,
Svaki gol se slavi kao stara pjesma dobru,
Neretva zelena, Miljacka tiho teče,
Ali kad zmajevi trče, cijela zemlja vrišti jače.

[Refren]
Zmajevi, napred! Bosna je u nama!
Pjevaj glasno, cijela zemljo!

[Mostovi]
Harmonika plače, pa se pretvara u krik,
Saz i bubanj zajedno, kao jedan jedini stih,
Sevdah nije samo tuga, sevdah je i pobjeda,
Ovo je pjesma zmajeva koja nikad ne blijedi.

[Zadnji refren]
Zmajevi, napred! Bosna je u nama!
Sevdah gori u nama!

[Kraj]
Zmajevi! Sevdah! Napred!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on sevdalinka, the traditional Bosnian urban folk-song genre
carrying "sevdah" — a word for aching, bittersweet longing — usually
sung with accordion or saz accompaniment, and on "Zmajevi" (Dragons),
the real nickname of the Bosnia and Herzegovina national football team.
The Miljacka (Sarajevo's river) and the Neretva (running through
Mostar) tie the song to both halves of the country. Bosnian, Croatian
and Serbian are mutually intelligible, so the lyric is written in
standard Bosnian; have a native speaker review grammar and idiom before
recording.
