GREECE
Official language: Greek

TITLE: "Πρωταθλητές της Ευρώπης" (Champions of Europe)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Rebetiko-rooted stadium anthem: a weeping bouzouki taxim opens over a
laïko 9/8-leaning groove, baglama chiming underneath, building from a
smoky, tavern-style lament into a full brass-and-crowd stadium roar —
the sound of Piraeus backstreets scaled up to sixty thousand voices.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Rooted in rebetiko and its descendant laïko, Greece's foundational urban
folk music born in Piraeus's dockside tavernas and carried by the
bouzouki and baglama. The lyric's spine is the real 2004 UEFA European
Championship win in Portugal, when Greece — long dismissed as a
minor footballing nation — beat the host nation in the final, one of
the biggest upsets in tournament football history and still the
country's proudest sporting memory.
