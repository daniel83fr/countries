VANUATU
Official languages: Bislama, English, French

TITLE: "Kastom Draem"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Vanuatu string-band music — ukulele, acoustic guitar and improvised
bamboo bass driving an island reggae-influenced groove — layered with
the deep, hollow pulse of the tam tam kastom slit-drum used in village
ceremonies, building to a Bislama stadium chant sung in full crowd
call-and-response.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Vanuatu's own string-band tradition, the guitar-and-ukulele
sound heard at every village nakamal and market since the mid-20th
century, and the tam tam, the carved wooden slit-drum central to kastom
(traditional) ceremony across the archipelago. The imagery draws on
Vanuatu's real geography — Port Vila Municipal Stadium and the active
volcanoes on islands like Tanna and Ambrym — as a metaphor for quiet
strength beneath the surface. Bislama, the true lingua franca and one of
three official languages, carries the full lyric.
