LIBYA
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Libya" (O Libya)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Bedouin-Mediterranean fusion anthem. Oud and zokra bagpipe carry the
melody over a derbake hand-drum rhythm, desert-wind textures giving way
to Mediterranean coastal percussion, opening into a full-band arena chorus
with massed crowd vocals.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Libya's Mediterranean coastline, the Roman ruins of Leptis Magna
(a real, UNESCO-listed landmark), and its Bedouin desert heritage. Have a
native Libyan Arabic speaker review grammar, dialect, and idiom before
recording — the transliteration here is a starting draft, not
production-ready Arabic script.
