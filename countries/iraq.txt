IRAQ
Official languages: Arabic, Kurdish

TITLE: "Ya Iraq al-Habib" (O Beloved Iraq)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Iraqi maqam-fusion stadium anthem. Oud and santur carrying the melodic
maqam line, khishba/dumbek hand-drum rhythm underneath, joined by a Kurdish
daf pulse in the bridge to honor both official languages, building into a
full-band arena chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Oud maqam figure over a slow hand-drum heartbeat)
Ya Iraq... ya habibi...

[Verse 1]
Between two rivers older than the words we use to pray,
Tigris and Euphrates still keep carrying us today,
Palm trees stood through every storm that tried to bring us down,
Every neighborhood still plays the ball through every town.

[Pre-Chorus]
From Baghdad's bridges to the Kurdish mountain air,
One country, many voices, but one shirt that we all wear.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Arabic + English)
Ya Iraq! Ya Iraq al-habib!
(O Iraq! O beloved Iraq!)
Nasr al-Iraq, ya Iraq al-habib!
(Victory for Iraq, O beloved Iraq!)

[Verse 2]
They said this cradle of the world had nothing left to give,
Then eleven men in blue reminded us how we still live,
The whole country stopped — Arab, Kurd, and everyone between —
For ninety minutes we were the most united scene.

[Chorus]
Ya Iraq! Ya Iraq al-habib!
Nasr al-Iraq, ya Iraq al-habib!

[Bridge]
Two rivers flowing into one same sea,
Two languages, one song, one victory —
The palm tree bends but never breaks in the storm,
This is the shape that Iraqi pride can form.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Ya Iraq! Ya Iraq al-habib!
Nasr al-Iraq, ya Iraq al-habib!
Ya Iraq! Ya Iraq!

[Outro — chant tag]
Iraq! Iraq! Ya habib!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Grounded in the Tigris and Euphrates rivers that define Mesopotamia, and
the real, widely remembered story of Iraq's 2007 AFC Asian Cup win, which
briefly united a country divided along sectarian lines around its football
team. Weaves in a Kurdish daf pulse to acknowledge Iraq's two official
languages. Have native Arabic and Kurdish speakers review phrasing before
recording.
