CONGO
Official language: French (Lingala and Kituba widely spoken)

TITLE: "Allez, Congo"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Rumba congolaise stadium anthem. Guitar-driven soukous rumba lines — the
genre Congo-Brazzaville helped give the whole world, recognized by UNESCO
— carrying the melody over a sebene guitar-solo build, exploding into a
full-band dance-rock chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Rumba guitar figure builds over a soft conga pattern)
Congo... allez, Congo...

[Verse 1]
The river runs so deep it hides the sun beneath its skin,
Rumba guitars taught the whole wide world just how to spin,
Sapeurs walk the street in colors brighter than the sky,
Elegance is armor here, and no one asks us why.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the river's endless current to the rumba dance-floor light,
One nation, style and rhythm, moving through the night.

[Chorus] (viral hook, French + English)
Allez, Congo! Allez, Congo!
(Go, Congo! Go, Congo!)
Ensemble on danse — allez, Congo!
(Together we dance — go, Congo!)

[Verse 2]
Every guitar solo builds until the whole crowd starts to sway,
The same way rumba's carried joy through every hard-won day,
Doesn't matter what the years or borders tried to draw,
We turned the river's rhythm into something like a law.

[Chorus]
Allez, Congo! Allez, Congo!
Ensemble on danse — allez, Congo!

[Bridge]
Guitar string bending like the river's bend,
A rhythm strong enough that it will never end —
Sapeur's suit and rumba's sway,
This is the anthem for the game-day sway.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Allez, Congo! Allez, Congo!
Ensemble on danse — allez, Congo!
Allez! Allez!

[Outro — chant tag]
Congo! Allez! Allez!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on rumba congolaise, the guitar-driven genre born on both banks of
the Congo River and inscribed by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage,
and La Sape, the real and distinctive Congolese culture of elegant
dressing (the sapeurs). Have a native French/Lingala speaker review
phrasing before recording.
