GIBRALTAR
Official language: English

TITLE: "Rock Solid"
(Llanito terrace chant — English with local Spanish-Gibraltarian flavor)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
A scrappy, brass-and-drum terrace chant built for the Victoria Stadium's
tiny but ferocious home end — closer to a British-style football terrace
sing-along than a big-nation anthem, deliberately small and defiant,
with a Mediterranean lilt underneath from the local Llanito habit of
switching between English and Spanish mid-line, handclaps driving the
tempo like a crowd that knows everyone in it by name.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Handclaps start, one small end singing loud)
Come on you Rock... vamos, Gibraltar...

[Verse 1]
Two and a half square miles and a limestone Rock behind us,
Barbary macaques watching from the ridge above the ground,
Everybody in this end, we all know each other's faces,
Smallest nation on the pitch but we never make a sound... quieter.

[Pre-Chorus]
They said we were too small to ever get a game,
Now UEFA's got us listed, mate, and singing out our name.

[Chorus] — the terrace hook
Rock solid! Rock solid!
Gibraltar, one Rock, uno equipo!
Rock solid! Rock solid!
Pequeño pero grande, that's the creed we keep.

[Verse 2]
From Main Street down to Casemates, the whole town's out tonight,
Llanito on the tongue, red and white on every scarf,
No one gave us minutes, no one gave us a chance,
So every point we get here, mate, is worth the whole romance.

[Chorus]
Rock solid! Rock solid!
Gibraltar, one Rock, uno equipo!
Rock solid! Rock solid!
Pequeño pero grande, that's the creed we keep.

[Bridge]
The Rock don't move, the Rock don't fall,
Been standing since before there were nations at all,
Ninety minutes, give it everything you've got,
Chico, this is our home and we forget it not.

[Final Chorus]
Rock solid! Rock solid!
Gibraltar, one Rock, uno equipo!
Rock solid! Rock solid!

[Outro]
Vamos, Gibraltar... Rock solid... vamos.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Gibraltar's official language is English, but everyday identity runs on
Llanito, the local code-switched blend of English and Andalusian
Spanish that flips mid-sentence ("uno equipo," "pequeño pero grande,"
"vamos") — a real, defining marker of Gibraltarian speech. The lyric
leans on Gibraltar's genuine underdog story: a territory of about
34,000 people and 2.6 square miles that only gained full UEFA and FIFA
membership in 2013-2016, plus the Rock itself and its resident Barbary
macaques, the only wild primates in Europe, as the literal backdrop to
every home match at Victoria Stadium.
