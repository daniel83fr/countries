PAKISTAN
Official languages: Urdu, English

TITLE: "Pakistan Zindabad" (Long Live Pakistan)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Qawwali-fusion stadium anthem. Tabla and dholak driving the rhythm,
harmonium runs and hand-claps building the qawwali call-and-response,
exploding into a full-band arena chorus with massed crowd vocals — the
energy of a packed National Stadium turned into a record.

LYRICS (Urdu, romanized):

[Ibtida]
(Harmonium drone rises under a rolling dholak pattern)
Pakistan... zindabad...

[Band 1]
K2 aksar qaumon ke khwab se bhi zyada buland khara hai,
Dariya-e-Sindh ne saltanaton ko is parcham se pehle hi sambhala,
Gali ke cricket mein lakdi ke bat se izzat aur shaan ka khel hota hai,
Har gali ke bachche ki wahi ek hi team dil mein basi hai.

[Chorus Se Pehle]
Qarakoram ke saaye se Arabi samandar ki lehron tak,
Ek qaum, sabz o safed, kandhe se kandha mila kar khari hai.

[Chorus] — a real, iconic national chant meaning "long live Pakistan"
Pakistan, zindabad! Pakistan, zindabad!
Sabse pehle, Pakistan zindabad!
Pakistan, zindabad! Pakistan, zindabad!

[Band 2]
Qawwal ki taali bowler ke daudne se pehle raftar set karti hai,
Ek arab chhaton se log akhri gend ka intezar karte hain,
Farq nahi padta guzashta baras ya scoreboard ne kya kaha,
Hum ne pahadon se ek qaum banayi hai — hum jaante hain qeemat kya hoti hai.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on "Pakistan Zindabad" ("Long live Pakistan"), a genuine, deeply
iconic national chant, alongside K2 and the Indus River — real,
non-generic landmarks — plus the qawwali hand-clap tradition and street
cricket, the country's true sporting religion. Written in romanized Urdu;
have a native Urdu speaker convert to Nastaliq script and review phrasing
before recording.
