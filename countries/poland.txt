POLAND
Official language: Polish

TITLE: "Biało-Czerwoni"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
A Podhale highlander-fiddle stadium anthem. The Podhale style — fast,
biting fiddle (skrzypce) lines and a droning basy bass, born in the
Tatra mountain villages around Zakopane — opens the track before it
slams into a flare-lit ultras terrace chant tempo, heavy drums and
massed crowd vocals carrying the hook.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on "Biało-Czerwoni" ("White-and-Reds"), the genuine, universally
used nickname of the Polish national team taken from the flag, and on
the Podhale highlander music of the Tatra mountains around Zakopane —
its fast fiddle (skrzypce) and droning basy are real, specific Polish
folk instruments, not generic "folk fiddle." The flare-lit atmosphere
reflects Poland's famously intense ultras stadium culture. Have a native
Polish speaker review the grammar and idiom before recording.
