MONTENEGRO
Official language: Montenegrin

TITLE: "Pjesma sa Lovćena" (Song from Lovćen)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
A gusle-rooted stadium anthem. The gusle is Montenegro's single-stringed
epic instrument, traditionally bowed to accompany a chanted historical
tale; here its droning, keening line opens the track as it would open a
mountain epic, before a heavy-footed brass-and-drum march built for a
small, proud nation's terrace enters under massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Uvod]
(Gusle ječi sama, kao pred staru pjesmu)
Crna Gora... sa vrha do mora...

[Stih 1]
Lovćen gleda dolje na uvale Jadrana,
Kotor spava u sjeni zidina starih vjekova,
Mi smo mali na karti, al' veliki u srcu,
Svaki kamen ovih planina znao je za slobodu.

[Pred-Refren]
Od gusala što pjevaju u tišini planinskoj,
Do stadiona koji viče kao jedan jedini glas.

[Refren] — the terrace hook, "Crna Gora, napred!" means "Montenegro, forward!"
Crna Gora, napred! Crna Gora, napred!
Sa Lovćena do mora, napred!
Crna Gora, napred! Crna Gora, napred!
Mali narod, veliko srce, napred!

[Stih 2]
Nemamo broj, al' imamo stijenu koja ne pada,
Svaki dječak iz Podgorice sanja isti dres,
Jadran nam čuva obalu, a planine dušu,
Svaki gol je kamen dodat na naš stari zid.

[Refren]
Crna Gora, napred! Crna Gora, napred!
Sa Lovćena do mora, napred!

[Most]
Gusle plaču staru priču o narodu koji ne kleči,
Truba i bubnjevi nose je dalje, do svakog reda,
Od crnih planina do mora koje sija,
Ovo je pjesma naroda koji se ne gubi.

[Završni Refren]
Crna Gora, napred! Crna Gora, napred!
Sa Lovćena do mora, napred!
Crna Gora, napred!

[Kraj]
Crna Gora! Napred, do kraja!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the gusle, Montenegro's UNESCO-recognized single-stringed epic
instrument used for centuries to sing the history of a small, mountainous
nation, and on Montenegro's real geography: Mount Lovćen, the sacred
mountain overlooking the Bay of Kotor, and the dramatic drop from
highland peaks to the Adriatic coast in the space of a few kilometers.
The lyric leans on Montenegro's genuine pride as one of Europe's smallest
football nations. Have a native Montenegrin speaker review the grammar
and idiom before recording.
