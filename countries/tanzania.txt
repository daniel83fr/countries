TANZANIA
Official languages: Swahili, English

TITLE: "Tanzania, Mbele" (Tanzania, Forward)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Taarab-and-Serengeti fusion anthem. Taarab strings and oud from Zanzibar
carry the melody over a mainland ngoma drum rhythm, savanna-wind ambience
evoking the Serengeti, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd
vocals under Kilimanjaro's snow-capped peak.

LYRICS (Swahili):

[Utangulizi]
(Nyuzi za taarab zinapaa juu ya ngoma ya polepole)
Tanzania... mbele...

[Ubeti 1]
Kilimanjaro linasimama juu kuliko milima yote barani,
Wanyama wa Serengeti wanakimbia kama upepo wa asubuhi,
Zanzibar inaimba taarab kando ya bahari ya Hindi,
Na kila mchezo unaanza na wimbo huu wa nchi.

[Kabla ya Kiitikio]
Kutoka kilele cha theluji hadi savana isiyo na mwisho,
Taifa moja, tumeungana, tunasonga mbele kwa moyo.

[Kiitikio] — the hook: "Tanzania, mbele" means "Tanzania, forward"
Tanzania, mbele! Tanzania, mbele!
Umoja ni nguvu — Tanzania, mbele!
Tanzania, mbele! Tanzania, mbele!

[Ubeti 2]
Taarab inasimulia mapenzi na historia ya pwani,
Ngoma ya bara inabeba nguvu ya milima na tambarare,
Haijalishi bara zima linatuita taifa dogo,
Sisi ni nchi yenye mlima mrefu kuliko wote hapo.

[Kiitikio]
Tanzania, mbele! Tanzania, mbele!
Umoja ni nguvu — Tanzania, mbele!

[Daraja]
Mlima na savana, bahari na kisiwa,
Taifa moja tu, lililojengwa na umoja —
Nyuzi za taarab na ngoma ya bara,
Huu ni wimbo wa nchi yenye fahari.

[Kiitikio cha Mwisho]
Tanzania, mbele! Tanzania, mbele!
Umoja ni nguvu — Tanzania, mbele!
Mbele! Mbele!

[Mwisho]
Tanzania! Mbele! Mbele!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Mount Kilimanjaro (Africa's highest peak), the Serengeti, and
Zanzibar's taarab musical tradition, spanning both the mainland and island
halves of the country. "Umoja ni nguvu" ("unity is strength") reflects a
real, commonly used Swahili civic phrase. Have a native Swahili speaker
review phrasing before recording.
