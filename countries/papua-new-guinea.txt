PAPUA NEW GUINEA
Official languages: Tok Pisin, English, Hiri Motu

TITLE: "Kundu i Krai" (The Kundu Drum Cries Out)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
PNG string-band anthem. String band music — PNG's own homegrown genre of
acoustic guitar and ukulele picking — carries the melody over a kundu
hand-drum pulse, the same skin drum used at highland sing-sing festivals,
with a massed Tok Pisin call-and-response chorus.

CULTURAL NOTE:
The kundu is a real hand-carved hourglass skin drum central to Papua New
Guinea's highland sing-sing festivals and traditional bilas (ceremonial
dress and body paint) displays, paired here with PNG's own string-band
guitar tradition. "Kapul" (cuscus, a native tree-dwelling marsupial) is a
recognized nickname used for PNG national sporting sides, and "wanbel"
(literally "one belly," meaning unity) is genuine everyday Tok Pisin for
being of one heart and mind.
