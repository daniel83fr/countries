HUNGARY
Official language: Hungarian

TITLE: "Aranycsapat Szíve" (Heart of the Golden Team)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
A csárdás-rooted stadium anthem: it opens lassú (slow), a solo violin
and cimbalom weeping over the melody in verbunkos style, then breaks
into a friss (fast) stomping 2/4 surge — the same slow-to-fast csárdás
structure danced at Hungarian weddings for two centuries — scaled up
with stadium drums and a massed male-choir chorus.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Structured on the real csárdás, Hungary's national dance form, which
moves from a slow lassú into a fast, stomping friss, mirrored here by
the song's own slow-to-fierce build, with verbunkos (18th-century
military recruiting music) coloring the rhythm. The lyric centers on
the legendary "Aranycsapat" (Golden Team) of the 1950s, led by Ferenc
Puskás and Nándor Hidegkuti, most famous for the 6-3 demolition of
England at Wembley in 1953 — still the single most mythologized result
in Hungarian football history.
