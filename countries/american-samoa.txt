AMERICAN SAMOA
Official languages: Samoan, English

TITLE: "Nicky's Net"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Samoan gospel-choir stadium anthem. Deep, layered a cappella-style choir
harmonies (the backbone of Samoan church and rugby-sideline singing) build
over fa'ataupati slap-dance body percussion and a log-drum (pate) pulse,
opening into a full-throated massed chant.

LYRICS (Samoan):

[Ulua'i Fa'aili]
(Pate log drum knocks a slow heartbeat, choir hums low)
Amerika Samoa... tatou o se atunuu... fa'afetai...

[Fuaiupu 1]
Tolusefulutasi i le leai, o lena togi na tiga mo tausaga,
Sa tu Nika Salapu iina ma folafola i totoa,
Le tagata lava lea, totigilima lava lea, motu lava lea, fa'atali valu tausaga,
Lua-tasi i Tonga, ma sa i ona lima le polo.

[A'o Le'i Fa'aili]
Sa taofia e le fanua meamata la'ititi o Tafuna le manava o le atunuu atoa,
Na aveina i tatou e le fa'a Samoa i tuiga uma.

[Fa'aili] — the hook: "talofa, tulai" means "hello, rise up"
Amerika Samoa! Talofa, tulai!
Motu e tasi, alaga tele e tasi,
Amerika Samoa! Talofa, tulai!
Sa matou fa'atali, sa matou talitonu, e le'i toe fa'afefe.

[Fuaiupu 2]
Ta'aloga fa'ataupati i tafatafa, pese fa'a-kolisi i tua o le sikoa,
Ta'i siva uma iinei ua suia i totonu o le agaga,
Fa'afetai i le faiaoga na va'ai i le mea e mafai ona avea ai i tatou,
Ua manuia — mai le mauga i le sami.

[Fa'aili]
Amerika Samoa! Talofa, tulai!
Motu e tasi, alaga tele e tasi,
Amerika Samoa! Talofa, tulai!

[Alalaupapa]
Pate ta, lima fa'ataupati, leo o a'e i luga,
Pago Pago atoa o pepese i le lagi,
Valu tausaga o faiaga na fausia ai le fiafia i lenei manumalo e tasi,
Na a'oa'o mai e le fa'a Samoa ia i tatou pe fa'apefea ona ulufale se nofoaga la'ititi.

[Fa'aili Mulimuli]
Amerika Samoa! Talofa, tulai!
Amerika Samoa, e le'i toe fa'afefe.

[Fa'ai'uga]
Talofa! Amerika Samoa! Manuia!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the real story of goalkeeper Nicky Salapu, who conceded 31 goals
in American Samoa's infamous 2001 loss to Australia and, still in goal a
decade later, kept a clean sheet as they beat Tonga 2-1 in 2011 for their
first-ever official win — a story later told in the documentary "Next
Goal Wins." The lyric leans on "fa'a Samoa" (the Samoan way) and Samoan
choral and fa'ataupati slap-dance traditions central to island identity.
Written in Samoan, one of American Samoa's two official languages. Have a
native Samoan speaker review grammar and idiom before recording.
