ALGERIA
Official languages: Arabic, Tamazight (Berber)

TITLE: "Ya Bladi" (O My Country)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Raï-fusion stadium anthem. Gasba flute and derbake hand-drum carrying the
raï groove (Oran's own musical export to the world), Kabyle mandole
strings woven in for the Tamazight verses, opening into a full-band arena
chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Gasba flute wail over a rolling derbake pattern)
Ya bladi... ya bladi...

[Verse 1]
Sahara dunes roll gold beyond where any road can reach,
The Mediterranean folds white foam on Oran's crowded beach,
A raï singer's voice still carries stories no border could contain,
Every desert star has watched this same team rise again.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the Aurès mountain villages to Algiers' white-walled shore,
One nation, Arab and Amazigh, carrying so much more.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Arabic + English)
Ya bladi! Ya bladi al-habiba!
(O my country! O beloved country!)
Yalla, ya bladi al-habiba!
(Come on, O beloved country!)

[Verse 2]
The desert fox runs quiet till the moment that he strikes,
Green and white against the sand under the stadium lights,
Doesn't matter what the decades of hardship left behind,
We built a pride as vast as any desert you could find.

[Chorus]
Ya bladi! Ya bladi al-habiba!
Yalla, ya bladi al-habiba!

[Bridge]
Two tongues, one country, singing the same refrain,
Sand and sea and mountain, joy after the pain —
Gasba flute and mandole, hand in hand,
This is the anthem of the desert fox's land.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Ya bladi! Ya bladi al-habiba!
Yalla, ya bladi al-habiba!
Ya bladi! Ya bladi!

[Outro — chant tag]
Bladi! Bladi! Al-Jazair!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on raï music (Algeria's globally famous musical export from Oran),
the Sahara's scale, and Algeria's dual Arabic/Tamazight heritage, woven
together rather than choosing one. "Les Fennecs" (the Desert Foxes) is the
national team's real nickname, referenced through the fox imagery. Have
native Arabic and Tamazight speakers review phrasing before recording.
