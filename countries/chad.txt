CHAD
Official languages: French, Arabic

TITLE: "Tchad, En Avant" (Chad, Forward)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Sahel-fusion stadium anthem. Kinde flute and calabash hand-drum patterns
from the Sahel belt carrying the melody, dual French and Arabic
call-and-response verses, opening into a full-band chorus with massed
crowd vocals under a Lake Chad sunset.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Calabash drum pulse rises under a kinde flute line)
Tchad... en avant...

[Verse 1]
Lake Chad shrinks and rises like the breathing of the land,
Fishermen and herders share the same wide stretch of sand,
Toubou riders cross the dunes the way their fathers always did,
Every child who's ever run this ground learned it as a kid.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the lakeside fishing nets to the Sahara's open door,
One nation, two tongues speaking, carrying more.

[Chorus] (viral hook, French + Arabic + English)
Tchad, en avant! Tchad, en avant!
(Chad, forward! Chad, forward!)
Yalla, Tchad, en avant!
(Come on, Chad, forward!)

[Verse 2]
The Sara wrestlers grip and pull the way the desert taught them how,
Every drumbeat in this nation answers to the exact same vow,
Doesn't matter how the maps once drew a border through our land,
We are one people underneath one flag, one open hand.

[Chorus]
Tchad, en avant! Tchad, en avant!
Yalla, Tchad, en avant!

[Bridge]
Lake and desert, north and south combined,
One nation carrying more than one same mind —
Flute and drumbeat, French and Arabic tongue,
This is the anthem that Chad has always sung.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Tchad, en avant! Tchad, en avant!
Yalla, Tchad, en avant!
En avant! En avant!

[Outro — chant tag]
Tchad! En avant!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Lake Chad (a genuine, defining geographic and economic feature
shared across borders) and Chad's real dual French/Arabic identity,
represented directly in the bilingual chorus, plus Sara wrestling, a real
traditional sport. Have native French and Arabic speakers each review
their half before recording.
