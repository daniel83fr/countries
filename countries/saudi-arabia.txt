SAUDI ARABIA
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Saudia" (O Saudi)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Ardah-driven stadium anthem. Ardah sword-dance drums and chant patterns
(the real Najdi celebration tradition — lines of men, swords raised, poetry
chanted over drums) form the backbone, oud melody threading through the
verses, opening into a full-band arena chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Ardah drum pattern builds, poetry-chant murmur underneath)
Ya Saudia... ya Saudia...

[Maqta' 1]
Suyuf marfu'a 'aliyan fi saff qadim ka-dhakirat al-sahra',
Shu'ara' yunshidun abyatan la yastati' jaysh an yuskitaha aw yadfinaha,
Suqur tuharriru fawqa al-kuthban 'inda al-fajr,
Wa mal'ab kura hawwala yaqin 'imlaq ila tin.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min saff al-tabl al-Najdi ila jumhur al-mal'ab al-hadir,
Umma wahida, qadima wa haditha, tughanni bi-sawt 'ali.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "Ya Saudia al-habiba" means "O beloved Saudi"
Ya Saudia! Ya Saudia al-habiba!
Yalla Saudia, ya Saudia al-habiba!
Ya Saudia! Ya Saudia al-habiba!

[Maqta' 2]
Qalu inna al-mustad'afin la yusqitun 'amaliqa buniyat bi-hadhihi al-tul,
Thumma ahad 'ashar qamisan akhdar athbatu khata' al-'alam kullihi bi-kurat wahida,
Tubul al-ardah allati ihtafalat bi-al-intisarat mundhu zaman ba'id,
Daqqat nafs al-iqa' 'indama ghadarna ka-man sajjalu al-hadaf.

[al-Jawqa]
Ya Saudia! Ya Saudia al-habiba!
Yalla Saudia, ya Saudia al-habiba!

[al-Jisr]
Al-suyuf la tasqutu fi al-ardah, tartafi'u ka-wahid,
Kullu daqqat tabl taqulu inna al-ihtifal bada' tawwan —
Sahra' wa saqr, qasida wa nasl,
Hadha huwa al-fakhr alladhi sana'athu al-qurun.

[al-Jawqa al-Akhira]
Ya Saudia! Ya Saudia al-habiba!
Yalla Saudia, ya Saudia al-habiba!
Ya Saudia! Ya Saudia!

[al-Khatima]
Saudia! Saudia! Ya habiba!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the ardah, Saudi Arabia's traditional sword-dance and poetry
performance genuinely used at national celebrations, and the country's
famous 2022 World Cup upset victory over a defending champion, a real,
still-celebrated underdog moment. Have a native Saudi Arabic speaker review
phrasing before recording.
