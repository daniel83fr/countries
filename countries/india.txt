INDIA
Official languages: Hindi, English

TITLE: "Jeetega Bhai, Jeetega" (We Will Win, Brother, We Will Win)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Bollywood-stadium fusion anthem. Tabla and dhol driving the rhythm, sitar
and shehnai carrying the melodic hook, harmonium swells building the
pre-chorus, exploding into a full orchestral-Bollywood chorus with massed
crowd vocals — the sound of a cricket stadium turned into a movie theme.

LYRICS (Hindi, romanized):

[Bhoomika]
(Tabla roll under a rising shehnai line)
Bharat... Bharat mata ki...

[Antara 1]
Himalaya ki barf se lekar Kanyakumari ke kinare tak,
Ek arab alag bhashayein ek hi score ke liye chillati hain,
Bheed mein Holi ke rang jaise pura aasman khul gaya ho,
Har train, har chhat ek hi run ko dekh rahi hai.

[Chorus Se Pehle]
Assam ke chai baghon se Thar ke sunehre ret tak,
Ek team, ek garjan, ek dhadkan pure desh mein.

[Chorus] — an actual popular Indian cricket-stadium chant meaning "we will win, brother, India will win"

Yeh ek aisa desh hai jisne kabhi kuch bhi kam daav par nahi lagaya.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Uses "Jeetega bhai jeetega, Hindustan jeetega," a genuine, already-viral
chant heard in Indian cricket stadiums, as the chorus hook rather than
inventing one from scratch, alongside Holi color imagery and the
Himalaya-to-Kanyakumari span that captures the country's scale and
diversity in one line. "Bharat Mata Ki Jai" is a real, widely used
patriotic chant used as the closing tag. Written in romanized Hindi; have
a native Hindi speaker convert to Devanagari script and review grammar
before recording.
