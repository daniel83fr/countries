INDIA
Official languages: Hindi, English

TITLE: "Jeetega Bhai, Jeetega" (We Will Win, Brother, We Will Win)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Bollywood-stadium fusion anthem. Tabla and dhol driving the rhythm, sitar
and shehnai carrying the melodic hook, harmonium swells building the
pre-chorus, exploding into a full orchestral-Bollywood chorus with massed
crowd vocals — the sound of a cricket stadium turned into a movie theme.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Tabla roll under a rising shehnai line)
Bharat... Bharat mata ki...

[Verse 1]
From the Himalayan snow line down to Kanyakumari's shore,
A billion different languages all shouting for one score,
Holi colors in the crowd like the whole sky came undone,
Every train, every rooftop watching one single run.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the tea gardens of Assam to the Thar's golden sand,
One team, one roar, one heartbeat across the whole land.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Hindi + English — an actual popular stadium chant)
Jeetega bhai jeetega, Hindustan jeetega!
(We will win, brother, we will win, India will win!)
Jeetega bhai jeetega, Hindustan jeetega!

[Verse 2]
A rickshaw driver's radio, a rooftop satellite dish,
Every single household making the exact same wish,
When the last ball's bowled and the whole country holds its breath,
This is a nation that has never once bet less than everything it's got left.

[Chorus]
Jeetega bhai jeetega, Hindustan jeetega!
Jeetega bhai jeetega, Hindustan jeetega!

[Bridge]
Saffron, white and green against the evening sky,
A billion hearts beating for the same reply —
We are the crowd that never sits back down,
We are the noise that wears the whole world's crown.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Jeetega bhai jeetega, Hindustan jeetega!
Jeetega bhai jeetega, Hindustan jeetega!
Jeetega! Jeetega!

[Outro — chant tag]
Bharat mata ki jai!
(Victory to Mother India!)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Uses "Jeetega bhai jeetega, Hindustan jeetega," a genuine, already-viral
chant heard in Indian cricket stadiums, as the chorus hook rather than
inventing one from scratch, alongside Holi color imagery and the
Himalaya-to-Kanyakumari span that captures the country's scale and
diversity in one line. "Bharat Mata Ki Jai" is a real, widely used
patriotic chant used as the closing tag.
