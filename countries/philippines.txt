PHILIPPINES
Official languages: Filipino, English

TITLE: "Laban, Pilipinas!" (Fight, Philippines!)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Kundiman-to-arena fusion anthem. Bamboo tinikling clap-rhythm and rondalla
string-ensemble melody opening the verses, kundiman-style romantic minor
key giving way to a full-band, brass-driven pop-rock chorus with massed
crowd vocals — jeepney horns and basketball-court echoes as texture.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Bamboo pole clap rhythm builds under a rondalla guitar line)
Pilipinas... laban...

[Verse 1]
Seven thousand islands but one basketball hoop on every street,
Jeepneys painted rainbow, rolling to the same drumbeat,
Bayanihan taught us how to carry a whole house as one,
Now we carry the whole nation's hope until the game is done.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the rice terrace carved by hand to the city's neon light,
One archipelago, one heartbeat, fighting through the night.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Filipino + English — a real, widely used sports chant)
Laban, Pilipinas! Laban, Pilipinas!
(Fight, Philippines! Fight, Philippines!)
Kaya natin 'to — laban, Pilipinas!
(We can do this — fight, Philippines!)

[Verse 2]
A fisherman in Palawan and a driver in Manila's jam,
Watching the same jersey on the same small television scan,
Doesn't matter how many islands stand between our hearts,
When that ball goes up, we're never truly far apart.

[Chorus]
Laban, Pilipinas! Laban, Pilipinas!
Kaya natin 'to — laban, Pilipinas!

[Bridge]
Bayanihan spirit, carrying the load,
Seven thousand islands on the same road —
Bamboo bends but never breaks in the storm,
This is the shape our fighting pride can form.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Laban, Pilipinas! Laban, Pilipinas!
Kaya natin 'to — laban, Pilipinas!
Laban! Laban!

[Outro — chant tag]
Laban, Pilipinas! Laban!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on "Laban, Pilipinas," a real, widely used Filipino sports chant, and
bayanihan — the genuine Filipino tradition of neighbors literally carrying
a house together — used here as the story's central metaphor for national
unity. Have a native Filipino speaker review phrasing before recording.
