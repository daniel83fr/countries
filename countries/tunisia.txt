TUNISIA
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Nusur Qartaj" (Eagles of Carthage)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Malouf-driven Mediterranean anthem. Oud and nay flute carry the malouf
melody (Tunisia's classical Andalusian-rooted music) over a darbuka hand-
drum rhythm, jasmine-market ambience, opening into a full-band chorus with
massed crowd vocals among the ruins of Carthage.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Naghamat al-nay tartafi' fawqa daqqat darbuka bati'a)
Ya Tunis... ya Tunis...

[Maqta' 1]
Qartaj al-qadima tanamu taht al-bahr al-mutawassit al-azraq,
Al-yasmin yatasallaqu kulla haitin fi al-madina al-'atiqa,
Suq al-madina yahmilu 'utur al-tarikh fi kulli zuqaq,
Wa kullu jeelin yahmilu al-raya bi-hubbin la yufaraq.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min atlal Qartaj ila al-mal'ab al-mudi' al-yawm,
Ummatun wahidatun, sabra, la tarkanu wa la tanam.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "Nusur Qartaj" means "Eagles of Carthage"
Nusur Qartaj! Nusur Qartaj!
Ya Tunis al-habiba, nusur Qartaj!
Nusur Qartaj! Nusur Qartaj!

[Maqta' 2]
Al-nusur tuharibu fi al-mal'ab bi-qalbin la yakhaf,
Kullu darbuka taduqqu tahmilu qissatan lam tunsa wa lam tuhaf,
La yuhimmu kam min al-sinin marrat bi-sumut wa sabr,
Nahnu ba'du ahfad man banaw Qartaj qabl al-dahr.

[al-Jawqa]
Nusur Qartaj! Nusur Qartaj!
Ya Tunis al-habiba, nusur Qartaj!

[al-Jisr]
Yasmin wa bahr, kilahuma yughanni,
Qartaj tabqa shahida 'ala kulli zaman,
Nay wa darbuka, iqa' la yansa,
Hadhihi ughniyatu ummatin katabat tarikhan jadidan.

[al-Jawqa al-Akhira]
Nusur Qartaj! Nusur Qartaj!
Ya Tunis al-habiba, nusur Qartaj!
Nusur Qartaj!

[al-Khatima]
Tunis! Nusur Qartaj!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Carthage (Tunisia's ancient namesake civilization), jasmine (a
real national symbol worn and sold throughout Tunis), and malouf classical
music. "Nusur Qartaj" (Eagles of Carthage) is the national football team's
real nickname. Have a native Tunisian Arabic speaker review phrasing
before recording.
