DOMINICA
Official language: English

TITLE: "Nature Isle Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Bouyon-driven stadium anthem. Bouyon (Dominica's own high-energy music
genre) drives the whole track, tambou lélé drum and accordion from
Kwéyòl folk tradition woven in, rainforest waterfall ambience, opening
into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Tambou lélé drum pattern builds under an accordion line)
Dominica... rising...

[Verse 1]
Three hundred sixty-five rivers cut through mountains green and steep,
The Boiling Lake still bubbles secrets that the earth won't keep,
Kalinago hands still weave the baskets that their ancestors wove,
And every match-day here still moves to rhythms that they chose.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the boiling lake at midday to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One nature isle, untamed, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Nature Isle rising! Nature Isle rising!
Three sixty-five rivers under open skies,
Nature Isle rising! Nature Isle rising!
Dominica, Dominica, watch our island rise.

[Verse 2]
The last Kalinago territory still calls this island home,
Every mountain, every river taught us we were never alone,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations fill the map's whole frame,
The wildest island in the Caribbean still carries its own name.

[Chorus]
Nature Isle rising! Nature Isle rising!
Three sixty-five rivers under open skies,
Nature Isle rising! Nature Isle rising!
Dominica, Dominica, watch our island rise.

[Bridge]
Rainforest canopy and river running wild,
An untamed island, nature's own proud child —
Bouyon rhythm and a tambou lélé beat,
This is the anthem of the greenest street.

[Final Chorus]
Nature Isle rising! Nature Isle rising!
Dominica, Dominica, watch our island rise.

[Outro]
Dominica! Nature Isle! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Dominica's real nickname, "the Nature Isle," its genuine claim of
365 rivers, and its status as home to the last remaining Kalinago (Carib)
territory in the Caribbean. English is Dominica's official language, so
the full lyric is written directly in it.
