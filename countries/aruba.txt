ARUBA
Official languages: Dutch, Papiamento

TITLE: "Un Isla Felis" (One Happy Island)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Dande-driven island anthem. Dande music (Aruba's own New Year folk
tradition, sung door to door) carries the melody over a wiri scraper and
cuatro guitar rhythm, divi-divi tree-wind texture, opening into a full-band
chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Papiamento):

[Introdukshon]
(Cuatro ta pica un patronchi riba un wiri lento)
Aruba... un isla felis...

[Kuplet 1]
E palu di divi-divi ta bùig ku e bientu tur dia,
Playanan blanku ta briya manera oro den dia,
Kada tambú ta konta un storia di antes tempu,
Kada wega ku nos hunga ta trese e mes ritmu.

[Promé ku Refrein]
Di e palu ku ta bùig pa e bientu te na e stadion yen di bochincha,
Un isla so, felis, ku no tin nunka un fin.

[Refrein] — the hook: "Un isla felis" means "one happy island"
Aruba, un isla felis! Aruba, un isla felis!
Nos tur huntu — un isla felis!
Aruba, un isla felis! Aruba, un isla felis!

[Kuplet 2]
E dande ta kanta di kas pa kas kada aña nobo,
Nos isla chikí ta hasi mas ku e mapa ta mustra,
No ta importa kuantu aña difísil a pasa,
E felisidat di Aruba nunka lo no kaba.

[Refrein]
Aruba, un isla felis! Aruba, un isla felis!
Nos tur huntu — un isla felis!

[Puente]
Divi-divi ta bùig pero e no ta kibra,
E ritmu di dande ta biba den nos alma,
Wiri i cuatro, un solo bos,
Esaki ta e himno di un isla ku ta glorioso.

[Refrein Final]
Aruba, un isla felis! Aruba, un isla felis!
Nos tur huntu — un isla felis!

[Final]
Aruba! Un isla felis!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on "One Happy Island," Aruba's real, widely used tourism slogan
that islanders have genuinely embraced as identity, the divi-divi trees
bent permanently by trade winds, and dande, the island's traditional New
Year door-to-door singing custom. Written in Papiamento, the language
Arubans actually speak day to day. Have a native Papiamento speaker
review phrasing before recording.
