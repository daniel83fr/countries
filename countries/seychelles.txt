SEYCHELLES
Official languages: Seychellois Creole, English, French

TITLE: "Sesel, Annou" (Seychelles, Let's Go)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Moutya-driven island anthem. Moutya hand-drum rhythm (a real UNESCO-
recognized Seychellois tradition) carries the pulse, coco de mer palm-frond
percussion texture, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd
vocals over granite-boulder beaches.

LYRICS (Seychellois Creole):

[Antre]
(Tanbour moutya i konmanse dousman anba en van lanmer)
Sesel... annou...

[Verse 1]
Lanmer touristik otour bann ros granit i briye,
Koko-de-mer i pous isi, napa lot kot i grandi,
Moutya i sante dan lannwit lo laplaz kot fe nwar,
Sak match nou zwe, i mem menm zistwar.

[Avan Refren]
Depi bann ros granit ziska teren ki eklere,
En sel pep, en sel lanmer, ansanm nou pe grandi.

[Refren] — the hook: "Sesel, annou" means "Seychelles, let's go"
Sesel, annou! Sesel, annou!
Ansanm nou pe zwe — Sesel, annou!
Sesel, annou! Sesel, annou!

[Verse 2]
Bann torti Aldabra i mars dousman me i mars lwen,
Nou osi nou mars dousman me nou mars byen,
Napa i konpar ki gran pei i krwar nou,
Nou en ti zil ki ena en gran leker anndan nou.

[Refren]
Sesel, annou! Sesel, annou!
Ansanm nou pe zwe — Sesel, annou!

[Pon]
Tanbour moutya i bat depi lontan,
En rythm ki fer nou ansanm koumadan,
Ros granit ek lanmer, en sel kado,
Sa mizik la i byen zoli, i byen bo.

[Refren Final]
Sesel, annou! Sesel, annou!
Ansanm nou pe zwe — Sesel, annou!

[Final]
Sesel! Annou! Annou!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on moutya, a real UNESCO-recognized Seychellois music and dance
tradition, the coco de mer (a giant seed found nowhere else on earth), and
the granite boulder beaches unique to the islands. Written in Seychellois
Creole, the language most islanders actually speak daily. Have a native
Seychellois Creole speaker review phrasing before recording.
