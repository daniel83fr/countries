YEMEN
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Yaman" (O Yemen)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Sana'ani oud-fusion anthem. Oud and qanbus lute (Yemen's own ancestor of
the oud) carrying the melody over a hand-drum rhythm rooted in Sana'ani
sung poetry, mudbrick-tower ambience in the verses, opening into a
full-band arena chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Qanbus lute figure over a slow hand-drum pulse)
Ya Yaman... ya Yaman...

[Maqta' 1]
Abraj al-tin tartafi'u min San'a' ka-madina munhuta min khubz,
Al-qahwa nabatat awwalan 'ala hadhihi al-tilal al-mudarraja qabla an tuqal al-kalima,
Al-lubban wa al-murr ja'ala hadha al-sahil jayzata al-'alam kullihi marratan,
Wa kullu jeel la yazalu yaqra'uha fi al-sama'.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min haql al-qahwa al-mudarraj fi al-jabal ila bab al-mina' al-maftuh,
Hadha sha'b hamala al-fakhr 'abra kathir akthar min qabl.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "Ya Yaman al-habib" means "O beloved Yemen"
Ya Yaman! Ya Yaman al-habib!
Yalla Yaman, ya Yaman al-habib!
Ya Yaman! Ya Yaman al-habib!

[Maqta' 2]
Qabla an ya'rifa al-'alam al-qahwa, kanat tilaluna ta'rifu al-ta'm bi-alfan,
Kullu madraja manhuta bi-al-yad, la hasad dhahaba hadran abadan,
'Indama yajtami'u al-shatat kulluhu hawla shasha wahida,
Nahnu ba'du sha'b al-sahil wa al-wadi al-'amiq.

[al-Jawqa]
Ya Yaman! Ya Yaman al-habib!
Yalla Yaman, ya Yaman al-habib!

[al-Jisr]
Burj tini waqifun 'aliyan didda al-sinin,
Madraj qahwa manhut 'abra al-amal, la al-khawf —
Watar al-'ud yujib 'ala nida' al-jabal,
Hadhihi ughniya lan tada'na naskutu.

[al-Jawqa al-Akhira]
Ya Yaman! Ya Yaman al-habib!
Yalla Yaman, ya Yaman al-habib!
Ya Yaman! Ya Yaman!

[al-Khatima]
Yaman! Yaman! Ya habib!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Sana'a's UNESCO-listed mudbrick tower architecture and Yemen's
status as the historic origin point of coffee cultivation (the port of
Mocha is Yemeni), plus the qanbus, a real Yemeni lute considered an
ancestor of the oud. Have a native Yemeni Arabic speaker review phrasing
before recording.
