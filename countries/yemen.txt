YEMEN
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Yaman" (O Yemen)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Sana'ani oud-fusion anthem. Oud and qanbus lute (Yemen's own ancestor of
the oud) carrying the melody over a hand-drum rhythm rooted in Sana'ani
sung poetry, mudbrick-tower ambience in the verses, opening into a
full-band arena chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Qanbus lute figure over a slow hand-drum pulse)
Ya Yaman... ya Yaman...

[Verse 1]
Mudbrick towers rise from Sana'a like a city carved from bread,
Coffee first grew on these terraced hills before the word was said,
Frankincense and myrrh once made this coast the whole world's prize,
And every generation still can read it in the skies.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the terraced mountain coffee field to the harbor's open door,
This is a people who have carried pride through so much more.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Arabic + English)
Ya Yaman! Ya Yaman al-habib!
(O Yemen! O beloved Yemen!)
Yalla Yaman, ya Yaman al-habib!
(Come on Yemen, O beloved Yemen!)

[Verse 2]
Before the world knew coffee, our hills already knew the taste,
Every terrace carved by hand, no harvest ever went to waste,
When the whole diaspora gathers around one single screen,
We are still the people of the coast and the ravine.

[Chorus]
Ya Yaman! Ya Yaman al-habib!
Yalla Yaman, ya Yaman al-habib!

[Bridge]
Mudbrick tower standing tall against the years,
Coffee terrace carved through hope, not fears —
Oud string answering the mountain's call,
This is the anthem that won't let us fall.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Ya Yaman! Ya Yaman al-habib!
Yalla Yaman, ya Yaman al-habib!
Ya Yaman! Ya Yaman!

[Outro — chant tag]
Yaman! Yaman! Ya habib!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Sana'a's UNESCO-listed mudbrick tower architecture and Yemen's
status as the historic origin point of coffee cultivation (the port of
Mocha is Yemeni), plus the qanbus, a real Yemeni lute considered an
ancestor of the oud. Have a native Yemeni Arabic speaker review phrasing
before recording.
