CANADA
Official languages: English, French

TITLE: "One Nation, Deux Voix" (One Nation, Two Voices)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Bilingual arena-rock anthem, echoing "O Canada" itself moving between
English and French. Powwow drum and fiddle open the verses honoring
Indigenous and francophone roots, building into a full-band hockey-arena
chorus with massed crowd vocals in both languages.

LYRICS (English and French):

[Intro]
(A powwow drum heartbeat rises under a rising fiddle line)
Canada... one nation...

[Verse 1 — English]
From the Rockies to the Atlantic, one long road unwound,
Maple leaves turn fire-red before the first snow's found,
A frozen pond taught every kid to skate before they'd walk,
And every rink from coast to coast still carries how we talk.

[Verse 2 — Français]
Du Saint-Laurent jusqu'au Pacifique, une seule voix qui chante,
La feuille d'érable rouge, une fierté vivante,
Chaque hiver nous a appris à tenir, à persévérer,
Et chaque match nous rappelle ce qu'on a toujours été.

[Pré-Refrain]
From the mountain to the maple, d'un océan à l'autre,
Deux langues, un seul coeur — nous sommes tous des nôtres.

[Chorus / Refrain] — the hook: "Deux voix, un coeur" means "two voices, one heart"
One nation, deux voix! One nation, deux voix!
D'un océan à l'autre — one nation, deux voix!
One nation, deux voix! One nation, deux voix!

[Bridge]
Powwow drum and fiddle string, side by side,
Two languages carrying the same true pride,
Maple leaf and northern lights as one,
This is the anthem under a frozen sun.

[Final Chorus]
One nation, deux voix! One nation, deux voix!
D'un océan à l'autre — one nation, deux voix!
One nation, deux voix!

[Outro]
Canada! One nation! Deux voix!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Deliberately bilingual, mirroring "O Canada" itself, which is genuinely
sung in both English and French. Built on hockey's central role in
Canadian identity, and the country's real bilingual, multicultural
character. Have native English and French speakers each review their
half before recording.
