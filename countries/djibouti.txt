DJIBOUTI
Official languages: French, Arabic (Somali and Afar widely spoken)

TITLE: "Djibouti, Yalla" (Djibouti, Come On)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Afar-Somali fusion stadium anthem. Oud and tanbura lute carrying the
melody over a camel-caravan-paced hand-drum rhythm, Somali balwo-style
verse phrasing, opening into a full-band arena chorus with massed crowd
vocals under a strait full of passing ships.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Oud drone over a slow, patient hand-drum pulse)
Djibouti... yalla, Djibouti...

[Verse 1]
Lake Assal sits below the world, whiter than any snow,
Camels cross the salt flats the way our people always go,
Ships from every ocean pass this narrow strait of blue,
And a small nation watches, knowing what it's built for too.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the lowest lake in Africa to the busy harbor door,
One small nation at the crossing, carrying more.

[Chorus] (viral hook, French/Arabic + English)
Djibouti, yalla! Djibouti, yalla!
(Djibouti, come on! Djibouti, come on!)
Ensemble, yalla — Djibouti!
(Together, come on — Djibouti!)

[Verse 2]
Afar and Somali voices singing from the same one shore,
A crossroads nation standing where the whole world's ships explore,
Doesn't matter that we're small against the map's wide frame,
Every ship that passes here still learns to know our name.

[Chorus]
Djibouti, yalla! Djibouti, yalla!
Ensemble, yalla — Djibouti!

[Bridge]
Salt flat white and harbor water blue,
A crossroads nation always seeing something new —
Camel step and ship's horn, side by side,
This is the anthem of a strait-side pride.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Djibouti, yalla! Djibouti, yalla!
Ensemble, yalla — Djibouti!
Yalla! Yalla!

[Outro — chant tag]
Djibouti! Yalla! Yalla!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Lake Assal (the lowest point in Africa and one of the saltiest
lakes on earth) and Djibouti's real identity as a crossroads at the
Bab-el-Mandeb strait, one of the world's busiest shipping lanes, plus its
Afar and Somali cultural heritage. Have native Somali, Afar, and Arabic
speakers review phrasing before recording.
