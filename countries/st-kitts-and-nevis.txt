ST KITTS AND NEVIS
Official language: English

TITLE: "Sugar Mas Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Sugar Mas carnival anthem. Steel pan and masquerade drum rhythms carry the
groove, sugar-cane field wind texture, building into a full-band soca
chorus with massed crowd vocals under Brimstone Hill Fortress.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Steel pan run over a rising masquerade drum pattern)
St Kitts... Nevis... rising...

[Verse 1]
Sugar cane once filled the fields where mountains meet the sea,
Brimstone Hill still watches over both our islands, standing free,
Sugar Mas parade lights up December's darkest night,
And every match-day gathering carries that same light.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the fortress on the hilltop to the stadium's roaring crowd,
Two small islands, one same heartbeat, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Sugar Mas rising! Sugar Mas rising!
Two small islands under the same wide skies,
Sugar Mas rising! Sugar Mas rising!
St Kitts, Nevis, watch our islands rise.

[Verse 2]
The smallest sovereign nation in the whole Americas wide,
Two islands linked forever, neither one denied,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the score,
We built a pride as sweet as any cane field or more.

[Chorus]
Sugar Mas rising! Sugar Mas rising!
Two small islands under the same wide skies,
Sugar Mas rising! Sugar Mas rising!
St Kitts, Nevis, watch our islands rise.

[Bridge]
Fortress stone and sugar cane, two islands strong,
One same carnival, one same song —
Steel pan ringing over the mountain and the sea,
This is the anthem of the smallest we can be.

[Final Chorus]
Sugar Mas rising! Sugar Mas rising!
St Kitts, Nevis, watch our islands rise.

[Outro]
St Kitts! Nevis! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Sugar Mas, the real national carnival held in December, Brimstone
Hill Fortress (a UNESCO World Heritage site), and the real fact that St
Kitts and Nevis is the smallest sovereign nation in the Americas by both
area and population. English is the official language, so the full lyric
is written directly in it.
