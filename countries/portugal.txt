PORTUGAL
Official language: Portuguese

TITLE: "Guitarra e Grito" (Guitar and Roar)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Fado-rooted stadium anthem, uptempo. The guitarra portuguesa (the
twelve-string, pear-shaped guitar that defines fado) opens the track with
its signature tremolo-picked melisma, then a full rhythm section — bass,
drum kit, viola de fado strumming — pushes the tempo from mournful fado
crawl to marching stadium pace, with a massed male-and-female crowd choir
carrying the chorus like a terrace chant.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on fado, Portugal's own UNESCO-listed music of longing (saudade),
and its defining instrument, the guitarra portuguesa — reimagined here at
stadium tempo instead of its usual mournful crawl, turning saudade into
collective pride. The lyric leans on real national touchstones: the
narrow streets of Lisbon's Alfama district (fado's historic home), the
Age of Discoveries seafaring identity, and the green-and-red of the
Portuguese flag, without leaning on generic beach clichés.
