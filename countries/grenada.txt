GRENADA
Official language: English

TITLE: "Spice Isle Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Big Drum-rooted stadium anthem. Big Drum (Carriacou's real African-
retention ceremonial drumming) carries the rhythm, nutmeg-market spice
ambience, opening into a full-band soca chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Big Drum pattern rises under a shac-shac rattle)
Grenada... rising...

[Verse 1]
Nutmeg grows in every valley, spice upon the morning air,
The flag itself still holds the seed that made this island rare,
Big Drum calls the ancestors the way it's always done,
And every match-day gathering still moves as one.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the nutmeg grove at sunrise to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One spice isle, deep-rooted, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Spice Isle rising! Spice Isle rising!
Nutmeg on the flag under open skies,
Spice Isle rising! Spice Isle rising!
Grenada, Grenada, watch our island rise.

[Verse 2]
The Big Drum dancer moves the way the ancestors once led,
Every generation carries what the elders said,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the score,
The smallest spice isle still delivers so much more.

[Chorus]
Spice Isle rising! Spice Isle rising!
Nutmeg on the flag under open skies,
Spice Isle rising! Spice Isle rising!
Grenada, Grenada, watch our island rise.

[Bridge]
Nutmeg spice and Big Drum beating strong,
An island proving where its roots belong —
Shac-shac rattle and a drummer's hand,
This is the anthem of the Spice Isle land.

[Final Chorus]
Spice Isle rising! Spice Isle rising!
Grenada, Grenada, watch our island rise.

[Outro]
Grenada! Spice Isle! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Grenada's real nickname, "the Spice Isle" (a major nutmeg
producer, with the nutmeg literally appearing on its flag), and Big Drum,
a genuine, deeply rooted African-heritage ceremonial music tradition
centered on Carriacou. English is Grenada's official language, so the
full lyric is written directly in it.
