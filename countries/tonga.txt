TONGA
Official languages: Tongan, English

TITLE: "Mate Ma'a Tonga"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Rooted in the Tongan lakalaka tradition — the massed standing choir of
call-and-response voices with synchronized arm movement, Tonga's own
UNESCO-recognized performance form — driven by heavy nafa log-drum
percussion and stamping feet, building into a full-throated stadium
chant modeled on the real sipi tau war dance.

LYRICS (Tongan):

[Kamata'anga]
(Nafa 'oku tā, va'e 'oku tu'utu'uni)
Tonga... mate ma'a Tonga...

[Fasi 1]
'I Teufaiva 'oku fonu 'a e fale va'inga 'i mu'a ke kamata,
Ko e 'Ikale Tahi 'oku hiki hake honau loto lahi,
Fanga si'i tama mei he ngaahi kolo 'o e fonua ni,
'Oku nau fua 'a e faka'apa'apa 'o e kau mātu'a.

[Ki mu'a 'i he Hiva Mahu'inga]
Mei he ngutu vaka 'o e ngaahi motu ki he fale va'inga lahi,
'Oku 'ikai ke ngata 'a e loto-to'a 'i he lelei pe kovi.

[Hiva Mahu'inga] — the real, famous chant: "Mate Ma'a Tonga" ("Die for Tonga")
Mate ma'a Tonga! Mate ma'a Tonga!
'Ikale Tahi, mate ma'a Tonga!
Mate ma'a Tonga! Mate ma'a Tonga!
Ko e fonua ni, mate ma'a Tonga!

[Fasi 2]
Sipi tau 'oku ta'ota'o 'i he lotoā ki mu'a ke fakahoko,
Va'e 'oku tu'utu'uni, nima 'oku hiki ki 'olunga,
Ko e lakalaka na'e a'usia 'e he kau mātu'a 'i mu'a,
'Oku kei ma'u pe he ngaahi tama 'oku va'inga he 'aho ni.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built entirely around "Mate Ma'a Tonga" ("Die for Tonga"), the real,
famous war cry chanted by 'Ikale Tahi (the Sea Eagles), Tonga's national
rugby team, before the sipi tau war dance they perform ahead of every
international match at Teufaiva Sport Stadium in Nuku'alofa. The lakalaka,
a formal standing choral dance with synchronized movement, is a genuine
UNESCO-listed Tongan tradition. A native Tongan speaker should review
grammar and idiom before recording.
