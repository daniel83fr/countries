CENTRAL AFRICAN REPUBLIC
Official languages: Sango, French

TITLE: "Zo Kwe Zo" (Every Person Is a Person)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Rainforest-percussion stadium anthem. Balafon and ngbaka log-drum patterns
carrying the rhythm, water-drum pulse borrowed from Aka forest music
traditions, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals under
a canopy-green banner.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Ngbaka log-drum pattern builds under a balafon run)
Centrafrique... zo kwe zo...

[Verse 1]
Rainforest canopy stretches farther than the eye can trace,
Ubangi River carrying more than water through this place,
Elephants move quiet through the Dzanga-Sangha green,
And every match-day gathering is the loudest forest scene.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the river's winding bend to the balafon's wooden keys,
One nation built on dignity that never learned to freeze.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Sango + English — the real national motto)
Zo kwe zo! Zo kwe zo!
(Every person is a person! Every person is a person!)
Centrafrique, zo kwe zo!
(Central Africa, every person is a person!)

[Verse 2]
The balafon key rings the way it's rung for centuries past,
Dignity is the one thing built to always last,
Doesn't matter what the outside world has said or shown,
We are the forest nation standing on our own.

[Chorus]
Zo kwe zo! Zo kwe zo!
Centrafrique, zo kwe zo!

[Bridge]
Every person matters, that's the oldest creed,
Written in our motto, planted like a seed —
River water, forest green, balafon key,
This is the anthem of a nation that's free.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Zo kwe zo! Zo kwe zo!
Centrafrique, zo kwe zo!
Zo kwe zo!

[Outro — chant tag]
Centrafrique! Zo kwe zo!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built directly on "Zo kwe zo" ("every person is a person"), the Central
African Republic's actual national motto in Sango — a real, deeply
meaningful phrase about human dignity — paired with the Ubangi River and
the country's genuine rainforest landscape. Have a native Sango speaker
review phrasing before recording.
