BURUNDI
Official languages: Kirundi, French

TITLE: "Uburundi, Twese Hamwe" (Burundi, All Together)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Royal-drum stadium anthem. Built around the ingoma — the sacred royal
drums of Burundi, played standing, danced around, carried on the head —
as the entire rhythmic core, with a single melodic flute line threading
between drum breaks, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd
vocals.

LYRICS (Kirundi, romanized):

[Intangamarara]
(A single ingoma drum strike, then the full royal drum ensemble builds)
Uburundi... twese hamwe...

[Umurongo wa 1]
Imisozi igihumbi irakiraka igicu ku nkengera y'ikiyaga Tanganyika,
Abatimbo baracina bakomeye ingoma bikoraniriye hejuru y'imitwe,
Buri gukubita kwaravuze imbere yuko abami bimikwa,
Kandi buri gikorwa c'umukino kigenda kuri urwo rutozo nyene.

[Imbere y'Umuvugo]
Kuva ku murongo w'ingoma zo ku musozi kugera ku kiyaga kirabagirana nijoro,
Igihugu kimwe, umuziki wa kera, cifuza kugenda.

[Umuvugo] — the hook: "twese hamwe" means "all together"
Uburundi! Twese hamwe!
Umutima umwe — Uburundi!
Uburundi! Twese hamwe!

[Umurongo wa 2]
Umutimbyi ntiyereka gusa umuziki, aracina uko avuza,
Umutwe uzamuye hejuru y'uburemere bw'imisi ibihumbi ya kera,
Igihe abari mu kibuga bose bakubitana amashi kuri uwo mukino usanzwe,
Iyi ni imwe ya kahise ingoma z'ababami zabwiye buri gihe.

[Umuvugo]
Uburundi! Twese hamwe!
Umutima umwe — Uburundi!

[Ikiraro]
Umusozi ku wundi ku wundi w'icyatsi,
Umuziki wa kera kurusha uwo umuntu yigeze abona —
Ingoma ku mutwe, ibirenge ku butaka,
Iki ni igihugu aho ingoma yimitswe.

[Umuvugo wa Nyuma]
Uburundi! Twese hamwe!
Umutima umwe — Uburundi!
Twese hamwe! Twese hamwe!

[Umuherezo]
Uburundi! Twese hamwe!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built entirely around the royal drummers of Burundi (Abatimbo), a
UNESCO-recognized, genuinely sacred and world-famous drumming and dance
tradition unique to Burundi, plus the country's real "land of a thousand
hills" landscape. Have a native Kirundi speaker review phrasing before
recording.
