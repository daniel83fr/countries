BURUNDI
Official languages: Kirundi, French

TITLE: "Uburundi, Twese Hamwe" (Burundi, All Together)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Royal-drum stadium anthem. Built around the ingoma — the sacred royal
drums of Burundi, played standing, danced around, carried on the head —
as the entire rhythmic core, with a single melodic flute line threading
between drum breaks, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd
vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(A single ingoma drum strike, then the full royal drum ensemble builds)
Uburundi... twese hamwe...

[Verse 1]
A thousand hills roll green around Lake Tanganyika's shore,
Royal drummers dance while carrying the weight of drums they bore,
Every strike has echoed since before the kings were crowned,
And every match-day crowd still moves to that exact same sound.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the hilltop drum line to the lakeside evening glow,
One nation, ancient rhythm, ready to go.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Kirundi + English)
Uburundi! Twese hamwe!
(Burundi! All together!)
Umutima umwe — Uburundi!
(One heart — Burundi!)

[Verse 2]
The drummer doesn't just play the beat, he dances as he plays,
Head held high beneath the weight of a thousand ancient days,
When the whole stand claps along to a rhythm three lives old,
This is the same story that the royal drums have always told.

[Chorus]
Uburundi! Twese hamwe!
Umutima umwe — Uburundi!

[Bridge]
Hill after hill after hill of green,
The oldest rhythm anyone has seen —
Drum on the head, feet on the ground,
This is the nation where the drum is crowned.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Uburundi! Twese hamwe!
Umutima umwe — Uburundi!
Twese hamwe! Twese hamwe!

[Outro — chant tag]
Uburundi! Twese hamwe!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built entirely around the royal drummers of Burundi (Abatimbo), a
UNESCO-recognized, genuinely sacred and world-famous drumming and dance
tradition unique to Burundi, plus the country's real "land of a thousand
hills" landscape. Have a native Kirundi speaker review phrasing before
recording.
