MAURITIUS
Official language: English (French and Mauritian Creole widely used)

TITLE: "Zil Nou, Sega Nou" (Our Island, Our Sega)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Sega-driven island anthem. Ravanne hand-drum, maravanne shaker, and
triangle carry the real sega rhythm — Mauritius's own national dance
music — building from a slow, swaying verse into a fast, celebratory
sega chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Mauritian Creole):

[Introdiksion]
(Ravann pe bate dousman, maravann pe swiv)
Moris... zil nou...

[Verset 1]
Lil ki ti perdi dodo me ki'nn gard so leker fran,
Lamer bler otour nou, korail anba, soley divan,
Sega dan nou disan, dipi zanfan ziska vye,
Sak fwa ki nou ganny, tou lil-la pe danse.

[Avan Refrin]
Depi montagn ver ziska laplaz dile,
Enn sel lil, tou kouler, ansam nou pe grandi.

[Refrin] — the hook: "Zil nou, sega nou" means "Our island, our sega"
Zil nou, sega nou! Zil nou, sega nou!
Ansam nou pe danse — zil nou, sega nou!
Zil nou, sega nou! Zil nou, sega nou!

[Verset 2]
Endien, Afriken, Sinwa, Ewropeen, ansam dan enn sel danse,
Sak koulti finn met so kontribision dan sa mizik la,
Napa okenn kestion ki bann gran pei krwar nou,
Nou'nn fer enn sel nasion ar tou seki ti vinn isi.

[Refrin]
Zil nou, sega nou! Zil nou, sega nou!

[Pon]

Enn rythm ki ras nou ansam koumadan,
Lamer ek montagn, korail ek disab,
Sa mizik la se zoli kado nou ena.

[Refrin Final]
Zil nou, sega nou! Zil nou, sega nou!
Ansam nou pe danse — zil nou, sega nou!

[Final]
Moris! Zil nou! Sega nou!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on sega, Mauritius's real national dance and music genre, and the
island's genuine multicultural blend of Indian, African, Chinese, and
European heritage. Written in Mauritian Creole (Kreol Morisien), the
language most Mauritians actually speak day to day, alongside English as
the constitutional working language. Have a native Kreol Morisien speaker
review phrasing before recording.
