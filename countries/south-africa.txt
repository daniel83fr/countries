SOUTH AFRICA
Official languages: isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, English, Sesotho, Setswana, and 6 others

TITLE: "Ubuntu, Bafana" (I Am Because We Are, Bafana)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Rainbow Nation stadium anthem, deliberately multilingual like South
Africa's own national anthem. Vuvuzela drone and marimba open the verses,
gumboot-dance stomping rhythm building underneath, opening into a
full-band chorus that moves between several of the country's official
languages, with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Sesotho, English):

CULTURAL NOTE:
Deliberately multilingual, echoing the structure of South Africa's own
national anthem (which itself moves through five languages), built around
"Ubuntu" — the real, deeply meaningful Nguni philosophy of shared humanity
("I am because we are") — and "Bafana Bafana," the beloved nickname of the
national football team. Have native speakers of each language section
review phrasing before recording.
