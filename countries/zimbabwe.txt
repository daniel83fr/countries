ZIMBABWE
Official languages: Shona, Ndebele, English (16 recognized languages)

TITLE: "Zimbabwe, Simuka" (Zimbabwe, Rise Up)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Mbira-driven stadium anthem. Mbira dzavadzimu (the sacred Shona thumb-
piano) carries the hypnotic melodic hook over a hosho gourd-shaker
rhythm, stone-ruin ambience evoking Great Zimbabwe, opening into a
full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Shona):

[Kutanga]
(Mbira inotanga kuridza, hosho ichitevera zvishoma nezvishoma)
Zimbabwe... simuka...

[Ndima 1]

Uye mutambo mumwe nomumwe unotakura chirevo icho chisingakanganwike.

[Pamberi peKorasi]

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Great Zimbabwe ruins (the medieval stone city that gave the
country its name) and mbira dzavadzimu, the sacred Shona thumb-piano
tradition, plus Victoria Falls. "Simuka" (rise up) is a real, commonly
used Shona word. Have a native Shona speaker review grammar and idiom
before recording.
