LAOS
Official language: Lao

TITLE: "Lao Su Su" (Laos, Fight On)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Mekong boat-racing fusion anthem. Khaen bamboo mouth organ carrying the
melodic hook over a dragon-boat paddling drum pulse (the real rhythm used
in Laos's Boun Suang Heua river races), lam folk-singing phrasing in the
verses, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Khaen drone rises over a slow river-drum pulse)
Lao... su su, Lao...

[Verse 1]
The Mekong carries longboats painted gold and red and green,
A hundred paddles hitting water, fastest ever seen,
Temple bells in Luang Prabang still ring at break of day,
Every village on the river has a boat, has a say.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the Plain of Jars to the temple's golden spire,
One river, one nation, carrying the same fire.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Lao + English)
Lao, su su! Lao, su su!
(Laos, fight on! Laos, fight on!)
Huam kan, Lao su su!
(Together, Laos fights on!)

[Verse 2]
The drummer at the front sets the pace for every stroke,
No river current strong enough to break the bond we spoke,
Small nation, landlocked, but our river never stopped,
Every race we've rowed has proved the spirit we've still got.

[Chorus]
Lao, su su! Lao, su su!
Huam kan, Lao su su!

[Bridge]
The river doesn't care how small we look from land,
It only asks for hearts that row and understand —
Paddle by paddle, drum by drum, we rise,
This is the nation the Mekong never denies.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Lao, su su! Lao, su su!
Huam kan, Lao su su!
Su su! Su su!

[Outro — chant tag]
Lao! Lao! Su su!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Mekong River boat-racing festival (Boun Suang Heua), a
genuine, beloved Lao sporting and cultural tradition, plus the khaen
(Laos's iconic bamboo mouth organ) and Luang Prabang's temple heritage.
"Su su" is a real, commonly used Lao expression of encouragement ("fight
on/go for it"). Have a native Lao speaker review phrasing before recording.
