LAOS
Official language: Lao

TITLE: "Lao Su Su" (Laos, Fight On)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Mekong boat-racing fusion anthem. Khaen bamboo mouth organ carrying the
melodic hook over a dragon-boat paddling drum pulse (the real rhythm used
in Laos's Boun Suang Heua river races), lam folk-singing phrasing in the
verses, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Lao, romanized):

[Kham Nam]
(Khaen drone rises over a slow river-drum pulse)
Lao... su su, Lao...

[Ton Thi 1]
Mae Nam Khong pha hue nyao thong dèng lae khiao,
Fai pai hoy tao nam vai thi sut thao khoei hen,
Kong hò vat nai Luang Prabang nyang dang yam sao,
Thuk muban thi nam mi hò, mi siang khong tang.

[Kon Kò Hong]
Tae Thong Hai Hin thueng chò vat kham,
Nam diao, sat diao, hè fai diao kan.

[Hong] — the hook: "su su" means "fight on"; "huam kan" means "together"
Lao, su su! Lao, su su!
Huam kan, Lao su su!
Lao, su su! Lao, su su!

[Ton Thi 2]
Phu ti klong nai hua hò tang changva hai thuk khob nam,
Bo mi kasae nam dai kheng heng pho ti cha hak sanya hao,
Muang noi, bo mi thale, tae mae nam hao bo koei yut,
Thuk kan khaeng hò dai phisut chit chai thi hao yang mi.

[Hong]
Lao, su su! Lao, su su!
Huam kan, Lao su su!

[Saphan]
Mae nam bo sonchai vaa hao noi khanad dai chak fang,
Man tong kan tae hua chai thi hò lae khao chai kan —
Fai lae fai, klong lae klong, hao lük khuen,
Ni khu sat thi Mae Khong bo koei patiseth.

[Hong Sut Thai]
Lao, su su! Lao, su su!

[Chop]

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Mekong River boat-racing festival (Boun Suang Heua), a
genuine, beloved Lao sporting and cultural tradition, plus the khaen
(Laos's iconic bamboo mouth organ) and Luang Prabang's temple heritage.
"Su su" is a real, commonly used Lao expression of encouragement ("fight
on/go for it"). Written in romanized Lao as a best-effort draft; have a
native Lao speaker convert to Lao script and review phrasing before
recording.
