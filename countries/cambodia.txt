CAMBODIA
Official language: Khmer

TITLE: "Domrei Cheat Khmer" (Roar of the Khmer Nation)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Pinpeat-fusion stadium anthem. Roneat xylophone and korng gong-circle
melody lines over a sampho drum heartbeat, kong thom bass gongs anchoring
the low end, building into a full-band chorus with hand-claps and massed
crowd vocals — classical Angkor court music turned into terrace energy.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Draws on the Angkor temples depicted on Cambodia's flag, the Apsara dance
tradition, the pinpeat classical ensemble, and Bokator/Pradal Serey (Khmer
martial arts) as a genuine source of national athletic pride, alongside the
Mekong and Tonle Sap that shape daily life. "Chey yo" is a real, widely used
Khmer victory chant. Written in romanized Khmer as a best-effort draft;
have a native Khmer speaker/poet convert to Khmer script and review verse
grammar and meter before recording.
