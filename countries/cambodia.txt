CAMBODIA
Official language: Khmer

TITLE: "Domrei Cheat Khmer" (Roar of the Khmer Nation)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Pinpeat-fusion stadium anthem. Roneat xylophone and korng gong-circle
melody lines over a sampho drum heartbeat, kong thom bass gongs anchoring
the low end, building into a full-band chorus with hand-claps and massed
crowd vocals — classical Angkor court music turned into terrace energy.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Roneat xylophone runs over a slow sampho drum roll)
Kampuchea... srolanh Kampuchea...

[Verse 1]
Stone towers older than the empires that came and went,
Apsara hands still telling stories no war could ever bend,
The Mekong carries silt and songs down to the Tonle Sap,
And every ring of every gong still finds its way back.

[Pre-Chorus]
From temple stone to river mist to the fighter's stance,
This nation moves like Bokator — a warrior's dance.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Khmer + English)
Kampuchea! Cheat Khmer!
(Cambodia! Khmer nation!)
Yeung teuk knong! Cheat Khmer!
(We stand together! Khmer nation!)

[Verse 2]
Five towers on our flag still catching the morning sun,
A thousand years of carving taught us we're never done,
When the ball goes forward and the whole stand starts to roar,
We are the same people who built temples out of war.

[Chorus]
Kampuchea! Cheat Khmer!
Yeung teuk knong! Cheat Khmer!

[Bridge]
We are stone that outlasted the empire's fall,
We are Apsara hands that remember it all —
The gong still rings the way it always rang,
Cambodia rises with the song we sang.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Kampuchea! Cheat Khmer!
Yeung teuk knong! Cheat Khmer!
Kampuchea! Kampuchea!

[Outro — chant tag]
Chey yo! Chey yo! Kampuchea!
(Victory! Victory! Cambodia!)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Draws on the Angkor temples depicted on Cambodia's flag, the Apsara dance
tradition, the pinpeat classical ensemble, and Bokator/Pradal Serey (Khmer
martial arts) as a genuine source of national athletic pride, alongside the
Mekong and Tonle Sap that shape daily life. "Chey yo" is a real, widely used
Khmer victory chant. Have a native Khmer speaker/poet review verse grammar
and meter before recording.
