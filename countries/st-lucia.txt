ST LUCIA
Official language: English

TITLE: "Twin Pitons Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Kwéyòl-fusion island anthem. Chak-chak shaker and cuatro guitar carry a
Gros Islet street-party rhythm, volcanic ambience evoking the Pitons,
opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Chak-chak shaker rhythm builds under a cuatro guitar line)
St Lucia... rising...

[Verse 1]
Twin peaks rise from the ocean like they're reaching for the sky,
Sulphur springs still bubble where the ancient fire runs dry,
More Nobel prizes per person than the whole world's ever seen,
And every match-day here still wears that quiet, proud sheen.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the Pitons at sunset to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One small island, twin-peaked, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Twin Pitons rising! Twin Pitons rising!
Two peaks reaching higher than the open skies,
Twin Pitons rising! Twin Pitons rising!
St Lucia, St Lucia, watch our island rise.

[Verse 2]
Gros Islet lights up Friday with a street that never sleeps,
Every islander still carries the rhythm that it keeps,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the score,
Two volcanic peaks still prove we carry so much more.

[Chorus]
Twin Pitons rising! Twin Pitons rising!
Two peaks reaching higher than the open skies,
Twin Pitons rising! Twin Pitons rising!
St Lucia, St Lucia, watch our island rise.

[Bridge]
Twin peaks standing tall against the tide,
An island small in size but giant in its pride —
Chak-chak shaker and a cuatro string,
This is the anthem that the whole coast sings.

[Final Chorus]
Twin Pitons rising! Twin Pitons rising!
St Lucia, St Lucia, watch our island rise.

[Outro]
St Lucia! Twin Pitons! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Pitons, St Lucia's iconic UNESCO-listed twin volcanic peaks,
and the real, remarkable fact that St Lucia has produced more Nobel
laureates per capita than any other country in the world. English is St
Lucia's official language, so the full lyric is written directly in it.
