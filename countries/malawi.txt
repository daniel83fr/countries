MALAWI
Official language: English

TITLE: "Warm Heart Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Gule wamkulu-rooted stadium anthem. Chitelela hand-drum rhythms and
masked-dance percussion (echoing the UNESCO-listed gule wamkulu tradition)
carry the pulse, malipenga horn-call melody in the verses, opening into a
full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals over Lake Malawi's shoreline.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Chitelela drum pattern rises under a distant malipenga horn call)
Malawi... warm heart...

[Verse 1]
The lake stretches so far it wears the name Lake of Stars at night,
A thousand fishing boats reflect the same soft evening light,
Every village still remembers how the masked dance came to be,
And every match-day gathering moves to that same century.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the lakeshore's gentle water to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One nation, warm and steady, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Warm Heart of Africa, rising! Warm Heart of Africa, rising!
Small on the map but our welcome runs wide,
Warm Heart of Africa, rising! Warm Heart of Africa, rising!
Malawi, Malawi, we carry you with pride.

[Verse 2]
Chitenje cloth wrapped tight around a mother's steady stride,
Every color that she's woven carries a nation's pride,
Doesn't matter what the bigger neighbors say we lack,
The warmest heart on the whole continent never has to look back.

[Chorus]
Warm Heart of Africa, rising! Warm Heart of Africa, rising!
Small on the map but our welcome runs wide,
Warm Heart of Africa, rising! Warm Heart of Africa, rising!
Malawi, Malawi, we carry you with pride.

[Bridge]
Lake of Stars reflecting a whole nation's glow,
A warmth that every visitor learns to know —
Masked dancer, drumbeat, lakeshore light,
This is the anthem for Malawi's night.

[Final Chorus]
Warm Heart of Africa, rising! Warm Heart of Africa, rising!
Malawi, Malawi, we carry you with pride.

[Outro]
Malawi! Warm heart! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on "the Warm Heart of Africa," Malawi's real, widely used national
nickname, Lake Malawi (also called "the Lake of Stars"), and gule wamkulu,
the UNESCO-recognized masked dance tradition. English is Malawi's official
language, so the full lyric is written directly in it.
