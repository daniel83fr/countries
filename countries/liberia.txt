LIBERIA
Official language: English

TITLE: "Liberia, Rise"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Palm-wine highlife stadium anthem. Palm-wine guitar picking (Liberia's own
acoustic guitar folk tradition) opens the verses, building with talking
drum and horn stabs into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals —
Monrovia street-corner music scaled to a stadium.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Palm-wine guitar pattern builds under a soft talking drum)
Liberia... rise...

[Verse 1]
Founded free before the chains of half the world had broken loose,
Monrovia's harbor still remembers every ship, every truce,
A boy from Clara Town once dribbled dreams too big to hold,
Grew up to wear the captain's band, then the nation's presidential gold.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the palm-wine guitar's slow string to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One small nation, self-made, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Liberia, rise! Liberia, rise!
The love of liberty still burns behind your eyes,
Liberia, rise! Liberia, rise!
One people, one flag, under these same skies.

[Verse 2]
War tried to take the rhythm but the rhythm never died,
Every street musician kept the palm-wine guitar alive,
Doesn't matter what the headlines said about us years ago,
We are still the nation that has always found a way to grow.

[Chorus]
Liberia, rise! Liberia, rise!
The love of liberty still burns behind your eyes,
Liberia, rise! Liberia, rise!
One people, one flag, under these same skies.

[Bridge]
From a boy with a ball to the highest office in the land,
This is proof of what a small nation's hope can stand —
Guitar string still ringing through the healing years,
This is the anthem past a nation's hardest fears.

[Final Chorus]
Liberia, rise! Liberia, rise!
The love of liberty still burns behind your eyes,
Liberia, rise! Liberia, rise!

[Outro]
Liberia! Rise! Rise!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Liberia's founding story as a nation established by freed people
of African descent, palm-wine guitar music (a real, distinctly Liberian
folk tradition), and George Weah's genuine journey from football legend to
president — a story unique to Liberia. English is Liberia's official
language, so the full lyric is written directly in it.
