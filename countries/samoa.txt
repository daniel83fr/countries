SAMOA
Official languages: Samoan, English

TITLE: "Siva Tau i Apia"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
A siva-rooted stadium anthem built on layered Samoan gospel-choir harmony
— the same dense, church-trained four-part singing heard every Sunday in
village congregations — set over a heavy communal log-drum (pate) and
foot-stomp pattern lifted straight from the siva tau war-dance itself.
The chorus opens the harmony out into full massed-crowd voice, tempo
locked to a marching, chest-slapping cadence.

LYRICS (Samoan):

[Ulua'i Vaega]
(Pate e ta, tulivae e pa'ū i le eleele)
Manu Samoa... o oe lea...

[Fuaiupu 1]
I Apia Park e talie ai le pa'ū o vae tetele,
Teuila ua mumū i luga o le laufanua ma le tino,
O tama uma na tupu a'e i le siva tau,
Ua le'i galo lava le upu na fofoa i le lotoa.

[A'o Le'i Oo i le Pese Autu]
Mai le tulivae e pa'ū i le tama'i nu'u,
E o'o mai i le uma o le tafaoga i le malae tele.

[Pese Autu] — the crowd hook: "Manu Samoa e! Siva tau, siva tau!" ("Our Samoan warriors! War dance, war dance!")
Manu Samoa e! Manu Samoa e!
Siva tau, siva tau!
Manu Samoa e! Manu Samoa e!
Le atunu'u e tasi, e tasi!

[Fuaiupu 2]
E le'i fai ma tulaga maualuga a isi atunu'u tele,

Susu ane, susu ane, fa'apea le lotu i le aso Sa,
Ma le fetu o le lagi na leo mai i le po.

[Pese Autu]

[Fetaula'iga]

O le siva na fa'aleoina e o matua ma augatama,
O lenei lea le pese na fofoa i le eleele.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built around the siva tau, the pre-match war dance performed by Manu Samoa
(Samoa's national rugby team) at Apia Park before kickoff — a real,
specific ritual distinct from the more widely known Māori haka, rooted in
Samoan oratory and dance. The teuila (Samoa's national flower, a red
ginger torch lily) and the church-choir harmony tradition, central to
Samoan village life every Sunday, anchor the imagery. A native Samoan
speaker should review grammar and idiom before recording.
