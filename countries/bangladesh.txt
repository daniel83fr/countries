BANGLADESH
Official language: Bengali (Bangla)

TITLE: "Bangladesh Jitbe" (Bangladesh Will Win)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Bengali folk-pop stadium anthem. Dhol and dhak drums driving the rhythm,
ektara drone and bansuri flute in the verses for a bhatiali river-song
feel, building into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals — the
sound of a Sher-e-Bangla National Stadium crowd turned into a record.

LYRICS (Bengali, romanized):

[Bhumika]
(Dhak drum roll over river sounds and a flute line)
Bangladesh... aaj Bangladesh...

[Charan 1]
Sobuj dhaner khet brishtir age nou hoye jhuke pore,
Podda ar Meghna amader anondo o bethaa boye niye chole,
Lal surjo othe emon ekta pataka'r opore jaar jonno amra rokto diyechi,
Egaro jon lok koti manusher chokhe jol ana pare.

[Kôrus-er Age]
Sylhet-er cha pahar theke Bay-er khola dôrja porjonto,
Ei desher protiti hridoyer dhoukdhouk ek-i jinis chay.

[Kôrus] — the hook: "jitbe abar Bangladesh" means "Bangladesh will win again"
Bangladesh, Bangladesh, jitbe abar Bangladesh!
Bangladesh, Bangladesh, jitbe abar Bangladesh!
Bangladesh, Bangladesh, jitbe abar Bangladesh!

[Charan 2]
Tigers-ra math-e neme ele shara math agun hoye jai,
Rickshaw-chalok ar montri ek-i asha buke niye tha-kai,
Otit-er scoreline kono kichu jai ashe na amader kache,
Amra emon ek desh toiri korechi jeta ei theke onek kothin obostha theke uthe eshechi.

[Kôrus]
Bangladesh, Bangladesh, jitbe abar Bangladesh!
Bangladesh, Bangladesh, jitbe abar Bangladesh!

[Setu]
Amra-i delta, amra-i nodir icha,
Jhor-e baka hoyechi kintu kokhono thameni amader chola —
Sobuj mathgulor jonno ar lal surjer jonno,
Ei-i sei juddher golpo jeta amra jitechi.

[Sesh Kôrus]
Bangladesh, Bangladesh, jitbe abar Bangladesh!
Bangladesh, Bangladesh, jitbe abar Bangladesh!

[Sesh]
Joy Bangladesh! Joy Bangladesh!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on real, specific national symbols — the red-on-green flag (sun of
independence on a field of green), the great rivers Padma and Meghna, the
tea gardens of Sylhet, and the near-religious following of the national
cricket team ("the Tigers"). "Joy Bangladesh" is a genuine, widely used
patriotic phrase. Written in romanized Bangla; have a native Bangla
speaker/poet convert to Bengali script and polish the verse grammar and
meter before recording — Bangla lyric-writing rewards internal rhyme that
a non-native draft can't fully capture.
