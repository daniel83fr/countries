MONGOLIA
Official language: Mongolian

TITLE: "Mongol, Uragshaa!" (Mongolia, Forward!)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Steppe-fusion stadium anthem. Morin khuur (horsehead fiddle) carrying the
melodic line over a galloping horse-hoof drum rhythm, khoomei (throat
singing) low drone woven through the verses, opening into a full-band
arena chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Morin khuur drone over a slow galloping drum pattern, throat-singing hum)
Mongol... uragshaa...

[Verse 1]
The steppe runs wider than the eye was ever built to hold,
Wrestlers circle at Naadam the way it's done since days of old,
A horse and rider move as one across the open land,
Every summer festival still fills the whole grandstand.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the Gobi's burning sand to the Khangai mountain's snow,
One nation on horseback, ready to go.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Mongolian + English)
Mongol, uragshaa! Mongol, uragshaa!
(Mongolia, forward! Mongolia, forward!)
Neg zurkh, Mongol, uragshaa!
(One heart — Mongolia, forward!)

[Verse 2]
Three manly games test the strength that built an empire wide,
Wrestling, archery, and horses — we still ride with the same pride,
Genghis Khan's shadow taught us to hold ground we've claimed,
When the whistle blows, we play like nothing's ever tamed.

[Chorus]
Mongol, uragshaa! Mongol, uragshaa!
Neg zurkh, Mongol, uragshaa!

[Bridge]
The wind across the steppe still carries our old song,
The horse still knows the way, and so do we, all along —
Fiddle string and hoofbeat, side by side,
This is the anthem of a nation's open-sky pride.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Mongol, uragshaa! Mongol, uragshaa!
Neg zurkh, Mongol, uragshaa!
Uragshaa! Uragshaa!

[Outro — chant tag]
Mongol! Mongol! Uragshaa!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Naadam, Mongolia's real national festival of the "three manly
games" (wrestling, archery, horse racing), and the morin khuur, Mongolia's
national instrument. "Uragshaa" (forward) is a real, commonly used
Mongolian word for rallying people on. Have a native Mongolian speaker
review phrasing before recording.
