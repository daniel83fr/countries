MONGOLIA
Official language: Mongolian

TITLE: "Mongol, Uragshaa!" (Mongolia, Forward!)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Steppe-fusion stadium anthem. Morin khuur (horsehead fiddle) carrying the
melodic line over a galloping horse-hoof drum rhythm, khoomei (throat
singing) low drone woven through the verses, opening into a full-band
arena chorus with massed crowd vocals.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Naadam, Mongolia's real national festival of the "three manly
games" (wrestling, archery, horse racing), and the morin khuur, Mongolia's
national instrument. "Uragshaa" (forward) is a real, commonly used
Mongolian word for rallying people on. Written as a best-effort romanized
draft (with some stray Cyrillic characters to clean up); have a native
Mongolian speaker convert fully to Cyrillic script and rework grammar
before recording.
