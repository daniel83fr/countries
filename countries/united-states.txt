UNITED STATES
Official language: none at the federal level; English de facto

TITLE: "Section 8 Roar"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
American Outlaws supporters'-culture stadium anthem — a driving marching-
band-meets-indie-rock stomp built for a traveling-support tifo section,
snare-and-bass-drum cadence up front (the kind a supporters' band beats
out pre-kickoff), gang-vocal group chant on the chorus, built to be sung
standing on benches in an away end.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Snare drum cadence starts alone, then a horn stab, then the whole section)
U-S-A... Outlaws... on our feet...

[Verse 1]
We drove twelve hours in a bus with duct tape on the seats,
Red bandanas, road-worn boots, we don't know how to sit,
From Columbus to the Azteca, we've sung through worse than this,
Section 8 stands up first and section 8 don't quit.

[Pre-Chorus]
Every stadium's a hometown when the Outlaws fill the row,
One flag, one chant, one country, wherever this bus goes.

[Chorus] — the shoutable hook
U-S-A! U-S-A!
We don't stop, we don't sit, U-S-A!
U-S-A! U-S-A!
Section 8 is here to stay, U-S-A!

[Verse 2]
They said we'd never travel, said this country don't care,
But here we are in away ends, scarves up in the cold night air,
Every fourth of July flare, every chant we ever wrote,
Started in a parking lot before the kickoff, drum and smoke.

[Chorus]
U-S-A! U-S-A!
We don't stop, we don't sit, U-S-A!

[Bridge]
From the Rapids to the Riverboat, from the Wall to the front row,
Every city's got a chapter and every chapter's got a flow,
Ninety minutes on our feet, that's the only way we know,
Section 8 don't sit down till the whistle says we go.

[Final Chorus]
U-S-A! U-S-A!
Section 8 is here to stay, U-S-A!
U-S-A! U-S-A!

[Outro]
U-S-A! Still standing!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the real culture of the American Outlaws, the USMNT's national
supporters' organization founded in 2007 with chapters ("sections," a
nod to "Section 8" in stadium seating) in cities across the country, who
are known for traveling in force to away World Cup qualifiers and for
their pre-match drum-and-flag marches into the stadium. The lyric leans on
concrete supporters'-group details — the bus trips, the bandanas, the
traveling chapters — rather than generic flag-waving patriotism.
