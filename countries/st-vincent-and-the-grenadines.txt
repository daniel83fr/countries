ST VINCENT AND THE GRENADINES
Official language: English

TITLE: "Thirty-Two Islands, One Heart"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Soca-and-steel-pan stadium anthem. Steel pan carries the melody over a
soca groove, sailing-boat rigging and ocean-spray texture evoking the
Grenadines' scattered cays, opening into a full-band chorus with massed
crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Steel pan run over a rising soca groove)
St Vincent... the Grenadines...

[Verse 1]
Thirty-two islands and cays scattered like a string of pearls,
La Soufrière still watches over every bay it curls,
Bequia's whalers rowed by hand before the engines came,
And every match-day here still carries that same old flame.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the volcano on the mainland to the smallest scattered cay,
One nation, many islands, finding its own way.

[Chorus]
Thirty-two islands, one heart! Thirty-two islands, one heart!
Scattered like pearls but never far apart,
Thirty-two islands, one heart! Thirty-two islands, one heart!
Vincy, Vincy, we were one right from the start.

[Verse 2]
Every fisherman on every cay still knows the same one tide,
Doesn't matter which small island that you call your own home-side,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the score,
Thirty-two islands still can carry so much more.

[Chorus]
Thirty-two islands, one heart! Thirty-two islands, one heart!
Scattered like pearls but never far apart,
Thirty-two islands, one heart! Thirty-two islands, one heart!
Vincy, Vincy, we were one right from the start.

[Bridge]
Volcano watching over every scattered isle,
Every island in the chain still wears the same wide smile —
Steel pan ringing over ocean spray,
This is the anthem of the scattered bay.

[Final Chorus]
Thirty-two islands, one heart! Thirty-two islands, one heart!
Vincy, Vincy, we were one right from the start.

[Outro]
St Vincent! The Grenadines! One heart!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the real fact that St Vincent and the Grenadines comprises 32
islands and cays, La Soufrière volcano, and Bequia's genuine traditional
whaling heritage. "Vincy" is the real, widely used affectionate nickname
for the country and its people. English is the official language, so the
full lyric is written directly in it.
