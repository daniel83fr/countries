SUDAN
Official languages: Arabic, English

TITLE: "Ya Sudan" (O Sudan)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Nubian-fusion stadium anthem. Oud and tambour lyre carry the melody over a
daluka hand-drum rhythm, imagery of the Blue and White Nile meeting at
Khartoum, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals among
the Nubian pyramids.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Naghamat al-tambur tartafi' fawqa daqqat al-daluka al-bati'a)
Ya Sudan... ya Sudan...

[Maqta' 1]
Al-Nil al-azraq wa al-Nil al-abyad yaltaqiyan fi qalb bilaadna,
Ahramat Meroe ta'kudu anna al-tarikh huna, huna wahdana,
Sahra' Nubiya tamtaddu ab'ad min ay khareeta,
Wa kullu jeelin yahmilu hadhihi al-ard bi-mahaba wa mahaba thaniya.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min multaqa al-nahrayn ila al-mal'ab al-mudi',
Ummatun wahidatun, sabra, la tarkanu wa la tamil.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "Ya Sudan, ya habiba" means "O Sudan, O beloved"
Ya Sudan! Ya Sudan al-habiba!
Yalla Sudan, ya Sudan al-habiba!
Ya Sudan! Ya Sudan al-habiba!

[Maqta' 2]
Suqur al-jidyan tuharibu fi al-mal'ab bi-qalbin la yakhaf,
Kullu nahr, kullu haram, yahmilu qissatan lam tunsa wa lam tuhaf,
La yuhimmu kam min al-sinin marrat bi-sumut wa sabr,
Nahnu ba'du ahfad man banaw al-ahramat qabl al-dahr.

[al-Jawqa]
Ya Sudan! Ya Sudan al-habiba!
Yalla Sudan, ya Sudan al-habiba!

[al-Jisr]
Nahrayn yaltaqiyan wa yasiran ka-nahr wahid,

Tambur wa daluka, iqa' la yansa,
Hadhihi ughniyatu ummatin katabat tarikhan jadidan.

[al-Jawqa al-Akhira]
Ya Sudan! Ya Sudan al-habiba!

[al-Khatima]
Sudan! Sudan! Ya habiba!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the confluence of the Blue and White Nile at Khartoum, and the
Nubian pyramids of Meroë — Sudan genuinely has more pyramids than Egypt.
"Suqur al-Jidyan" (the Falcons of Jediane) is a real nickname of the
national football team. Have a native Sudanese Arabic speaker review
phrasing before recording.
