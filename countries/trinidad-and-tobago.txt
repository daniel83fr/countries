TRINIDAD AND TOBAGO
Official language: English

TITLE: "Steel and Soul"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Steelpan-driven soca stadium anthem. A full steelpan orchestra (pan
invented right here in Trinidad) carries the melodic hook over a soca
groove built for Carnival, call-and-response with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Steelpan orchestra runs over a rising soca groove)
Trinidad... Tobago... steel and soul...

[Verse 1]
Oil drum turned to melody, that's a story none can steal,
Every Carnival morning this whole island learns to feel,
Tobago's reef sits quiet while her sister dances loud,
Two islands, twin heartbeats, one and the same proud crowd.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the pan yard practicing at midnight till the dawn,
To the stadium roaring louder than the break of morn.

[Chorus]
Steel and soul! Steel and soul!
Pan was born right here, that's the story that we hold,
Steel and soul! Steel and soul!
Trinidad, Tobago, watch the whole island roll.

[Verse 2]
Soca move the body way before the whistle blow,
Every fete, every match, we carry the same flow,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the score,
Twin islands built a rhythm the whole world's dancing for.

[Chorus]
Steel and soul! Steel and soul!
Pan was born right here, that's the story that we hold,
Steel and soul! Steel and soul!
Trinidad, Tobago, watch the whole island roll.

[Bridge]
Steel pan ringing like a hundred silver bells,
Soca beat that every island body knows so well,
Twin islands, one horizon, one same sun,
This is the anthem of a rhythm never done.

[Final Chorus]
Steel and soul! Steel and soul!
Trinidad, Tobago, watch the whole island roll.

[Outro]
Trinidad! Tobago! Steel and soul!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on steelpan, the only acoustic instrument invented in the 20th
century, born in Trinidad from oil drums, and soca music, the genre that
powers Trinidad's world-famous Carnival. English is the official language,
so the full lyric is written directly in it.
