QATAR
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Yalla Qatar"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Khaleeji-modern fusion anthem. Oud and mizmar horn carrying the melody
over a fijiri pearl-diver hand-drum rhythm, stadium-scale synths and
falcon-cry sound texture, opening into a full-band arena chorus with
massed crowd vocals under a glittering Doha skyline.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Fijiri hand-drum pulse rises under a falcon's distant cry)
Yalla... yalla, Qatar...

[Maqta' 1]
Qabla an tartafi' al-abraj min al-sahra' mithl hulm,
Ghawwasu al-lu'lu' ghannu aghani al-fijiri taht daw' al-qamar al-shahib,
Al-an al-saqr yarkabu al-riyah fawqa dhahab al-ufuq,
Wa al-'alam kulluhu ja'a marratan li-yushahida qissatan tuhka.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min qa' al-lu'lu' al-'amiq ila qimmat al-burj al-lami',
Umma saghira, qalbun kabir — istadfna al-'alam ka-dayf.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "yalla Qatar" means "come on, Qatar"
Yalla Qatar! Yalla Qatar!
Habibi, yalla Qatar!
Yalla Qatar! Yalla Qatar!

[Maqta' 2]
Al-saqr 'ala al-mi'sam la yazalu yu'allimu al-sabr, la al-mutarada,
Bananaa masrahan waqafa 'alayhi al-'alam kulluhu fi makan wahid,
La yuhimmu kayfa ta'ummu al-umam al-akbar al-sinin,
Hawwalna al-sahra' ila shay' yahtifu lahu al-kawkab kulluhu.

[al-Jawqa]
Yalla Qatar! Yalla Qatar!
Habibi, yalla Qatar!

[al-Jisr]
La lu'lu'a 'amiqa jiddan, la burjan mabniyan 'aliyan jiddan,
Fatahna baytana li-nurahhib bihim jami'an —
Saqr wa kathib wa zujaj didda al-sama',
Hadhihi ughniyatu umma tarat 'aliyan.

[al-Jawqa al-Akhira]
Yalla Qatar! Yalla Qatar!
Habibi, yalla Qatar!
Yalla! Yalla! Qatar!

[al-Khatima]
Yalla, yalla, Qatar!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Anchors the pride in Qatar's pearl-diving heritage (the fijiri tradition),
falconry (a deep, real cultural practice, not decoration), and the country's
own recent story of hosting the 2022 FIFA World Cup — a genuinely
transformative moment in its national narrative. Have a native Qatari
Arabic speaker review phrasing before recording.
