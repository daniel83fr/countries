QATAR
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Yalla Qatar"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Khaleeji-modern fusion anthem. Oud and mizmar horn carrying the melody
over a fijiri pearl-diver hand-drum rhythm, stadium-scale synths and
falcon-cry sound texture, opening into a full-band arena chorus with
massed crowd vocals under a glittering Doha skyline.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Fijiri hand-drum pulse rises under a falcon's distant cry)
Yalla... yalla, Qatar...

[Verse 1]
Before the towers rose out of the desert like a dream,
Pearl divers sang their fijiri songs beneath the moon's pale gleam,
Now the falcon rides the wind above the skyline's gold,
And the whole world came here once to watch a story told.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the pearl bed deep below to the tower's shining crest,
Small nation, big heart — we hosted the world as our guest.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Arabic + English)
Yalla Qatar! Yalla Qatar!
(Come on, Qatar! Come on, Qatar!)
Habibi, yalla Qatar!
(My love, come on Qatar!)

[Verse 2]
The falcon on the wrist still teaches patience, not the chase,
We built a stage the whole world stood on in one single place,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the years,
We turned the desert into something the whole planet cheers.

[Chorus]
Yalla Qatar! Yalla Qatar!
Habibi, yalla Qatar!

[Bridge]
No pearl too deep, no tower built too tall,
We opened up our home to welcome them all —
Falcon and dune and glass against the sky,
This is the anthem of the nation that flew high.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Yalla Qatar! Yalla Qatar!
Habibi, yalla Qatar!
Yalla! Yalla! Qatar!

[Outro — chant tag]
Yalla, yalla, Qatar!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Anchors the pride in Qatar's pearl-diving heritage (the fijiri tradition),
falconry (a deep, real cultural practice, not decoration), and the country's
own recent story of hosting the 2022 FIFA World Cup — a genuinely
transformative moment in its national narrative. Have a native Qatari
Arabic speaker review phrasing before recording.
