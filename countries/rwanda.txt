RWANDA
Official languages: Kinyarwanda, French, English, Swahili

TITLE: "Rwanda, Komera" (Rwanda, Be Strong)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Intore-driven stadium anthem. Ingoma royal drums and the Intore warrior-
dance rhythm carry the entire pulse, flute melody weaving between drum
breaks, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals across
the land of a thousand hills.

LYRICS (Kinyarwanda):

[Intangiriro]
(Ingoma zitangira, umuziki w'Intore uzamuka buhoro)
Rwanda... komera...

[Umurongo wa 1]
Imisozi igihumbi ihagaze itegereje izuba rirasa,
Abasare b'Intore bakina nk'uko ba sekuruza bakinaga,
Ingoma ziravuga uko zaravugaga imyaka myinshi ishize,
Buri gihe umupira ugiye, dukomeza kwizera n'umutima wose.

[Mbere y'Umuvugo]
Kuva ku musozi uzamutse kugeza ku kibuga cyuzuye abantu,
Igihugu kimwe, gikomeye, kigenda mu bumwe budasenywa.

[Umuvugo] — the hook: "Rwanda, komera" means "Rwanda, be strong"
Rwanda, komera! Rwanda, komera!
Twese hamwe — Rwanda, komera!
Rwanda, komera! Rwanda, komera!

[Umurongo wa 2]
Intore zirasimbuka nk'uko imipira igenda ku kibuga,
Buri gikombe kiravuga inkuru y'igihugu cyubatswe bushya,
Ntibiba ngombwa ibyo imyaka ishize yaduhaye,
Turacyari igihugu cyize kwiyubaka no kwiga.

[Umuvugo]
Rwanda, komera! Rwanda, komera!

[Ikiraro]

Ubumwe n'amahoro ni byo dukomeza kwizera —
Ingoma n'Intore, hamwe biracyari kimwe,
Iri ni ihoro ry'igihugu cyubatse ku bumwe.

[Umuvugo wa Nyuma]

[Umuherezo]

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Intore warrior dance and ingoma royal drums, and Rwanda's
genuine, widely known "land of a thousand hills" landscape, framing the
country's real post-genocide unity and rebuilding story through the word
"komera" (be strong), commonly used in Rwanda as encouragement. Have a
native Kinyarwanda speaker review grammar and idiom before recording.
