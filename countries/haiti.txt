HAITI
Official languages: Haitian Creole, French

TITLE: "Ayiti, Leve Kanpe" (Haiti, Rise Up and Stand)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Rara-driven stadium anthem. Vaksin bamboo trumpets and rara drums — the
real processional carnival music of Haiti — carry the entire rhythm, iron
konpa guitar lines woven in, opening into a full-band chorus with massed
crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Haitian Creole):

[Entwodiksyon]
(Vaksin yo ap kònen sou yon bat tanbou rara k ap grandi)
Ayiti... leve kanpe...

[Vèsè 1]
Sitadèl la kanpe wo sou mòn depi tan lontan,
Premye nasyon nwa ki te libere tèt li san pran souf,
Rara a ap mache nan lari a chak fwa karanaval rive,
Epi chak match nou jwe pote menm memwa sa a.

[Anvan Refren]
Depi tèt mòn Sitadèl la rive nan estad k ap boule,
Yon sèl pèp, ki toujou kanpe, ki pa janm bese tèt li.

[Refren] — the hook: "leve kanpe" means "rise up and stand"
Ayiti, leve kanpe! Ayiti, leve kanpe!
Tout ansanm nou kanpe — Ayiti, leve kanpe!
Ayiti, leve kanpe! Ayiti, leve kanpe!

[Vèsè 2]
Vaksin yo ap chante istwa ke pesonn pa ka efase,
Chak tanbou rara pote yon batay ke nou te genyen deja,
Pa gen enpòtans konbyen ane difisil nou kanpe deja,
Nou se pèp ki te montre tout mond lan jan yo dwe leve.

[Refren]
Ayiti, leve kanpe! Ayiti, leve kanpe!
Tout ansanm nou kanpe — Ayiti, leve kanpe!

[Pon]
Vaksin ak tanbou, yon sèl vwa k ap chante,
Sitadèl la ap gade, li p ap janm bliye,
Mòn ak lanmè, yon sèl istwa,
Sa se chan yon pèp ki pa janm ka kraze.

[Refren Final]
Ayiti, leve kanpe! Ayiti, leve kanpe!
Tout ansanm nou kanpe — Ayiti, leve kanpe!
Leve kanpe! Leve kanpe!

[Final]
Ayiti! Leve kanpe!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Citadelle Laferrière (the mountaintop fortress built after
independence), rara processional music, and Haiti's real, historic status
as the first Black republic and the first nation born of a successful
slave revolution. Written in Haitian Creole, the language spoken by nearly
all Haitians daily. Have a native Haitian Creole speaker review phrasing
before recording.
