MALDIVES
Official language: Dhivehi

TITLE: "Dhivehi Raajje" (The Maldivian Nation)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Boduberu-driven island anthem. Boduberu (the Maldives' signature "big
drum" ensemble) building from a slow chant into a frenzied, accelerating
rhythm — its real traditional structure — layered with island guitar and
ocean-wave sound design, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd
vocals.

LYRICS (Dhivehi, romanized):

[Fashamun]
(Boduberu drums begin slow, chant rising over gentle wave sound)
Dhivehi Raajje... Dhivehi Raajje...

[Bandi 1]
Ekas rasgai dhihen jehey nilamehaka in'dhu bohvefa,
Ehen kaamah furagandhalaa dhaumeh mieh netheve, feenumaa aakah tha,
Mahujaanunge dhozun'dhuveh mi kudhi kiyaamuge ekulhun ithuru,
Aai dhandu ge san'dhaanugai adhives fahangaifa boalaa gothun.

[Kôrus ge Kurin]
Fadhuge dhashun fen dho'ah, iru ashi vareh boalaa velaanah,
Miee ehen rasgan'du emme kudi, magu emme ithuru dhemi.

[Kôrus] — the hook: "Dhivehi Raajje" is the real Dhivehi name for the Maldives; "ekee vamaa" means "let's go together"
Dhivehi Raajje! Dhivehi Raajje!
Ekee vamaa, Dhivehi Raajje!
Dhivehi Raajje! Dhivehi Raajje!

[Bandi 2]
Boduberu vaguthu fashaigen dhen ithuru vejje,
Avahah, boa kohlaa gothun — dhandu ge san'dhaanaa gulhigen,
Dhuniyeah fenna ekani thasveeru, beat eh nu bunevey,
Eii mahujaanunge kolha kaanthama gengaadhinu iru dhaathi.

[Kôrus]
Dhivehi Raajje! Dhivehi Raajje!
Ekee vamaa, Dhivehi Raajje!

[Buriji]

Miee Dhivehinnah engey rithum eh.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on boduberu, the Maldives' real signature drum tradition, which
genuinely starts slow and accelerates into a frenzy — a natural, authentic
structure for a song's own build. "Dhivehi Raajje" is the actual Dhivehi
name for the Maldives, making it an instantly recognizable, non-generic
hook. Written in romanized Dhivehi as a low-confidence best-effort draft
— Dhivehi has limited training data available to this model — so a
native Dhivehi speaker/lyricist should treat this as a sketch to rework
in Thaana script before recording.
