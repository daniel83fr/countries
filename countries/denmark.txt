DENMARK
Official language: Danish

TITLE: "Dynamit '92"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Danish rock-pop stadium anthem in the lineage of Danish "sømandssang" turned arena rock — think the big, unpretentious guitar-and-chant sound Danish "rolighed" (roligan) crowds have sung since the early 90s. Driving four-on-the-floor drums, a chugging clean-toned guitar riff, group-shouted backing vocals, built for terraces swaying arm in arm with beer in hand.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the real story of "Danish Dynamite" — Denmark's national football team, which was not even qualified for UEFA Euro 1992 until Yugoslavia was excluded due to war just eleven days before the tournament, went on to win the entire championship as massive underdogs. The lyric also honors the "roligan" identity, the real, deliberately peaceful counter-image Danish fans built in the 1980s-90s in contrast to hooliganism (from Danish "rolig," meaning calm) — famous for singing, dancing, and never causing trouble on the terraces.
