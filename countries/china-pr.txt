CHINA PR
Official language: Standard Chinese (Mandarin)

TITLE: "Zhongguo Jiayou" (Go, China)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Modern Chinese stadium anthem. Guzheng and erhu carrying the melodic hook
over taiko-scale drums and lion-dance cymbal crashes, dizi flute
ornamentation in the verses, opening into a full-band, synth-and-guitar
chorus built for a packed stadium chant.

Zhongguo... jiayou...

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built around "jiayou" (加油, literally "add oil"), the single most iconic and
widely used cheer in Chinese sporting culture worldwide — an instantly
recognizable, already-viral hook rather than an invented one — paired with
the Great Wall and lion-dance imagery that read as authentically Chinese to
both locals and outsiders. Written in pinyin; have a native Mandarin
speaker confirm characters, tone, and phrasing for singability before
recording.
