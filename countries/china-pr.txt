CHINA PR
Official language: Standard Chinese (Mandarin)

TITLE: "Zhongguo Jiayou" (Go, China)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Modern Chinese stadium anthem. Guzheng and erhu carrying the melodic hook
over taiko-scale drums and lion-dance cymbal crashes, dizi flute
ornamentation in the verses, opening into a full-band, synth-and-guitar
chorus built for a packed stadium chant.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Deep drum roll and cymbal crash, erhu cuts through)
Zhongguo... jiayou...

[Verse 1]
Ten thousand li of wall still climbing up the mountain spine,
A river of red lanterns marking out the finish line,
Grandmother's hands still fold the dumplings before the game,
Every generation passes down the same old flame.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the Great Wall's shadow to the stadium light,
One dragon, one heartbeat, rising through the night.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Mandarin + English)
Zhongguo, jiayou! Zhongguo, jiayou!
(China, add oil! Go, China!)
Yi qi jiayou! Zhongguo, jiayou!
(Add oil together! Go, China!)

[Verse 2]
The drum before the lion dance still sets the whole street's pace,
A billion different stories wearing one red-and-gold face,
When the whistle starts the match the whole nation leans in close,
This is the roar that traveled every dynasty and road.

[Chorus]
Zhongguo, jiayou! Zhongguo, jiayou!
Yi qi jiayou! Zhongguo, jiayou!

[Bridge]
We are the wall that never learned to fall,
We are the drum that answers every call —
Add the oil, let the fire climb,
This is our moment, this is our time.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Zhongguo, jiayou! Zhongguo, jiayou!
Yi qi jiayou! Zhongguo, jiayou!
Jiayou! Jiayou! Zhongguo!

[Outro — chant tag]
Jiayou! Jiayou! Zhongguo!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built around "jiayou" (加油, literally "add oil"), the single most iconic and
widely used cheer in Chinese sporting culture worldwide — an instantly
recognizable, already-viral hook rather than an invented one — paired with
the Great Wall and lion-dance imagery that read as authentically Chinese to
both locals and outsiders. Have a native Mandarin speaker confirm tone and
phrasing for singability before recording.
