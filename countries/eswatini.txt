ESWATINI
Official languages: English, siSwati

TITLE: "Eswatini, Phambili" (Eswatini, Forward)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Reed-dance stadium anthem. Sibhaca stomping-dance rhythm and cowhide
drum patterns carrying the pulse (the same energy used at Umhlanga Reed
Dance and Incwala ceremonies), horn calls in the verses, opening into a
full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Cowhide drum builds under a rising sibhaca stomp pattern)
Eswatini... phambili...

[Verse 1]
Reeds carried on a thousand shoulders down to the royal kraal,
Mountains fold around this kingdom standing straight and tall,
Sibhaca feet stamp harder than the drum can even keep,
And every match-day crowd still moves the way our grandparents leap.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the mountain kraal at sunrise to the stadium's roaring floor,
One small kingdom, proud tradition, carrying more.

[Chorus] (viral hook, siSwati + English)
Eswatini, phambili! Eswatini, phambili!
(Eswatini, forward! Eswatini, forward!)
Sonke sindawonye — Eswatini, phambili!
(We are all together — Eswatini, forward!)

[Verse 2]
The stomp before the whistle sets the beat for everyone,
A small kingdom proving pride was never measured big or done,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count us on their list,
We built a rhythm here that no one else could ever twist.

[Chorus]
Eswatini, phambili! Eswatini, phambili!
Sonke sindawonye — Eswatini, phambili!

[Bridge]
Reed and drum and mountain kraal as one,
A kingdom's rhythm underneath the sun —
Sibhaca stomp still shaking the ground,
This is the anthem of the smallest crowd that's loud.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Eswatini, phambili! Eswatini, phambili!
Sonke sindawonye — Eswatini, phambili!
Phambili! Phambili!

[Outro — chant tag]
Eswatini! Phambili!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on sibhaca, Eswatini's real stomping dance tradition, and the Reed
Dance and Incwala royal ceremonies that remain central to Swazi cultural
life today, in one of Africa's last remaining monarchies. Have a native
siSwati speaker review phrasing before recording.
