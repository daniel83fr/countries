MADAGASCAR
Official languages: Malagasy, French

TITLE: "Madagasikara, Mandroso" (Madagascar, Forward)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Valiha-driven island anthem. Valiha (Madagascar's national instrument, a
bamboo tube zither) carries the melodic hook over a hazolahy log-drum
rhythm, baobab-silhouette ambience in the verses, opening into a full-band
chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Malagasy):

[Fampidirana]
(Valiha miloko eo ambonin'ny feon'ny amponga mikadondadona)
Madagasikara... mandroso...

[Andininy 1]
Ny baobabo mijoro toy ny mpiambina ny tany taloha,
Ny gidro mihazakazaka amin'ny ala tsy mbola nofoanana,
Ny vary maitso mihena ho volomena eny amoron-tanety,
Ary ny valiha dia mitantara izay tsy voasoratra na oviana.

[Talohan'ny Fihirana]
Avy amin'ny havoana mavo ka hatramin'ny kianja mikorontana,
Firenena iray, mandroso, misy fireharehana.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the valiha (Madagascar's national instrument), the baobab trees
that define the island's silhouette, and Madagascar's unique biodiversity.
Have a native Malagasy speaker review grammar and idiom before recording.
