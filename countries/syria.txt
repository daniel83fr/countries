SYRIA
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Souriya" (O Syria)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Damascene oud-fusion anthem. Oud and qanun carrying the melodic line — the
deep Syrian classical music tradition centered in Damascus and Aleppo —
over a derbake hand-drum rhythm, jasmine-scented minor-key verses opening
into a full-band arena chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Solo oud figure over a slow qanun shimmer)
Ya Souriya... ya Souriya...

[Maqta' 1]
Dimashq tanaffasat qabla an tu'raf kalimat "madina" nafsuha,
Al-yasmin yatasallaqu kulla ha'it fi kulli sahn dar,
Aswaq Halab hamalat al-baharat wa al-ughniya min imbraturiya ila imbraturiya,
Wa kullu jeel hafiza 'ala nar watar al-'ud thabita.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min azqat al-suq al-qadim al-mughattat ila bab al-jabal al-maftuh,
Hadha sha'b hamala al-fakhr 'abra kathir min qabl.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "Ya Souriya al-habiba" means "O beloved Syria"
Ya Souriya! Ya Souriya al-habiba!
Yalla Souriya, ya Souriya al-habiba!
Ya Souriya! Ya Souriya al-habiba!

[Maqta' 2]
Al-yasmin la yazalu yatasallaqu judranan shahidat alaf 'am tamurr,
Kullu naftha fi kulli sahn la tazalu ta'kis nafs al-sama' al-qadima,
'Indama yushahidu al-shatat kulluhu min mi'at ard mukhtalifa,
Farq wahid, qamis wahid, la yazalu yasilu kulla yad mutafarriqa.

[al-Jawqa]
Ya Souriya! Ya Souriya al-habiba!
Yalla Souriya, ya Souriya al-habiba!

[al-Jisr]

Hajar Dimashq yatadhakkaru al-jami',
Hadhihi ughniyatu ma la yumkin an yulgha.

[al-Jawqa al-Akhira]

[al-Khatima]
Souriya! Souriya! Ya habiba!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Damascus, the "City of Jasmine" and one of the oldest continuously
inhabited cities in the world, plus the deep Syrian oud and qanun classical
music tradition and Aleppo's historic marketplace culture. Have a native
Syrian Arabic speaker review phrasing before recording.
