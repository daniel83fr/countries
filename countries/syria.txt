SYRIA
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Souriya" (O Syria)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Damascene oud-fusion anthem. Oud and qanun carrying the melodic line — the
deep Syrian classical music tradition centered in Damascus and Aleppo —
over a derbake hand-drum rhythm, jasmine-scented minor-key verses opening
into a full-band arena chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Solo oud figure over a slow qanun shimmer)
Ya Souriya... ya Souriya...

[Verse 1]
Damascus breathed before the word for city was even known,
Jasmine climbing every wall of every courtyard stone,
Aleppo's markets carried spice and song from empire to empire,
And every generation kept the oud string's steady fire.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the old souk's covered lanes to the mountain's open door,
This is a people who have carried pride through so much before.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Arabic + English)
Ya Souriya! Ya Souriya al-habiba!
(O Syria! O beloved Syria!)
Yalla Souriya, ya Souriya al-habiba!
(Come on Syria, O beloved Syria!)

[Verse 2]
The jasmine still climbs walls that saw a thousand years go by,
Every courtyard fountain still reflects the same old sky,
When the whole diaspora watches from a hundred different lands,
One team, one shirt, still reaches every scattered pair of hands.

[Chorus]
Ya Souriya! Ya Souriya al-habiba!
Yalla Souriya, ya Souriya al-habiba!

[Bridge]
Jasmine on the wall that never learned to fall,
Oud string still answering the ancient call —
Damascus stone remembers everyone,
This is the anthem of what can't be undone.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Ya Souriya! Ya Souriya al-habiba!
Yalla Souriya, ya Souriya al-habiba!
Ya Souriya! Ya Souriya!

[Outro — chant tag]
Souriya! Souriya! Ya habiba!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Damascus, the "City of Jasmine" and one of the oldest continuously
inhabited cities in the world, plus the deep Syrian oud and qanun classical
music tradition and Aleppo's historic marketplace culture. Have a native
Syrian Arabic speaker review phrasing before recording.
