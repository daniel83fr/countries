MONTSERRAT
Official language: English

TITLE: "Emerald Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Calypso-driven volcanic-island anthem. Steel pan and calypso guitar open
the verses, low volcanic rumble texture from the Soufrière Hills, building
into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Steel pan run over a low volcanic rumble)
Montserrat... rising...

[Verse 1]
The Emerald Isle they call us, Irish roots run deep and true,
The volcano took our capital but never took our view,
Half the island buried now beneath the ash and stone,
But the half that's left still sings the loudest, sings alone.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the ash beneath the mountain to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One small island, ash-born, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Emerald rising! Emerald rising!
Volcano took the city but it never took our eyes,
Emerald rising! Emerald rising!
Montserrat, Montserrat, watch the emerald rise.

[Verse 2]
Plymouth sleeps beneath the ash but we don't sleep at all,
Every islander still answers to the same old calypso call,
Doesn't matter that the world forgot us for a while,
We are still the smallest island with the biggest smile.

[Chorus]
Emerald rising! Emerald rising!
Volcano took the city but it never took our eyes,
Emerald rising! Emerald rising!
Montserrat, Montserrat, watch the emerald rise.

[Bridge]
Ash and emerald, buried town and open sky,
An island proving it still knows how to fly —
Steel pan ringing over volcanic ground,
This is the anthem of the emerald sound.

[Final Chorus]
Emerald rising! Emerald rising!
Montserrat, Montserrat, watch the emerald rise.

[Outro]
Montserrat! Emerald! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Montserrat's real nickname, "the Emerald Isle of the Caribbean"
(reflecting genuine Irish heritage on the island), and the real, dramatic
history of the Soufrière Hills volcano, whose eruptions buried the former
capital, Plymouth, and reshaped the island's story. English is
Montserrat's official language, so the full lyric is written directly in
it.
