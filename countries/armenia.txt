ARMENIA
Official language: Armenian

TITLE: "Արարատի Շունչը" (The Breath of Ararat)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Duduk-led stadium anthem. The duduk (the mournful, breath-driven Armenian
apricot-wood woodwind) opens alone, soulful and slow, before a driving
folk-dance rhythm in the style of an Armenian shourjpar circle dance
enters underneath, building to a massed-choir stadium chorus.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Centers on Mount Ararat, the snow-capped peak that is Armenia's most
sacred national symbol and appears on its coat of arms, even though the
mountain itself now lies across the border in Turkey — a real and
emotionally loaded fact referenced here through Khor Virap, the actual
monastery from which Ararat is famously viewed. The music is built around
the duduk, the double-reed apricot-wood woodwind central to Armenian
folk and liturgical music, paired with the driving rhythm of the shourjpar
circle dance to move from mournful solo breath into full communal stadium
sound.
