ZAMBIA
Official language: English

TITLE: "Chipolopolo, Rise"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Zambezi-driven stadium anthem. Kalimba (thumb piano) melody over a
kayamba shaker and hand-drum rhythm, waterfall-mist sound texture from
Victoria Falls, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Kalimba figure ripples over a rising hand-drum pattern)
Zambia... Chipolopolo...

[Verse 1]
Mosi-oa-Tunya thunders smoke that no one's ever tamed,
The Zambezi carries thunder underneath a river's name,
Copper ran beneath this ground before it ran through wire,
And every Chipolopolo strike still carries that same fire.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the smoke that thunders down to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One nation, copper bullets, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Chipolopolo, rise! Chipolopolo, rise!
The smoke that thunders taught us how to reach the skies,
Chipolopolo, rise! Chipolopolo, rise!
Zambia, Zambia, watch the copper bullets fly.

[Verse 2]
Nineteen ninety-three took brothers from the very sky above,
Two thousand twelve brought healing on the ground we learned to love,
Doesn't matter how the years of grief once weighed us down,
We turned our sorrow into gold and wore the champion's crown.

[Chorus]
Chipolopolo, rise! Chipolopolo, rise!
The smoke that thunders taught us how to reach the skies,
Chipolopolo, rise! Chipolopolo, rise!
Zambia, Zambia, watch the copper bullets fly.

[Bridge]
Falls that thunder, copper deep below,
A nation's grief that turned into a glow —
Kalimba string and river mist as one,
This is the anthem underneath the sun.

[Final Chorus]
Chipolopolo, rise! Chipolopolo, rise!
Zambia, Zambia, watch the copper bullets fly.

[Outro]
Zambia! Chipolopolo! Rise!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Victoria Falls ("Mosi-oa-Tunya," the Smoke That Thunders) and
copper, historically the backbone of Zambia's economy and the source of
"Chipolopolo" (Copper Bullets), the real nickname of the national football
team. References the real 1993 plane crash that killed the national team
and the emotional 2012 AFCON title won near that same site. English is
Zambia's official language, so the full lyric is written directly in it.
