VIETNAM
Official language: Vietnamese

TITLE: "Việt Nam, Cố Lên!" (Vietnam, Go!)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Đàn bầu-driven stadium anthem. Đàn bầu (Vietnam's unique one-string
monochord) carries the wavering melodic hook over a trống cơm hand-drum
rhythm, water-puppet-theater percussion accents, opening into a full-band
arena chorus with massed crowd vocals — a motorbike-horn city energy
folded into the beat.

LYRICS (Vietnamese):

(Đàn bầu bends a single note over a slow trống cơm pulse)

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on "cố lên" (literally "try harder/go on"), the single most iconic
and universally used Vietnamese cheer at every sporting event, paired with
the đàn bầu — a genuinely unique Vietnamese instrument capable of
bending a single string into a full melody. Have a native Vietnamese
speaker confirm tonal accuracy before recording, since Vietnamese is tonal
and melody must respect word tones.
