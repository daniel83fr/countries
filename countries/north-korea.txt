NORTH KOREA
Official language: Korean

TITLE: "Arirang Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Folk-rooted stadium anthem built on the Arirang tradition. Gayageum
(zither) and piri (reed flute) carrying the melody over a janggu
hourglass-drum rhythm, verses in a slow, yearning folk-song register that
opens into a full-orchestral, massed-choir chorus for the stadium sections.

LYRICS (Korean, romanized):

[Sijak]
(Gayageum figure over a slow janggu drum pulse)
Arirang... Arirang...

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built entirely on Arirang, the centuries-old Korean folk song recognized
by UNESCO and shared across the whole Korean peninsula, plus Mount Paektu
(Paektusan) and taekwondo — deliberately chosen as culturally rich,
non-political touchstones focused on heritage and sport rather than
politics. Written in romanized Korean; have a native Korean speaker
convert to Hangul and review phrasing before recording.
