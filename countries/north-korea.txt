NORTH KOREA
Official language: Korean

TITLE: "Arirang Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Folk-rooted stadium anthem built on the Arirang tradition. Gayageum
(zither) and piri (reed flute) carrying the melody over a janggu
hourglass-drum rhythm, verses in a slow, yearning folk-song register that
opens into a full-orchestral, massed-choir chorus for the stadium sections.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Gayageum figure over a slow janggu drum pulse)
Arirang... Arirang...

[Verse 1]
Mount Paektu's crater holds a lake as still as glass and sky,
Every folk song ever sung here starts the same reply,
Taekwondo hands move quiet, then explode into the air,
Generations pass this rhythm down like something we all share.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the mountain crater lake down to the river's bend,
The same old melody still finds us at the end.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Korean + English)
Arirang, Arirang, urinara Arirang!
(Arirang, Arirang, our nation's Arirang!)
Ileona, Arirang!
(Rise up, Arirang!)

[Verse 2]
The drum before the athlete steps out into the open light,
A thousand years of rhythm packed into a single fight,
When the whole crowd sings the melody their grandparents once knew,
That's the sound of a whole nation singing something true.

[Chorus]
Arirang, Arirang, urinara Arirang!
Ileona, Arirang!

[Bridge]
The crater lake reflects a sky that never really moves,
While a folk song passed by hand still somehow still improves —
One melody, a thousand years, still sung the same,
This is the sound that carries this nation's name.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Arirang, Arirang, urinara Arirang!
Ileona, Arirang!
Arirang! Arirang!

[Outro — chant tag]
Arirang! Arirang! Arirang!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built entirely on Arirang, the centuries-old Korean folk song recognized
by UNESCO and shared across the whole Korean peninsula, plus Mount Paektu
(Paektusan) and taekwondo — deliberately chosen as culturally rich,
non-political touchstones focused on heritage and sport rather than
politics. Have a native Korean speaker review phrasing before recording.
