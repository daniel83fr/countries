KYRGYZSTAN
Official language: Kyrgyz

TITLE: "Kyrgyzstan, Alga!" (Kyrgyzstan, Forward!)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Nomadic-fusion stadium anthem. Komuz (three-stringed lute) melody over a
galloping horse-hoof rhythm played on frame drums, temir komuz (jaw harp)
texture, building into a full-band chorus with throat-singing-inspired
low harmonies and massed crowd vocals.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Manas epic (one of the world's longest oral epic poems and a
cornerstone of Kyrgyz identity), the Tian Shan mountains, and kok-boru
(the traditional horseback goat-carrying game, a genuine national sporting
passion). "Alga" (forward) is a real, widely understood Kyrgyz/Turkic word
used in exactly this rallying way. Written as a best-effort romanized
draft (with some stray Cyrillic characters to clean up); have a native
Kyrgyz speaker convert fully to Cyrillic script and rework grammar before
recording.
