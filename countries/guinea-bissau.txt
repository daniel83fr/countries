GUINEA-BISSAU
Official language: Portuguese (Crioulo widely spoken)

TITLE: "Vamos, Guiné-Bissau"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Gumbe-driven island-and-mainland anthem. Gumbe rhythm (Guinea-Bissau's own
signature genre, blending Portuguese and West African traditions) carrying
the groove, calabash and hand-drum polyrhythms, opening into a full-band
chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Calabash hand-drum pattern builds under a gumbe guitar line)
Guiné-Bissau... vamos...

[Verse 1]
Bijagós islands scattered green against the Atlantic blue,
Cashew trees in every yard, a harvest all year through,
Gumbe rhythm carries stories no colonizer ever wrote,
And every family on the mainland learns it note by note.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the island archipelago to the mainland's crowded street,
One nation, rhythm-woven, moving on one beat.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Portuguese/Crioulo + English)
Vamos, Guiné-Bissau! Vamos, Guiné-Bissau!
(Come on, Guinea-Bissau! Come on, Guinea-Bissau!)
Juntu, vamos — Guiné-Bissau!
(Together, come on — Guinea-Bissau!)

[Verse 2]
Cabral taught us freedom is a harvest, not a gift,
Every cashew season proves how far this nation still can lift,
Doesn't matter that the world forgets us on the map,
We built a rhythm no one else could ever quite recap.

[Chorus]
Vamos, Guiné-Bissau! Vamos, Guiné-Bissau!
Juntu, vamos — Guiné-Bissau!

[Bridge]
Island and mainland, cashew and the tide,
A rhythm born of struggle, carrying its pride —
Gumbe guitar and calabash as one,
This is the anthem underneath the sun.

[Final Chorus — full crowd]
Vamos, Guiné-Bissau! Vamos, Guiné-Bissau!
Juntu, vamos — Guiné-Bissau!
Vamos! Vamos!

[Outro — chant tag]
Guiné-Bissau! Vamos!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on gumbe, Guinea-Bissau's own musical genre, the Bijagós
archipelago (a UNESCO biosphere reserve), and the country's status as one
of the world's largest cashew producers — a real economic and cultural
backbone. References Amílcar Cabral, the country's independence leader.
Have a native Portuguese/Crioulo speaker review phrasing before recording.
