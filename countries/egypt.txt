EGYPT
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Misr" (O Egypt)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Mizmar-driven stadium anthem. Mizmar horn and tabla baladi rhythm carrying
the pulse (the sound of a real Cairo street celebration), oud melody
weaving through the verses, opening into a full-band arena chorus with
massed crowd vocals under the shadow of the pyramids.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Mizmar horn wail over a driving tabla baladi rhythm)
Ya Misr... ya Misr...

[Maqta' 1]
Al-Nil hamala imbraturiyat aqdam min ma tastati'u al-nujum an tarsumaha,
Al-ahramat turaqibu al-shuruq yalmasu wajh madina haditha,
Kullu haratin fi al-Qahira tuhamhimu hutafan qabl al-mubarat,
Khamsat alaf 'am min al-tarikh tushajji'u nafs al-ism al-wahid.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min tayyar al-nahr al-qadim ila al-mudarraj al-hadir,
Ummatun wahida, aqdam qissa, walakin bi-yad ashabb.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "Ya Misr al-habiba" means "O beloved Egypt"
Ya Misr! Ya Misr al-habiba!
Yalla Misr, ya Misr al-habiba!
Ya Misr! Ya Misr al-habiba!

[Maqta' 2]
Al-Fara'ina yartadun al-qamis al-an, la al-taj alladhi kanu yartadunahu,
Kullu darba la tazalu tahmilu qisasan 'umruha alaf al-sinin,
La yuhimmu ma taquluhu al-nata'ij al-haditha al-layla,
Nahnu ba'du ummat al-nahr allati 'allamat al-'alam al-kitaba awwalan.

[al-Jawqa]
Ya Misr! Ya Misr al-habiba!
Yalla Misr, ya Misr al-habiba!

[al-Jisr]
Al-hajar yatadhakkaru kulla man ja'a wa raha,
Al-nahr yastamirru fi al-jarayan wa al-ughniya tastamirru,
Mizmar wa tabla, haram wa raml,
Hadhihi ughniyatu aqdam ard.

[al-Jawqa al-Akhira]
Ya Misr! Ya Misr al-habiba!
Yalla Misr, ya Misr al-habiba!
Ya Misr! Ya Misr!

[al-Khatima]
Misr! Misr! Ya habiba!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the Nile and the pyramids — Egypt's defining, globally recognized
landmarks — and "the Pharaohs," the real nickname of Egypt's national
football team, framing modern sporting pride as a continuation of ancient
history rather than a separate story. Have a native Egyptian Arabic
speaker review phrasing before recording.
