UGANDA
Official languages: English, Swahili

TITLE: "Pearl Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Adungu-driven stadium anthem. Adungu bow-harp (a signature Ugandan
instrument) carries the melody over an engoma drum rhythm, source-of-the-
Nile water-rush ambience, opening into a full-band chorus with massed
crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Adungu harp figure over a slow engoma drum pulse)
Uganda... pearl rising...

[Verse 1]
The Nile is born right here where Lake Victoria spills out clear,
Rwenzori's snow-capped peaks stand watching over every year,
Gorillas move through misted forest older than the maps we drew,
And every match-day crowd still carries what this land is true to.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the river's very source to the stadium's roaring crowd,
One nation, pearl of Africa, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Pearl of Africa, rising! Pearl of Africa, rising!
The source of the great river starts right here at home,
Pearl of Africa, rising! Pearl of Africa, rising!
Uganda, Uganda, wherever we roam.

[Verse 2]
The Cranes take flight across the pitch the way the real ones fly,
Every generation learns the reason and the why,
Doesn't matter what the bigger nations count us small,
The river that feeds a continent starts here, before it all.

[Chorus]
Pearl of Africa, rising! Pearl of Africa, rising!
The source of the great river starts right here at home,
Pearl of Africa, rising! Pearl of Africa, rising!
Uganda, Uganda, wherever we roam.

[Bridge]
Source of the river, roof of misted green,
The most beautiful pearl the world has ever seen —
Adungu string and engoma drum as one,
This is the anthem underneath the sun.

[Final Chorus]
Pearl of Africa, rising! Pearl of Africa, rising!
Uganda, Uganda, wherever we roam.

[Outro]
Uganda! Pearl! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Uganda's real status as the source of the Nile at Lake Victoria,
the Rwenzori Mountains, and "the Pearl of Africa," the country's famous
nickname. "The Cranes" is the national football team's real nickname.
English is one of Uganda's official languages, so the full lyric is
written directly in it.
