TURKS AND CAICOS ISLANDS
Official language: English

TITLE: "Ripsaw Rising"

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Ripsaw-driven island anthem. Ripsaw music (a real Turks and Caicos genre
played on an actual handsaw, plus guitar and concertina) carries the whole
rhythm, coral-reef wave-wash texture from Grace Bay, opening into a
full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(A ripsaw scrapes out a rhythm under a rising guitar line)
Turks... Caicos... rising...

[Verse 1]
Grace Bay's sand is whiter than any beach you've ever known,
Conch shells scattered on the tide like something the sea has sown,
The ripsaw plays a rhythm on a blade that's meant to cut,
Now it plays the music of an island's open heart, wide shut.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the reef below the surface to the stadium's roaring crowd,
Small islands, ocean-hearted, standing proud.

[Chorus]
Ripsaw rising! Ripsaw rising!
White sand and coral water under open skies,
Ripsaw rising! Ripsaw rising!
Turks and Caicos, watch our islands rise.

[Verse 2]
Every conch diver here still moves without a single sound,
Every islander still knows the reef that we are bound,
Doesn't matter how the bigger nations count the score,
We built a pride as deep as any reef or ocean floor.

[Chorus]
Ripsaw rising! Ripsaw rising!
White sand and coral water under open skies,
Ripsaw rising! Ripsaw rising!
Turks and Caicos, watch our islands rise.

[Bridge]
A blade turned instrument, a reef turned home,
Small islands proving that they'll never be alone —
Ripsaw scraping out a rhythm strong,
This is the anthem of the island song.

[Final Chorus]
Ripsaw rising! Ripsaw rising!
Turks and Caicos, watch our islands rise.

[Outro]
Turks and Caicos! Ripsaw! Rising!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built entirely on ripsaw music, a genuine and distinctive local tradition
where a handsaw is bent and bowed like an instrument, and Grace Bay, one
of the world's most celebrated beaches. English is the official language,
so the full lyric is written directly in it.
