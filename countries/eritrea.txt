ERITREA
Working language: Tigrinya (also Arabic, English)

TITLE: "Eritrea, Kedem" (Eritrea, Forward)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Krar-driven Red Sea anthem. Krar (six-stringed lyre) carrying the melodic
hook over a kebero hand-drum rhythm built to mimic a cyclist's steady
cadence — a nod to Eritrea's real cycling culture — Art Deco Asmara street
ambience, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Tigrinya, romanized):

[Mekseri]
(Krar lyre figure over a steady, pedaling kebero drum pulse)
Eritrea... kedem...

[Gemgem 1]
Asmara kafe-tat Art Deco kalilen zeye deggemu zeliyu zema,

[Nikab Korus]
Kab dabar bishiklet gebri wesken kab jebena buni sinu,
Hade nesu hager, be'zub tekonimu, be'edmeti k'da'a.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Eritrea's genuine, world-renowned cycling culture (a legacy of
Italian colonial-era roads that turned into one of Africa's strongest
cycling traditions) and Asmara's UNESCO-listed Art Deco architecture.
Written in romanized Tigrinya as a best-effort draft; have a native
Tigrinya speaker convert to Ge'ez script and review grammar before
recording.
