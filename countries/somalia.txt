SOMALIA
Official languages: Somali, Arabic

TITLE: "Soomaaliya, Kaca" (Somalia, Rise Up)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Oud-and-poetry stadium anthem. Kaban (Somali oud) carries the melody over
a hand-drum caravan rhythm, verses phrased in the cadence of traditional
Somali oral poetry (gabay), opening into a full-band chorus with massed
crowd vocals along the Indian Ocean coast.

LYRICS (Somali):

[Hordhac]
(Kaban baa ku dhawaaqaysa dhaanto tabla ah oo tartiib ah)
Soomaaliya... kaca...

[Aayad 1]
Xeebtayada dheer baa qaba badda oo dhan, dhinac ilaa dhinac,
Geela wuxuu maraa jid uu abtirsiinyadu u taqaan tan iyo bilaad,
Gabayaa baa ka hadlay taariikh aan la qorin weligeed,
Cid kastana way sii waday sheekadaas oo aan la illaabi karin.

[Ka Hor Faaqidaanta]
Xeebta dheer ilaa garoonka buuqa leh,
Umad keliya, adkaysi, aan waligeed dhicin.

[Faaqidaanta] — the hook: "Soomaaliya, kaca" means "Somalia, rise up"
Soomaaliya, kaca! Soomaaliya, kaca!
Dhammaanteen, kaca — Soomaaliya!
Soomaaliya, kaca! Soomaaliya, kaca!

[Aayad 2]
Kaban baa ku sheekaynaya waxa sanadihii dhaafay,
Gabaygu wuxuu sheegaa in adkaysi weligiis aanu dhammaan,
Kama xanaaqin sanadihii adag ee soo maray taariikhda,
Weli waxaan nahay dadka ugu adkaysta ee dunida taqaan.

[Faaqidaanta]
Soomaaliya, kaca! Soomaaliya, kaca!
Dhammaanteen, kaca — Soomaaliya!

[Buundo]
Kaban iyo gabay, hal cod oo la hadlaya,
Xeebta iyo geela, hal dhulna aan wax ka dheelin,
Dhaanto iyo sheeko, hal iyo hal,
Kani waa heestii umad aan weligeed dhicin.

[Faaqidaanta Ugu Dambeeya]
Soomaaliya, kaca! Soomaaliya, kaca!
Dhammaanteen, kaca — Soomaaliya!
Kaca! Kaca!

[Dhammaad]
Soomaaliya! Kaca! Kaca!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Somalia's real, deep oral poetry tradition (gabay), often called
"a nation of poets," its long Indian Ocean coastline, and camel-herding
nomadic heritage. Have a native Somali speaker review grammar and idiom
before recording.
