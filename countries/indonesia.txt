INDONESIA
Official language: Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia)

TITLE: "Garuda di Dadaku" Reimagined — "Bangkit, Indonesia" (Rise, Indonesia)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Gamelan-fusion stadium anthem. Gamelan gong-chime and kendang drum patterns
opening the verses, angklung bamboo rattle texture, exploding into a
dangdut-tinged pop-rock chorus with massed crowd vocals — the sound of
17,000 islands roaring as one stadium.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Gamelan gongs ring out over a slow kendang drum pulse)
Indonesia... bangkit...

[Verse 1]
Seventeen thousand islands but we all beat one drum,
From Sumatra's misty forest to where Papua's rivers run,
The Garuda on our crest has flown through storm and through the calm,
Every voice from every island singing the same psalm.

[Pre-Chorus]
From Borobudur's stone stairways to Bali's temple bell,
One archipelago, one heartbeat — you can hear it swell.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Bahasa Indonesia + English)
Bangkit, Indonesia! Bangkit, Indonesia!
(Rise, Indonesia! Rise, Indonesia!)
Garuda terbang tinggi, Indonesia!
(The Garuda flies high, Indonesia!)

[Verse 2]
A fisherman in Flores and a coder in Jakarta's tower,
Watching the same jersey in the exact same hour,
Bhinneka Tunggal Ika — unity in every different tongue,
When the Garuda takes the field, we all sing as one.

[Chorus]
Bangkit, Indonesia! Bangkit, Indonesia!
Garuda terbang tinggi, Indonesia!

[Bridge]
Islands scattered like stars across the sea,
Every single one still beats the same as me —
Gong and drum and bamboo in the wind,
This is the sound of a nation that won't bend.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Bangkit, Indonesia! Bangkit, Indonesia!
Garuda terbang tinggi, Indonesia!
Bangkit! Bangkit!

[Outro — chant tag]
Garuda! Garuda! Indonesia!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Anchors the story in the Garuda (the mythic bird on Indonesia's national
emblem and the nickname of its football team), the national motto "Bhinneka
Tunggal Ika" (Unity in Diversity — essential given 17,000+ islands and
hundreds of languages), and landmarks like Borobudur. Have a native Bahasa
Indonesia speaker review phrasing and meter before recording.
