SLOVAKIA
Official language: Slovak

TITLE: "Fujara nad štadiónom" (Fujara Over the Stadium)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Slovak folk-rock stadium anthem built around the fujara, the giant
overtone shepherd's flute from the Tatra highlands (a UNESCO-recognized
instrument), its low breathy drone opening the track before a driving
rock rhythm section and massed male-choir crowd vocals take over. Tempo
feels like a highland march quickening into a terrace stomp, with valaška
(shepherd's axe) stick-clacking woven into the percussion.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on the fujara, a two-metre-long overtone flute unique to central
Slovakia's shepherd culture and inscribed on UNESCO's list of intangible
heritage, and on the High Tatras (Kriváň peak specifically), the country's
defining mountain range and a recurring symbol of Slovak identity. The
valaška, a decorated shepherd's axe used in folk dance, supplies the
percussive stick-clack woven through the terrace rhythm. Have a native
Slovak speaker review the grammar and idiom before recording.
