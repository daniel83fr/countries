JORDAN
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Urdunn, Yalla" (O Jordan, Come On)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Bedouin-fusion stadium anthem. Rebab (single-string Bedouin fiddle) and oud
carrying the melodic line over a hand-drum desert rhythm, mijwiz reed pipe
accents, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals — the
sound of a Wadi Rum campfire scaled up to a football stadium.

LYRICS (Arabic, transliterated):

[Muqaddima]
(Rebab drone over a slow hand-drum pulse, wind sound in the desert)
Ya Urdunn... yalla...

[Maqta' 1]
Manhuta min al-hajar al-wardi, madina makhfiya fi al-tilal,
Al-Batra hafizat sirrana qabla dahsha al-'alam al-hadith,
Niran al-badu taht al-nujum fi raml Wadi Rum al-ahmar,
Kullu qabila, mamlaka wahida, nahr wahid 'abra al-ard.

[Qabla al-Jawqa]
Min sukun al-Bahr al-Mayyit ila al-qal'a fawq,
Hadhihi mamlaka saghira tahmilu hubban 'azim.

[al-Jawqa] — the hook: "yalla Urdunn" means "come on, Jordan"
Ya Urdunn! Yalla, Urdunn!
Habibi, yalla Urdunn!
Ya Urdunn! Yalla, Urdunn!

[Maqta' 2]
Al-sahra' 'allamatna al-sabr, wa al-jibal 'allamatna al-fakhr,
Mamlaka buniyat 'ala al-karam, maftuha, al-dhira' mamduda,
'Indama yabda' al-safir, al-jumhur yanfajiru mithl matar al-sahra',
Saghira 'ala al-khareeta, walakin sarkhatuna tabqa ba'ida wa wadiha.

[al-Jawqa]
Ya Urdunn! Yalla, Urdunn!
Habibi, yalla Urdunn!

[al-Jisr]
Madina wardiya manhuta bi-ayadin mundhu zaman ba'id,
La tazalu tu'allimuna kayfa yakburu fakhruna —
Mamlaka wahida, sha'b wahid, waqifin ka-wahid,
Taht nafs al-nujum, taht nafs al-shams.

[al-Jawqa al-Akhira]
Ya Urdunn! Yalla, Urdunn!
Habibi, yalla Urdunn!
Yalla! Yalla! Urdunn!

[al-Khatima]
Urdunn! Urdunn! Yalla!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Petra (the rose-red city carved into stone, Jordan's most famous
landmark) and Wadi Rum's Bedouin desert heritage, with "yalla" (come
on/let's go) as an authentic, widely used Arabic expression that makes the
hook naturally chantable. Have a native Jordanian Arabic speaker review
phrasing before recording.
