JORDAN
Official language: Arabic

TITLE: "Ya Urdunn, Yalla" (O Jordan, Come On)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Bedouin-fusion stadium anthem. Rebab (single-string Bedouin fiddle) and oud
carrying the melodic line over a hand-drum desert rhythm, mijwiz reed pipe
accents, opening into a full-band chorus with massed crowd vocals — the
sound of a Wadi Rum campfire scaled up to a football stadium.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Rebab drone over a slow hand-drum pulse, wind sound in the desert)
Ya Urdunn... yalla...

[Verse 1]
Carved from rose-red stone, a city hidden in the hills,
Petra kept our secret long before the modern world's thrills,
Bedouin fires under stars in Wadi Rum's red sand,
Every tribe, one kingdom, one river through the land.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the Dead Sea's stillness to the citadel above,
This is a small kingdom carrying a giant kind of love.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Arabic + English)
Ya Urdunn! Yalla, Urdunn!
(O Jordan! Come on, Jordan!)
Habibi, yalla Urdunn!
(My love, come on Jordan!)

[Verse 2]
The desert taught us patience, and the mountains taught us pride,
A kingdom built on hospitality, open, arms out wide,
When the whistle starts the crowd erupts like desert rain,
Small on the map, but our roar still carries far and plain.

[Chorus]
Ya Urdunn! Yalla, Urdunn!
Habibi, yalla Urdunn!

[Bridge]
Rose-red city carved by hands so long ago,
Still teaches us the shape our pride should grow —
One kingdom, one people, standing as one,
Under the same stars, under the same sun.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Ya Urdunn! Yalla, Urdunn!
Habibi, yalla Urdunn!
Yalla! Yalla! Urdunn!

[Outro — chant tag]
Urdunn! Urdunn! Yalla!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Petra (the rose-red city carved into stone, Jordan's most famous
landmark) and Wadi Rum's Bedouin desert heritage, with "yalla" (come
on/let's go) as an authentic, widely used Arabic expression that makes the
hook naturally chantable. Have a native Jordanian Arabic speaker review
phrasing before recording.
