JAPAN
Official language: Japanese

TITLE: "Ganbare Nippon" (Do Your Best, Japan)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Taiko-drum stadium anthem. Massive taiko drum ensemble driving the pulse
(the same drumming used at matsuri festivals), koto melody in the verses,
shakuhachi flute breath textures, opening into a synth-and-guitar arena
chorus with massed crowd chant vocals.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built around "Ganbare Nippon," a genuine, already-iconic cheer heard at
Japanese sporting events worldwide, alongside cherry blossoms, Mount Fuji,
and taiko festival drumming — all deeply real, not decorative. Written in
romanized Japanese; have a native Japanese speaker convert to kanji/kana
and confirm phrasing and natural cadence before recording.
