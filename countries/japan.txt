JAPAN
Official language: Japanese

TITLE: "Ganbare Nippon" (Do Your Best, Japan)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Taiko-drum stadium anthem. Massive taiko drum ensemble driving the pulse
(the same drumming used at matsuri festivals), koto melody in the verses,
shakuhachi flute breath textures, opening into a synth-and-guitar arena
chorus with massed crowd chant vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(A single taiko strike, then a rolling festival drum pattern builds)
Nippon... ganbare...

[Verse 1]
Cherry blossoms fall like confetti on the training ground,
Fuji watches quiet while the whole stadium shakes with sound,
Grandfather's festival drum still beats the way it always did,
Every child in every city learns the rhythm as a kid.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the shrine gate's red to the stadium's blinding light,
One nation, one heartbeat, drumming through the night.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Japanese + English — echoes a real, widely used cheer)
Nippon, ganbare! Nippon, ganbare!
(Japan, do your best! Japan, do your best!)
Minna de, ganbare Nippon!
(Everyone together — go, Japan!)

[Verse 2]
The samurai learned patience is the sharpest kind of blade,
Discipline before the glory, that's the promise that we made,
When the whistle blows the taiko answers with a roar,
This is a small island that has always played for more.

[Chorus]
Nippon, ganbare! Nippon, ganbare!
Minna de, ganbare Nippon!

[Bridge]
Falling blossoms teach us beauty doesn't have to stay,
Every season passes but the spirit finds its way —
Drum by drum, breath by breath, we rise as one,
This is the discipline of the rising sun.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Nippon, ganbare! Nippon, ganbare!
Minna de, ganbare Nippon!
Ganbare! Ganbare!

[Outro — chant tag]
Nippon! Nippon! Ganbare!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built around "Ganbare Nippon," a genuine, already-iconic cheer heard at
Japanese sporting events worldwide, alongside cherry blossoms, Mount Fuji,
and taiko festival drumming — all deeply real, not decorative. Have a
native Japanese speaker confirm phrasing and natural cadence before
recording.
