GEORGIA
Official language: Georgian

TITLE: "მთები გვასმენენ" (The Mountains Hear Us)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Georgian polyphonic choral chant reworked as a stadium anthem — the deep three-part vocal harmony of traditional Georgian singing (UNESCO-recognized) carried by a droning panduri line, over a slow, heavy processional drum that opens into a full-throated massed crowd roar.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on Georgian polyphonic singing, a centuries-old three-part vocal harmony tradition recognized by UNESCO as a Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity, traditionally accompanied by the panduri, a three-stringed lute central to Georgian folk and church music. The lyric ties that ancient choral sound to the towering Caucasus Mountains that ring Georgia's valleys, imagining the same layered voices rising from a modern football stadium.
