IRAN
Official language: Persian (Farsi)

TITLE: "Iran-e Man, Pirooz Bad" (My Iran, Be Victorious)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Persian classical-fusion stadium anthem. Tar and setar melody lines over a
daf frame-drum pulse (the same drum used in Zurkhaneh strength-house
rituals), santur shimmer in the pre-chorus, opening into a full-band arena
chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(Daf frame drum builds a slow ceremonial pulse)
Iran... Iran-e man...

[Verse 1]
Under Persepolis' broken columns the wind still tells its tale,
In the Zurkhaneh the strong men still swing the ancient scale,
Poets wrote our history before a single flag was flown,
Every verse of Ferdowsi still lives in every home.

[Pre-Chorus]
From the Alborz mountain shadow to the Gulf's warm shore,
This is a people who have carried pride for years before.

[Chorus] (viral hook, Persian + English)
Iran, Iran! Pirooz bad Iran!
(Iran, Iran! Victorious be Iran!)
Iran-e jan, pirooz bad Iran!
(My dear Iran, victorious be Iran!)

[Verse 2]
The strongman spins his heavy club to a rhythm three thousand years old,
Every match we play, we're carrying a story that's already told,
When the whole stand rises and the anthem starts to swell,
We are the empire that outlived every wall that ever fell.

[Chorus]
Iran, Iran! Pirooz bad Iran!
Iran-e jan, pirooz bad Iran!

[Bridge]
Stone remembers what the years try to erase,
We are still standing in the very same place —
Poetry and iron, patience and flame,
Every generation carries the same name.

[Final Chorus — full stadium]
Iran, Iran! Pirooz bad Iran!
Iran-e jan, pirooz bad Iran!
Pirooz bad! Pirooz bad!

[Outro — chant tag]
Iran, Iran, Iran!

CULTURAL NOTE:
Draws on Persepolis, the daf drum and Zurkhaneh ("house of strength") — an
ancient Persian martial and athletic tradition still practiced today — and
the deep national reverence for classical poets like Ferdowsi. "Pirooz"
(victorious) is a real, commonly used Persian word, making the chorus land
as genuinely Persian rather than generic. Have a native Farsi speaker
review verse phrasing and meter before recording.
