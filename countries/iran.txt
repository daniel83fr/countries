IRAN
Official language: Persian (Farsi)

TITLE: "Iran-e Man, Pirooz Bad" (My Iran, Be Victorious)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Persian classical-fusion stadium anthem. Tar and setar melody lines over a
daf frame-drum pulse (the same drum used in Zurkhaneh strength-house
rituals), santur shimmer in the pre-chorus, opening into a full-band arena
chorus with massed crowd vocals.

LYRICS (Persian, romanized):

[Moghadameh]
(Daf frame drum builds a slow ceremonial pulse)
Iran... Iran-e man...

[Band-e Avval]
Zir-e sotoon-haye shekaste-ye Takht-e Jamshid, bad hanuz dastan migoyad,
Dar Zurkhaneh mardan-e ghavi hanuz mile-haye kohan ra micharkhanand,
Sha'eran tarikh-e ma ra pish az yek parcham neveshtand,
Har beyt-e Ferdowsi hanuz dar har khaneh zende ast.

[Pish az Kor]
Az sayeh-ye kuh-haye Alborz ta sahel-e garm-e khalij,
In mardomi hastand ke saal-ha ghorur ra hamel budand.

[Kor] — the hook: "pirooz bad Iran" means "victorious be Iran"
Iran, Iran! Pirooz bad Iran!
Iran-e jan, pirooz bad Iran!
Iran, Iran! Pirooz bad Iran!

Vaghti tamam-e jayegah bar mikhizad va soroud shoru be oj gereftan mikonad,
Ma hamun emperatori hastim ke az har divari ke oftad, biroon amad.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Draws on Persepolis, the daf drum and Zurkhaneh ("house of strength") — an
ancient Persian martial and athletic tradition still practiced today — and
the deep national reverence for classical poets like Ferdowsi. "Pirooz"
(victorious) is a real, commonly used Persian word, making the chorus land
as genuinely Persian rather than generic. Written in romanized Farsi;
have a native Farsi speaker convert to Persian script and review verse
phrasing and meter before recording.
