SAN MARINO
Official language: Italian

TITLE: "Piccoli Ma Fedeli" (Small But Faithful)

STYLE / GENRE TAG:
Italian brass-band bandella anthem — the small-town wind-and-brass band
sound found across Italy and San Marino's own Monte Titano festivals.
Clarinet and trumpet trade the main melody over a warm, oom-pah brass
band pulse, staying modest and close-knit rather than bombastic, before
swelling into a defiant, full-throated crowd chorus.

CULTURAL NOTE:
Built on San Marino's real identity as the world's oldest surviving
republic, founded in 301 AD atop Monte Titano, and its equally real
football story: with a population of about 33,000, San Marino's national
team is one of international football's most famous perennial
underdogs, having won only a single competitive match in its history —
turning that struggle itself into the source of pride ("piccoli ma
fedeli," small but faithful). The bandella brass-band sound reflects
San Marino and neighboring Italian hill-town festival music, kept
deliberately modest rather than grandiose.
